## What Changed

APS's April 27, 2026 anniversary record identifies TSMC as locating in APS service territory in 2021. The same record says APS reached an 8,643 MW system peak on August 7, 2025 and expects peak demand to exceed 13,000 MW within fourteen years.

## Evidence Stage

This supports `named serving utility` and `utility-wide demand context`.

It does not support `customer-specific service agreement`, `disclosed campus load`, `dedicated infrastructure complete`, `facility energized`, `operating consumption`, `reliability performance`, or `available spare capacity`.

## Why It Matters

FTFN can now stop treating the TSMC utility territory as an open location question. The harder capacity question becomes more precise: find the customer agreement, planning or regulatory record, infrastructure scope, construction, energization, and operating evidence for the campus and each facility.

## What To Watch Next

Track APS and Arizona Corporation Commission filings, customer-specific service terms, disclosed load forecasts, substation and transmission projects, construction, energization, consumption, reliability, and facilities charges tied to TSMC.
