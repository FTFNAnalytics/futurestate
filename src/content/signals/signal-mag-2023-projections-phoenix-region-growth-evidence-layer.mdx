## What Changed

FTFN selected a named Maricopa Association of Governments Open Data item for the U.S. Southwest Chip Corridor dossier: the 2023 projections of population, housing, and employment for Maricopa and Pinal Counties. The source item states that the projections are used as inputs for regional transportation, air-quality, and planning programs.

This matters because Phase 49 added MAG as a broad local-system rail. Phase 50 narrows that rail into a concrete dataset, item ID, layer structure, and evidence boundary.

## Why It Matters

Semiconductor manufacturing regions are not only shaped by fabs. They are shaped by whether housing, transportation, land use, employment geography, workforce access, and public services can absorb growth. A regional projections dataset gives FTFN a stronger baseline for asking those questions around the Phoenix-region chip corridor.

The dataset is also operationally useful because it is exposed through an ArcGIS FeatureServer with query and extract support. That makes it a candidate for later static exports or a local evidence table, once FTFN decides which geography and variables belong in the public dossier.

## Dependency Stack

- MAG projection methodology and regional adoption context
- Population, housing, and employment inputs
- Municipal planning areas
- Regional analysis zones and traffic analysis zones
- Transportation and air-quality models
- Local plans, development records, and employment data inputs
- Facility, workforce, utility, water, and permitting records that still need to be selected separately

## Evidence Limits

This signal does not prove that the region has enough trained semiconductor workers, housing capacity, transportation capacity, power, water, supplier depth, or permitting throughput for any named fab, supplier, or data-center project. It is a planning-evidence layer, not a local readiness conclusion.

The dataset should be paired with workforce data, training-program capacity, permit records, utility planning, water-provider records, and company or facility evidence before FTFN makes stronger local conversion claims.

## What To Watch Next

Use this record as a local dossier layer, then select the next named records: Arizona workforce and training sources, supplier facility records, Phoenix permit or planning records, utility planning or docket records, and provider-level water evidence tied to specific geographies.
