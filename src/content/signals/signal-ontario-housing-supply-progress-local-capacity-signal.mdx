## What Changed

Ontario's housing supply tracker gives FTFN an official basis for treating housing targets and progress as a local-system signal.

## Why It Matters

Housing supply is not only a market number. It depends on planning decisions, serviced land, construction labor, financing, infrastructure, and public legitimacy.

## Dependency Stack

- Provincial targets and tracking
- Municipal planning approvals
- Servicing and infrastructure capacity
- Construction labor and materials
- Project financing and buyer demand

## Evidence Limits

This signal should not be read as proof that a specific municipality can deliver its target or that a specific project is viable. Those claims require local planning, servicing, permit, financing, and infrastructure evidence.

## What To Watch Next

Watch municipal development approvals, servicing plans, infrastructure funding, construction labor signals, and CMHC housing starts and completion data.
