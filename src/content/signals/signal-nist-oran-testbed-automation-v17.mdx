## What changed

The NIST software page identifies version 1.7 and documents supported configurations, including multiple user-equipment connections, split central and distributed units, inter-DU handover, network slicing, and time-series performance visualization.

## Evidence stage

This supports `versioned research software + documented laboratory configurations`. It does not support `certified product`, `multi-vendor commercial interoperability`, `secure deployment`, or `6G network`.

## Why it matters

The artifact turns O-RAN testing from an institutional intention into something another laboratory can inspect, install, configure, and evaluate.

## What to watch next

Track reproducible external deployments, published test results, conformance suites, security findings, certification use, standards incorporation, and operator adoption.
