## What Changed

Statistics Canada reported that the total value of Canadian building permits was $12.4 billion in May 2026, down 1.7% from April. The release estimated 24,100 authorized dwellings nationally, including 20,200 multi-unit and 3,900 single-family units. Within the Toronto census metropolitan area, multi-unit construction intentions increased by $129 million.

## Why It Matters

The mixed monthly result is useful precisely because it resists a simple boom-or-bust reading. Permit values and authorized units show upstream intentions. Financing, labor, materials, servicing, starts, market absorption, and completions determine whether those intentions become delivered housing.

## Dependency Stack

- Permit applications and approvals
- Starts, completions, and units under construction
- Project financing
- Labor and materials
- Servicing and infrastructure capacity
- Starts, completions, and occupancy

## Evidence Limits

The monthly values are subject to revision, and a permit does not prove financing, a construction start, completion, affordability, occupancy, or delivery timing. The Toronto figure refers to multi-unit permit value, not completed units. Use CMHC starts and completions plus municipal infrastructure and financing evidence to test conversion.

## What To Watch Next

Watch monthly permit changes alongside housing starts, completions, municipal servicing capacity, and financing conditions.
