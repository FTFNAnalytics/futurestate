## Current state

BOD 23-01 defines recurring federal asset-discovery and vulnerability-enumeration work across covered IP-addressable assets.

## Evidence boundary

The directive does not disclose agency compliance, inventory completeness, scan coverage, vulnerability closure, contractor execution, or private-sector implementation.

## Next records

Track implementation guidance, agency reports, inspector-general findings, asset coverage, scan cadence, exceptions, remediation performance, and non-federal adoption evidence.
