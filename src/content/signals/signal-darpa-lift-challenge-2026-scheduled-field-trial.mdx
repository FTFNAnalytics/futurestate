## What Changed

DARPA moved the Lift Challenge from an application pool to a named first group of 72 invited teams. The announcement says more than 200 teams had completed critical requirements and milestones and schedules a live performance trial in Dayton, Ohio, for August 2-9, 2026.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `named performers + scheduled field trial`. It does not support `trial completed`, `target achieved`, `winner selected`, `technology transitioned`, or `service deployed`.

## Why It Matters

The field-trial format creates a measurable bridge between novel concepts and observed performance. That makes the next update much stronger than another strategy or solicitation summary: it can be tied to a dated course, a payload-to-weight metric, official results, and prize actions.

## What To Watch Next

Recheck DARPA after August 9 for completed-event evidence, measured payload-to-weight ratios, disqualifications or failures, prize decisions, follow-on agreements, transition partners, manufacturing plans, certification work, and operational trials.
