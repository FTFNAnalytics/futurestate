## What Changed

DOE's Transmission Facilitation Program page identifies Southline as a $477 million awarded capacity-contract action. The proposed first phase is a 175-mile, 748 MW line from Hidalgo County, New Mexico, to Pima County, Arizona.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `awarded capacity contract + named project`. It does not support `notice to proceed`, `construction underway`, `interconnected`, `energized`, or `capacity delivered`.

## Why It Matters

The record turns a national transmission-needs finding into a project-level delivery chain. FTFN can now ask which regulatory, financial, construction, and interconnection gates have actually closed and which remain open.

## What To Watch Next

Track current permits, financing, rights of way, notice to proceed, contractor activity, substations, line construction, interconnection agreements, energization, available transfer capability, and delivered service. Reconcile all dates against current project records.
