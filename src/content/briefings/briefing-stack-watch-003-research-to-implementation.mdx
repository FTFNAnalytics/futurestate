## The implementation ladder

Phase 55K established that federal research direction increasingly depends on materials, production, power, compute, communications, security, logistics, and receiving systems. Phase 55L asks a narrower question: where has that direction crossed into a named action that can be followed over time?

The eight records do not sit at the same maturity level. The Talon record contains obligations and outlays. The MP Materials action is an executed loan. The NAPMP and Southline records are final or awarded funding actions. The OpenAI record is a prototype agreement with a much smaller amount obligated at award than its stated value. DARPA has named teams and scheduled a trial. Project Pele has delivered fuel. NIST has software, a testbed, and accepted standards contributions.

## What the records support

Together, the records support a bounded conclusion: the research agenda is generating identifiable implementation trails. Each trail now has a recipient, project, instrument, facility, event, material, artifact, or date that can be checked again.

## What they do not prove

They do not prove that facilities are complete, production is sustained, trials succeed, transmission is energized, reactors operate, AI prototypes are accepted, wireless systems interoperate, or any capability has scaled.

## Editorial use

Phase 55O publication decision: publish this briefing with seven Published implementation records and the DARPA Lift Challenge retained as a visibly In Review scheduled trial. The synthesis is useful because it compares evidence stages without promoting the held record. Correct or reopen the briefing if an underlying award, obligation, agreement, delivery, standards artifact, or scheduled-trial record changes materially.
