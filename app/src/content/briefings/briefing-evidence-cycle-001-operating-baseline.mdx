## Propagation contract

The required sequence is:

`source check → receipt → signal decision → canonical dossier → reader pathway → dependency map → Outcomes Watch → update log → Git-backed release validation`

A material record change must update every affected surface in the same reviewed change set. A bounded no-change decision retains the prior record state, names what was checked and what remains missing, and still moves through the assigned reader surfaces. A blocked check needs a visible blocker receipt. A missed date without a receipt or an explicit reschedule fails the Phase 60 gate.

## First complete proof

Receipt `receipt-58-darpa-2026-08-11-no-material-change` is the seed proof. The source check advanced the DARPA source freshness date; the result signal remained In Review; the autonomy adoption dossier, autonomy pathway, shared adoption map, Outcomes Watch, this digest, and the Phase 60 update all carry the same bounded decision and August 14 next check.

The proof does not say that results do not exist. It says only that the official surfaces reviewed on August 11 did not supply the final measured-results artifact required by the gate.

## No silent overdue work

A check is due on its recorded day and becomes silently overdue only after that date passes without a decision date and receipt. A technical or access blocker is not a reason to omit the check; it becomes a bounded blocker receipt with the attempted source, the limitation, and the next action. A schedule change requires a Watch Note.
