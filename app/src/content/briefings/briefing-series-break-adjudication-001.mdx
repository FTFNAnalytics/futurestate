## Why breaks need their own register

A trend is often most misleading at the point where its definition changes. Phase 69 therefore gives every cohort a separate prospective break register. It records what would stop comparison before any observation is accepted.

No actual Phase 69 break event is recorded. All eight registers are in **No Admitted Series** because every Phase 68 cohort remains in Acquisition.

## Ten break types

1. entity or identity change;
2. facility, project, service, geography, population, customer, product, or stage change;
3. numerator or denominator unit change;
4. denominator or exposure-definition change;
5. period, cadence, partial-period, or missing-period change;
6. collection, calculation, test, threshold, or method-version change;
7. issuing authority, acceptance authority, or reporting-obligation change;
8. revision, correction, restatement, or supersession;
9. failure, outage, rejection, exclusion, adverse observation, or hidden exception;
10. attribution boundary or alternative-explanation change.

## Four possible decisions

- **Compatible:** the reviewed field did not change, or the source supplies an exact continuity statement.
- **Bridged with limits:** a public transformation connects two segments while preserving uncertainty and affected periods.
- **Segmented:** both segments remain useful, but they cannot be joined into one comparable series.
- **Rejected or blocked:** the break is hidden, the bridge is incomplete, or the requested transformation would invent comparability.

These decisions require a real artifact, independent review, receipt, and propagation. They are not inferred from the prospective taxonomy.

## Minimum bridge record

A bridge must identify both series segments, the exact changed field, original definitions and units, transformation or non-comparability decision, formula where applicable, precision, uncertainty, affected periods, exceptions, alternative explanations, authority, reviewer, receipt, and reader-surface propagation.

The default is preservation, not repair. Missing values remain missing. Different entities remain different. A change in reporting rules may require a new segment even when the labels look identical.

## Entity-specific boundaries

The eight registers add four file-specific stop rules apiece. They prevent, among other transfers, pooling TSMC fabs; inserting citywide housing totals into one Toronto application; converting an approved tariff into metered service; merging Space Coast sites or operators; combining Thacker Pass and Rhyolite Ridge; pooling agency or system PQC migrations; transferring a pilot ARIA method into production; or joining testing mileage to Waymo passenger service.

## Publication boundary

Series-break adjudication does not make a series, value, trend, outcome, comparison, or causal claim. A documented bridge is itself a bounded decision and can conclude that comparison remains prohibited.

## Phase 70 review and admission control

[Observation Review Desk 001](/briefings/observation-review-desk-001/) and the [Series Admission Protocol](/briefings/series-admission-protocol-001/) add empty review, revision-lineage, and admission dockets after Phase 69 intake. They create zero submissions, reviews, accepted observations, receipts, revision events, series, or outcomes.
