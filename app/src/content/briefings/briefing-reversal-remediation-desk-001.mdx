## Decisions need an exit

A reversible decision is only reversible when the authority, technical path, resources, records, and responsible actor for reversal are known. A sunset date is only meaningful when expiry changes the legal or operating state.

Phase 75 creates eight post-decision audit and remediation registers. Every register remains **Inactive - No Authorized Decision**.

## Twelve audit gates

The audit verifies the current decision and terms, authorization compliance, commitment delivery, baseline integrity, benefit and harm realization, distribution, counterfactual uncertainty, adverse events, safeguards, challenges, dissent, sunset, renewal, remediation, independent review, and public receipt.

The audit is not an implementation self-report. Implementers can supply evidence, but an accountable independent review decides whether the record supports continuation, amendment, pause, reversal, remedy, reauthorization, sunset, or closure.

## Triggered review

Twelve trigger classes cover deadline, budget, capacity, scope, output, harm, disparity, stop-work, challenge, authority, evidence correction, and sunset. Each trigger carries a date, source, affected commitment, materiality decision, response deadline, and receipt.

No event is inferred from silence. Conversely, absence of a self-reported event cannot close an externally documented concern.

## Remediation is not a footnote

Ten action classes distinguish correcting the public record, amending implementation, adding resources, escalating safeguards, pausing, stopping, rolling back, providing remedy or restoration, reauthorizing, and terminating.

Every action preserves the responsible owner, affected people, due date, evidence requirement, acceptance test, residual harm, challenge route, and closure receipt. Completion of an action does not automatically establish that the harm was repaired.

## Sunset and renewal

Renewal requires a fresh decision against the current evidence, implementation record, harms, distribution, challenges, authority, and alternatives. The previous authorization cannot extend itself.

If the review is late or evidence is incomplete, the contract follows the authorization terms: expire, hold, restrict, or seek a bounded extension through the proper authority. Silent continuation is disabled.

## Public correction and propagation

A reversal or remediation decision propagates to the decision docket, commitment ledger, impact audit, source and signal interpretation, named file, pathway, map, local system, Outcomes Watch, update, export, and Phase 64 matrix without rewriting prior history.

The [Phase 75 registry](/evidence/accountability/) exposes the inactive controls. No trigger, audit, sunset, reversal, remediation, compensation, restoration, correction, withdrawal, reviewer, or receipt currently exists.

It retains negative results, failures, harms, distribution, dissent, exceptions, shared dependencies, cumulative burden, residual duties, and permanent archives while creating zero comparisons, lessons, transfer findings, portfolio conclusions, supersessions, retirements, decommissioning closures, receipts, scores, rankings, or stage changes.

The [Public Deliberation Registry](/evidence/deliberation/) adds eight inactive standing and notice registers, eight inactive deliberation and response dockets, eight inactive mandate and appeal registers, and eight inactive adaptive-review ledgers. It preserves rights, access, consultation, consent boundaries, comments, hearings, dissent, appeals, future-user interests, and reopening duties while creating zero standing decisions, participation records, reasoned responses, legitimacy findings, mandates, adaptive reviews, receipts, scores, rankings, or stage changes.

The [Interjurisdictional Compacts Registry](/evidence/compacts/) adds eight inactive authority and externality maps, eight inactive shared-value contribution compacts, eight inactive mutual-aid continuity and dispute registers, and eight inactive emergency-authority normalization ledgers. It preserves jurisdiction, treaties, rights, externalities, allocation, contributions, continuity, disputes, civil safeguards, expiry, restoration, and democratic reauthorization while creating zero compacts, activations, emergencies, extensions, rights suspensions, normalization decisions, receipts, scores, rankings, or stage changes.
