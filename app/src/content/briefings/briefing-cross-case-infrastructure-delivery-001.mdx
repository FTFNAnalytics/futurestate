## Similar projects are not the same system

A fabrication plant, housing approval, transmission expansion, launch facility, and mineral-processing project can all be described as infrastructure delivery. That category helps readers find related work, but it does not establish comparability. Each project sits inside a different authority structure, financing model, permitting stack, physical network, labor market, acceptance regime, and local burden envelope.

Phase 76 therefore compares exact decisions rather than category labels. A lesson can travel only after both decisions have independent Phase 75 audits and the pair passes a separate sixteen-gate comparability review.

## Match the delivery mechanism

The review asks what was authorized, who committed resources, which capacity already existed, which dependencies sat outside the implementer, and what counted as accepted delivery. “Construction started,” “facility completed,” “service energized,” “permit issued,” and “benefit realized” remain distinct events.

A project delivered through a regulated utility tariff may not teach the same institutional lesson as one delivered through municipal servicing conditions or a federal licence. A capital milestone can transfer as a scheduling practice while its authorization model, risk allocation, or community safeguards do not.

## Context is part of the finding

Transfer conditions record geography, technical maturity, workforce depth, supplier concentration, grid and water capacity, procurement rules, affected populations, exposure periods, and external shocks. Reviewers distinguish a mechanism that plausibly travels from a background condition that happened to be present.

This prevents a successful outcome in a high-capacity jurisdiction from becoming a universal recommendation for places without the same institutions, assets, fiscal room, or remedy paths.

## Failure is reusable evidence

Delayed, cancelled, narrowed, paused, or harmful projects are not removed from the learning set. Failure records can reveal dependency fragility, unrealistic baselines, missing authority, weak acceptance criteria, or safeguards that acted too late. Null results can show that an intervention did not overcome the binding constraint.

Negative evidence is useful only when its identity and context remain intact. Phase 76 keeps the failed condition, dissent, correction, harm, and remediation lineage attached to the lesson.
