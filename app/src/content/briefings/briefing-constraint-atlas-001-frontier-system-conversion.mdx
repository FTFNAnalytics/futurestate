## The shared pattern

The five local systems are not comparable industries, but they expose a common delivery pattern:

**demand, policy, or resource → authority and finance → local receiving systems → physical delivery → acceptance → operation → measured outcome**

The Southwest chip corridor and Northern Virginia compute corridor turn technology demand into power, water, construction, and workforce requirements. Nevada turns mineral policy into water, permit, finance, commissioning, and customer-qualification requirements. The Space Coast turns mission demand into site and operator authority, shared infrastructure, services, and range utilization. Ontario turns housing targets into legal approvals, servicing, permits, construction, and occupied homes.

## The recurring bottlenecks

### Power

System forecasts, service territories, rates, and transmission decisions reveal the delivery framework. They do not establish an executed service agreement, completed infrastructure, energization, metered load, spare capacity, or reliability for a named project.

### Water and environment

Plans, agreements, rights, and permits define governance and conditions. They do not establish completed works, accepted capacity, actual use, treatment performance, compliance, mitigation, or durable environmental outcomes.

### Workforce and suppliers

Training programs, consortia, and regional labor baselines identify delivery mechanisms and context. They do not establish occupation-specific sufficiency, completion, placement, retention, vacancy reduction, or supplier readiness.

### Authority and public acceptance

Council decisions, land-use rules, site licences, environmental records, and mining permits are real legal stages. They remain distinct from condition satisfaction, construction, operating authority, use, and locally accepted outcomes.

### Construction, acceptance, and qualification

This is the common weak point. Topping out, a conditional route, financial close, a capital plan, or an environmental decision can all be meaningful without establishing that an asset is complete, inspected, commissioned, accepted, qualified, used, or reliable.

## Comparison boundary

The Atlas compares the shape of evidence dependencies. It does not rank corridors, create a readiness score, normalize unlike units, or imply technological and economic equivalence. Quantitative comparison must wait for compatible identities, periods, definitions, denominators, methods, and outcome stages.
