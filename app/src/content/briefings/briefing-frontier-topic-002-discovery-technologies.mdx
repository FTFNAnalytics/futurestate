## Evidence shelf

The shelf establishes available NISAR public radar data and funded data-system infrastructure intended to support research and AI.

The Phase 65 review shelf is published in [Phase 65 Undercovered Frontier Systems](/research/phase65-undercovered-frontier-systems-2023-2026/). The shelf makes the evidence trail navigable; it does not change the source record's authority or stage.

## Interpretation boundary

It does not establish sustained use, reproduced findings, local decisions, productivity gains, or scientific impact attributable to either capability.

This briefing therefore keeps proposal, authorization, commitment, implementation, validation, acceptance, recurring operation, and comparable outcome as separate editorial states. A record can be strong evidence of one state while remaining silent about the next.

## Reporting questions

- Public radar data and research-data awards create observable capability, not demonstrated downstream decisions.
- A testbed, dataset, or award becomes evidence of impact only through named users and reproducible work.
- Discovery reporting should preserve access, use, validation, and outcome as separate stages.

## Next records

- Named institutional users and documented workflows
- Reproducible analyses tied to a versioned dataset or instrument
- Independent decisions or outcomes that cite the research output
