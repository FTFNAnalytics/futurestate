## Why this desk exists

Phase 68 answers a narrow question: when is a set of observations actually a comparable series? The answer cannot be “when two numbers are available.” A series needs a stable named entity, exact operating scope, accepted recurrence, stable measure and denominator, compatible periods and methods, and a visible trail of exceptions, breaks, revisions, and alternative explanations.

The current result is intentionally sparse. All eight named files remain in **Acquisition**. The registry publishes the admission contract and candidate measures, not trend lines, values, rankings, or outcome claims.

## Eight compatibility tests

1. **Identity:** every observation resolves to the same named entity, facility, project, service, or adoption case.
2. **Scope:** geography, asset, population, receiving system, and stage remain explicit and compatible.
3. **Accepted recurrence:** repeated service, output, use, shipment, monitoring, or operation is accepted for that same entity.
4. **Measure:** definition, numerator, unit, and attribution boundary remain stable.
5. **Denominator:** population, capacity, asset count, service exposure, output, or customer denominator is stable or reconciled.
6. **Period:** cadence, partial periods, and missing observations are visible and compatible.
7. **Method:** collection, calculation, corrections, revisions, and version changes remain inspectable.
8. **Exceptions:** failures, outages, rejected units, adverse observations, breaks, and alternative explanations remain visible.

## What breaks a series

A series stops at an unreconciled change in entity, facility, operator, geography, service, product, customer, permit, algorithm, configuration, unit, denominator, period, method, revision policy, or exception definition. A bridge must explain the change; interpolation, aggregation, or editorial convenience cannot repair it.

The no-transfer rule is especially important here. Citywide housing totals cannot fill a project file. Regional workforce evidence cannot fill a fab result. Testing miles cannot fill passenger-service outcomes. An environmental decision cannot fill site-and-operator mission performance. Different lithium projects cannot become one production series.

## Admission workflow

1. Return a real artifact through the correct Phase 67 recurrence or outcome packet.
2. Verify entity, authority, scope, date, method, denominator, exception, and correction history.
3. Establish accepted recurring operation before testing an outcome series.
4. Compare every observation across all eight compatibility dimensions.
5. Preserve breaks and adverse observations; do not smooth or omit them.
6. Require independent human review and a bounded Phase 60 receipt.
7. Propagate the decision through the source, signal, ledger, gate, matrix, dossiers, pathways, maps, Outcomes Watch, update log, export, and release checks.
