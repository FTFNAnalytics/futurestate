## The conversion problem

The strongest batch-two records do not answer whether industrial capacity has arrived. They identify where that answer must be built.

GAO shows that substitution and recycling are constrained by infrastructure, economics, standards, and manufacturing qualification. DOE names 19 processing selections, but the projects still need executed awards, sites, permits, equipment, validation, customers, and production. EIA provides generator inventory and plant operations, but neither dataset alone establishes deliverable service for a named load.

## National inputs are not local outcomes

Four national evidence classes now have clearer boundaries:

1. a technology assessment describes options and adoption constraints;
2. a selection announcement creates project follow-up lanes;
3. generator inventory and plant operations establish different power-system facts; and
4. labor and housing datasets establish regional baselines.

None of these stages proves that a local project can secure material, power, water, workers, housing, permits, financing, or customers on its delivery schedule.

## The local records are becoming inspectable

Phoenix's preliminary capital program names the North Gateway Water Reclamation Plant and assigns it project code WS90320008. That is a stronger trail than a generic wastewater claim, but the record remains upstream of adopted authorization, procurement, construction, commissioning, operating capacity, and service.

Toronto's capital-project pipeline creates a similar upstream rail for infrastructure expected to enter procurement. The pipeline does not guarantee a solicitation, award, construction start, accepted asset, or service outcome.

## What this synthesis supports

The combined evidence supports a method: follow every capacity claim through a stage-specific chain and preserve the difference between national inputs and local conversion.

## What this synthesis does not support

It does not support a regional readiness score, an industrial-capacity total, a project delivery forecast, a workforce-sufficiency claim, an available-utility-capacity claim, or a conclusion that announced funding has produced operating supply.
