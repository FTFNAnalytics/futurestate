A credential is not demonstrated capability without valid accessible assessment, application, transfer, uncertainty, human judgment, challenge, correction, and longitudinal evidence.

## Evidence architecture

The Phase 87 chain begins only after verified population health and wellbeing. It separates early-childhood and school access, inclusion, teaching and learning; postsecondary, vocational, apprenticeship, adult-learning, affordability and institutional continuity; learning, assessment, credentials, demonstrated capability and pathways into decent work and civic life; then libraries, research, public knowledge, media and information literacy, arts, culture, crisis continuity, community-learning recovery and long-horizon human development. Every transition needs lawful authority, learner and community standing, complete denominators, accessible participation, distribution review, independent challenge, human decision and a dated receipt.

## Early childhood, schools, access, inclusion, and learning

Count lawful enrollment, attendance, participation, belonging, teaching conditions, supports, exclusion, return, progression and actual learning. Preserve disability accommodation, language, Indigenous jurisdiction, family support, transport, food, health, safety, affordability, educator capacity and continuity. Enrollment is not learning; attendance volume cannot substitute for valid evidence of learning, capability, agency, wellbeing, relationships or rights.

## Postsecondary, vocational, apprenticeship, and affordability

Trace admission, denial, transfer, tuition, complete learner costs, grants, debt, housing, food, care, devices, unpaid practice, faculty and instructor capacity, facilities, placements, supervision, persistence, stop-out, completion, teach-out and record portability. A training seat is not access to decent work. Completion must remain separate from capability, credential recognition, licensure, hiring, job quality, pay, conditions, retention, further learning and civic pathways.

## Assessment, credentials, capability, and transition

Define the capability, context, curriculum, pedagogy, practice, feedback, assessment validity, reliability, accessibility, bias, uncertainty and human judgment. Preserve multiple forms of demonstration, prior learning, Indigenous and community knowledge, portability, revocation, appeal and correction. A credential is not demonstrated capability, and neither a credential nor training completion proves a durable work, public-service or civic outcome.

## Public knowledge, culture, information literacy, and recovery

Public knowledge needs libraries, archives, schools, universities, media, arts and cultural institutions; accessible hours and formats; provenance, methods and uncertainty; peer challenge, correction and preservation; translation, extension, public explanation and community use. Published research is not usable public knowledge, and digital access is not information literacy. Institution reopening is not community learning recovery; measure restored workers, programs, language, memory, participation, trust, cultural vitality, backlog repair and long-term access.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 86 predecessor, adopted education-access baseline, enrollment or admission decision, learning or inclusion finding, affordability or debt decision, credential or capability finding, work or civic transition conclusion, research or public-knowledge finding, cultural classification, community-learning recovery conclusion, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.

## Phase 88 labor, livelihood, and economic-democracy control

The [Work, Labor, Livelihoods And Economic Democracy Registry](/evidence/work-labor-livelihoods/) adds eight inactive job-access dossiers, eight inactive job-quality ledgers, eight inactive worker-voice and economic-democracy registers, and eight inactive livelihood-security and just-transition ledgers. It creates zero hiring, job-quality, pay, safety, bargaining, ownership, livelihood, transition, receipt, score, ranking, or stage decisions.
