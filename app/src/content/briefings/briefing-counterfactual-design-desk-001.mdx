## Why descriptive change is not a causal effect

A same-entity series may show that a result changed. It does not reveal what would have happened without the intervention. Attribution and causal effect require a comparison with that unobserved alternative, plus assumptions strong enough to identify the contrast.

Phase 72 does not assume causal research is appropriate for every named file. Each docket begins **Inactive - No Causal Claim Proposed**. A descriptive publication may remain the strongest defensible output.

## Ten alternative-explanation categories

The register tests pre-existing trends, concurrent interventions, policy changes, markets, capacity and suppliers, workforce and learning, infrastructure and receiving systems, measurement changes, selection and composition, and external events.

Each category begins Not Assessed. Reviewers may decide that a category is applicable, not applicable with evidence, supported, weakened, unresolved, or disqualifying. They cannot convert missing analysis into “none.”

## Six design families

Phase 72 names randomized assignment, interrupted time series, difference in differences, synthetic control, matched comparison, and regression discontinuity as candidate families. Naming a family does not make it suitable.

The design must follow the assignment mechanism, intervention timing, available baseline, treatment unit, donor or control pool, spillovers, confounding risks, and exact estimand. Automatic design selection is disabled.

## Twelve design gates

The docket requires a causal question, treatment unit, outcome and estimand, baseline, comparison construction, identification assumptions, interference assessment, confounding assessment, missing-data plan, robustness and falsification tests, power and sensitivity plan, and pre-registration with dual review and propagation.

Every assumption must be falsifiable or explicitly limited. Parallel trends cannot be asserted without evidence. A convenient neighboring project is not automatically a control. A before-and-after contrast is not automatically causal.

## Registration before result inspection

The design, exclusions, estimand, comparison set, model, robustness tests, and failure thresholds must be frozen before result inspection. Phase 72 sets `result_inspection_allowed` to false in every docket. A registered receipt is the boundary that may later permit the planned analysis.

Changing the design after inspection requires a visible amendment and cannot be represented as the original pre-registration.

## Publication boundary

A registered design still does not guarantee a causal result. Reviewers must inspect execution, assumption tests, uncertainty, robustness, adverse evidence, and deviations. Phase 72 publishes no design, result, effect estimate, attribution, causal claim, comparison, score, or rank.
