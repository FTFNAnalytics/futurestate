## Exact identity resolution

Twenty action keys now have a direct official identity: a GAO report number and recommendation number shown together on the current product page. FTFN does not invent a separate opaque database ID.

## Held mappings

HHS-04 combines GAO-24-106276 Recommendations 1 and 2. VA-02 combines GAO-25-106874 Recommendations 1 and 2. Both stay In Review until recommendation-specific child records preserve the split.

## Response and outcome boundary

Eighteen exact recommendations are Open and two are Open – Partially Addressed. Those statuses and the associated agency responses support follow-through, not implementation, closure, agency performance, operating outcome, value, or causation. The Phase 56F ledger therefore remains unchanged.
