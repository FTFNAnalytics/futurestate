## Conversion stack

The critical-minerals corpus now connects national production and import reliance to commodity-specific gallium exposure, federal funding and technology programs, and named projects involving heavy rare earths, nickel, and lithium. This is enough to identify distinct supply-chain questions, but not to declare that domestic supply is secure.

The next content must pair each commodity with its actual receiving system and each facility with its downstream delivery trail. Relevant records include permits and agreements, construction, refining or separation equipment, commissioning, product specifications, customer qualification, accepted lots, offtake, recurring output, recycling capacity, and demonstrated substitution.

## Boundary

Import reliance is not a forecast of shortage. A funding selection is not an executed award. An award or loan is not built capacity. Nameplate capacity is not qualified output. Technology options for substitution or recycling are not adopted commercial capacity. Every claim should retain its commodity, facility, stage, period, unit, method, and acceptance boundary.
