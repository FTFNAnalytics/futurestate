## Why one result is not a body of evidence

Phase 73 can eventually produce a bounded, adjudicated result. It cannot establish that the result travels to another population, agrees with an independent method, survives contradictory evidence, or deserves body-level language. Phase 74 gives each result a separate eligibility decision and each named file a synthesis protocol.

All thirty-two inputs remain **Inactive - No Adjudicated Result**. No effect, interval, claim, replication, correction status, reviewer, or receipt has entered synthesis.

## Fourteen synthesis-input gates

Each result must preserve its adjudication lineage, question, population, setting, measure, estimand, scale, intervention or exposure, comparison, time basis, design, assumptions, provenance, uncertainty, deviations, robustness, adverse evidence, replication, bounded claim, correction readiness, and dual-review receipt.

An input decision is not a vote for the preferred conclusion. Exclusion reasons remain visible, and several results sharing one cohort, source, operator, codebase, or assumption cannot be counted as independent confirmation.

## Fourteen body-level synthesis gates

Before synthesis, reviewers lock the exact question and enumerate every eligible, excluded, null, adverse, corrected, withdrawn, and missing result. They then assess independence, measure compatibility, design diversity, direction, magnitude, uncertainty, heterogeneity, bias, transfer, triangulation, replication, claim language, dissent, and correction readiness.

A synthesis may hold or narrow when studies cannot be combined. Pooling is one possible method, never the default and never automatic.

## Compatibility and independence

Compatible labels do not guarantee compatible estimands. Two studies may name the same outcome while changing the denominator, follow-up, intervention version, comparison group, geography, or decision threshold. Transformations must be declared, reversible, and reviewed.

Independence is also explicit. Repeated analyses of the same source or cohort can test robustness, but they do not create independent replication merely because they use different code or language.

## Triangulation and replication portfolios

Triangulation asks whether independent designs, measures, mechanisms, settings, and negative evidence support the same bounded interpretation. A replication portfolio records direct replications, conceptual replications, reproduction attempts, disagreements, and unresolved failures without treating agreement as a score.

## Evidence grades and bounded language

Seven qualitative classes run from no eligible adjudicated results through single, mixed, descriptive, associative, attribution, and causal bodies. The selected grade constrains the sentence; it does not summarize institutional importance or choose an intervention.

No grade may be assigned automatically. A body with strong results can still remain mixed because of dependence, transfer limits, harms, corrections, or material contradictions.

## Contradictions, dissent, and correction

Ten contradiction categories cover direction, magnitude, time, population, measures, intervention, design, provenance, adverse evidence, and correction lineage. A contradiction can be unresolved without being erased or averaged away.

Dissent remains attached to the exact synthesis version. A corrected or withdrawn input triggers reevaluation of every dependent synthesis, challenge response, and decision translation.

## Synthesis boundary

The [Evidence Synthesis And Decision Registry](/evidence/synthesis/) and [public export](/data/evidence-synthesis-challenge-decision-translation.json) expose empty contracts. They contain no adjudicated input, synthesis, contradiction decision, evidence grade, body-level claim, recommendation, reviewer, or receipt.
