Separate reserve balances from usable, lawful and restorable emergency capability.

## Governed physical-economy chain

Phase 94 begins only after a verified Phase 93 productive-transformation record. It separates reliable industrial utilities; qualified and circular material supply; maintained manufacturing systems and accepted production; then logistics, inventories, reserves, emergency conversion, resilient access, technology sovereignty and just industrial transition. Each transition requires authority, exact identities, baselines, dependencies, distribution, alternatives, stress tests, independent review, a human decision and a dated receipt.

## Energy, water, and industrial utilities

Trace resources, assets, operators, networks, demand, interconnections, outages, tariffs, affordability, environmental burdens and restoration. Installed capacity is not delivered reliable energy.

## Minerals, materials, processing, and circularity

Trace deposits, grades, recovery, refining, purification, specifications, assays, standards, customer qualification, supplier tiers, rights, restoration and secondary recovery. Resource endowment is not qualified material supply.

## Manufacturing, quality, and accepted production

Trace factories, lines, tooling, automation, maintenance, workforce, suppliers, process control, yield, customer acceptance and field reliability. A factory announcement is not accepted production.

## Logistics, reserves, sovereignty, and just transition

Trace orders, inventory, shipment custody, modes, nodes, access, allocation, reserves, conversion, mutual aid, circular flows, technology and worker/community transitions. Logistics throughput is not resilient access. Domestic-content percentage is not technological sovereignty. A decarbonization target is not a just industrial transition.

## Decision boundary

The registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 93 predecessor, utility, material, manufacturing, logistics, reserve, conversion, sovereignty or transition decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
