## What Changed

Phase 55X moved the three local journeys from broad authority shelves into implementation dossiers. The new records are deliberately uneven: some create current forecast or regulatory context, some expose project-specific obligations, and two Florida records close an actual construction or operating stage.

## Northern Virginia

The 2026 PJM forecast makes large-load uncertainty more explicit. Revised Virginia generator guidance provides a current permit and testing baseline. PUR-2026-00021 creates another named transmission case, and three 2026 air permits create facility-level compliance trails. None of those records establishes completed transmission, customer energization, tested generators, water service, or community acceptance. Project Raspberry is retained as a Virginia multi-permit comparator, not as evidence about Northern Virginia capacity.

## Nevada

Rhyolite Ridge's renewed water permit identifies pre-operation notice, inspection, modeling, and monitoring gates. The official air-permit file exposes detailed controls but cannot support a current-valid-permit claim because the displayed document contains unresolved draft and expiration markers. Thacker Pass records add formal decision provenance, reclamation assurance, and a monitor-test-report air-compliance contract. They do not establish current renewal, commissioning, qualified lithium carbonate, sustained output, or local outcomes.

## Florida Space Coast

FAA completed the LC-39A Starship environmental decision, but operator licensing remains separate. NASA's causeway bridge record closes construction and opening for a named shared asset. The prescribed-burn record documents one real multi-agency operation during an active countdown. The PHSF record adds a mission-linked facility-upgrade trail, while SIMO remains market engagement for a future service contract.

## Publication Decision

Eight of the twelve signals are Published with narrow claims. Four remain In Review: the Morrisville-Wishing Star case, Project Raspberry comparator, Rhyolite Ridge air-permit validity record, and NASA SIMO planned acquisition. The three local pathways remain In Review because the new evidence does not close service, qualified-output, or mission-use conclusions.
