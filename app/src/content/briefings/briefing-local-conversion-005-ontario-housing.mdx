## The conversion question

Ontario housing needs a delivery ledger, not another comparison of targets and announcements. The relevant chain begins with a named application and follows it through legal authority, servicing, building permission, construction, completion, and occupancy.

Toronto's development pipeline and CMHC's June 2026 stage baseline distinguish potential supply, approvals awaiting starts, active construction, starts, and completions. Application 24 254930 provides the canonical named trail. It has progressed beyond a staff recommendation to a bounded Council-stage record while retaining enactment and condition gates. Toronto Water, IESO, and Toronto Hydro proceedings provide infrastructure context.

## What remains open

Aggregate stock and flow measures do not form a matched cohort. Council adoption is not enacted amendments. Enactment is not satisfied servicing, a building permit, a start, a completed unit, or occupancy. A citywide capital plan and provincial demand outlook do not allocate capacity to a named development.

The next decisive artifact is the linked sequence for the selected application: enacted instruments, satisfied conditions, a building permit, construction start, completion, and occupancy. Broader conclusions should use matched municipal or project cohorts that preserve cancellations, financing, and timing.

## Reader use

Read the system as **target → application → legal authority → servicing → permit → start → completion → occupancy**. Report movement at the exact stage reached and keep citywide context separate from project delivery.
