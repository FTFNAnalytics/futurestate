## The accountability gap

Evidence can narrow uncertainty. It cannot choose the decision maker, supply legal authority, settle value judgments, allocate resources, or absorb responsibility for harms. Phase 75 therefore begins with the decision record rather than the preferred outcome.

All eight accountability dossiers remain **Inactive - No Authorized Institutional Decision**. No decision owner, selected option, rationale, authorization, condition, reviewer, or receipt has been entered.

## Sixteen gates before a decision record opens

The contract fixes the exact decision and version, responsible owner, governing authority, question, complete option set, status quo, supporting synthesis, challenge lineage, values, benefits, harms, affected groups, feasibility, conflicts, recusals, dissent, rationale, recommendation boundary, conditions, monitoring, sunset, independent review, receipt, notice, and propagation.

A signature without scope is not enough. An authority citation without a current delegation is not enough. A recommendation copied into an authorization instrument is still two artifacts with two accountable actors.

## Options and the status quo

The option set must include doing nothing, delaying, piloting, narrowing, conditioning, phasing, reversing, and stopping when those choices are genuinely available. Reviewers record which options were considered, excluded, or combined and why.

The status quo is not a zero-cost baseline. Its ongoing harms, distribution, capacity demands, and irreversible effects receive the same scrutiny as an active intervention.

## Evidence and judgment remain separate

The Phase 74 synthesis, evidence grade, contradictions, external challenges, uncertainty, and dissent remain attached to the decision version. The decision record then identifies where evidence ends and institutional judgment begins.

That boundary makes legitimate disagreement visible. Two decision makers can read the same evidence and prefer different options because they weight risk, delay, equity, reversibility, or public obligation differently.

## Conflicts, recusal, and dissent

Funding, organizational interests, prior advocacy, implementation ownership, and personal conflicts are recorded before the decision. Recusal is a named event with scope and replacement authority; it cannot be inferred from silence.

Dissent stays attached to the selected option and rationale. A minority position is neither erased by the final vote nor converted into a separate recommendation automatically.

## Conditions, sunset, and receipt

An authorization can be unconditional, conditional, time-limited, pilot-only, or prohibited. Conditions identify who must act, what evidence must return, which thresholds govern pause or stop-work, and when renewal requires a new decision.

The [Decision Accountability And Realized Impact Registry](/evidence/accountability/) and [public export](/data/decision-accountability-realized-impact.json) expose these empty contracts. They do not assert that any recommendation was made or any institution acted.
