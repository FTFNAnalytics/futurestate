## Adoption stage

ARIA 0.1 evaluated seven applications from five organizations across model testing, red teaming, and field testing. It is a concrete assurance artifact, while NASA's public use-case record illustrates why deployment inventories still need comparable operating measures.

## Boundary and next record

A pilot is not certification, and an inventory is not assured performance. Reopen when a named system evaluation is tied to authorization, deployment monitoring, incidents, corrective action, independent review, or stable mission outcomes.
