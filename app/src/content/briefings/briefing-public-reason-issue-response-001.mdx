## Intake is only the beginning

Public-comment systems can count submissions, publish transcripts, tag topics, and acknowledge receipt while leaving the substantive decision unchanged and unexplained. Phase 77 requires a material-issue matrix that connects each question to the relevant evidence, authority, alternative, affected groups, proposed response, decision effect, dissent, and unresolved uncertainty.

This does not require a unique essay for every repeated sentence. It requires assurance that distinct positions are not lost when submissions are grouped and that the final response addresses the substance rather than the volume or tone of participation.

## Materiality is reviewable

A convenor decides whether an issue is within scope, supported, duplicative, resolved, deferred, or outside authority. Those classifications can themselves exclude important evidence. Phase 77 therefore records the reason, reviewer, challenge path, and version history of each materiality decision.

An issue outside one agency’s authority may still be relevant to cumulative burden, implementation feasibility, rights, or another responsible institution. The response should identify the boundary and referral rather than using jurisdiction as a reason to erase the concern.

## Respond to alternatives and no action

Deliberation is weak when participants can comment only on a finished proposal. The common record should expose the decision question, plausible alternatives, status quo, deferral, no action, safeguards, sequencing choices, and exit options early enough to matter.

The response explains what evidence supports or rejects each material alternative, which tradeoffs remain value judgments, who bears effects, what conditions were added, and what uncertainty remains. “Not preferred” is not a reason unless the public objective and decision criteria are stated.

## Comment count is not evidentiary weight

Large numbers can reveal salience, mobilization, or process reach. They do not prove factual accuracy, representative coverage, rights compliance, equitable distribution, consent, or legitimacy. Coordinated campaigns and repeated templates may still raise material issues; a single submission may expose a decisive safety, rights, accessibility, or evidence problem.

Phase 77 reports volume descriptively and keeps it separate from evidence quality and public authority. It prohibits automatic sentiment scoring, majority-rule inference, comment weighting by institutional status, and legitimacy scores.

## Reasons create accountability

A reasoned response is specific enough that a reader can see how the decision maker understood the issue, what evidence and authority it used, what alternatives it considered, why it accepted or rejected the position, what conditions or remedies follow, and how the issue can be appealed or reopened.

Independent review checks for omissions, circular reasoning, unsupported inference, conflict, inconsistency, unequal treatment, and missing adverse evidence. Minority rationale and unresolved disagreement remain attached to the closure receipt.

## Closure is not permanence

The response docket can close only the defined process and record version. New evidence, corrected data, changed conditions, implementation failure, a material incident, an appeal, or missed condition can reopen the mandate. Phase 77 links each future reasoned response to explicit adaptive triggers.

No Phase 77 comment, issue, response, dissent, closure receipt, or reopening event currently exists. The issue matrix is empty because no authorized participation process has opened.
