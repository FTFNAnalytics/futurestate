## Current project stage

The Virginia record now exposes both the commercial rule and one named transmission project. GS-5 sets future large-load terms; Golden-Mars carries a conditional approval with a specific local dependency. Together they make the delivery stack inspectable without proving capacity is available to a customer.

## Decision boundary

A forecast is not a commitment. A rate is not a contract. A certificate is not a constructed asset, and an energized asset is not by itself durable customer service or acceptable local performance.

## Next conversion record

Track the final route and land decision, construction, inspections, energization, a named customer agreement, metered demand, reliability, cost allocation, permit compliance, and community effects as separate records.
