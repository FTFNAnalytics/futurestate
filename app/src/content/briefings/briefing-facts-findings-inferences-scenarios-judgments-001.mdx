Keep observations, administrative and scientific findings, synthesis, scenarios and judgment distinct.

## Governed public-knowledge completion chain

Phase 102 begins only after a verified Phase 101 civilizational-resilience record. It separates canonical synthesis and claim lineage; civic decision literacy and public reason; accessible reader navigation and translation; then content completeness, correction, archives and evergreen stewardship.

## Canonical synthesis

Trace claims to sources, decisions, receipts, revisions, uncertainty and counterevidence. A synthesis is not new evidence, and an explanation is not a recommendation or authorization.

## Civic decision literacy

Trace decision authority, baselines, options, tradeoffs, distribution, rights, causation, scenarios, delivery capability and accountability without covertly deciding for the reader.

## Reader navigation and access

Trace orientation, learning paths, cross-links, progressive disclosure, accessibility, translation and low-bandwidth access. Navigation is not comprehension or access.

## Content closure and evergreen stewardship

Trace coverage, gaps, source health, claim lineage, maintenance owners, refresh triggers, correction, archives and retained history. Publication volume is not completeness, an archive is not erasure, and a stable page is not evergreen truth.

## Decision boundary

The registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 101 predecessor, synthesis, literacy, navigation, accessibility, completeness or evergreen decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
