Declarations and reopening do not prove readiness or recovery; measure exercises, continuity, backlog, disability equity, workforce recovery, morbidity, mortality, function, trust, and long-horizon wellbeing.

## Evidence architecture

The Phase 86 chain begins only after verified housing and place stability. It separates primary, preventive, community, home, and accessible care; acute, emergency, hospital, specialty, behavioral-health, medicines, workforce, quality, and continuity; public-health surveillance, vaccination, outbreak response, environmental and occupational exposure; then disability rights, equity, preparedness, recovery, and long-horizon population wellbeing. Each transition needs lawful authority, affected-public standing, complete denominators, uncertainty, accessible participation, distribution review, independent challenge, human decision, and a dated receipt.

## Access, prevention, affordability, and continuity

Count attachment, wait, travel, accessibility, language, eligibility, referral, denial, cost, lost income, medicines, follow-up, and continuity rather than listed providers or coverage alone. Nominal capacity is not timely access, and coverage is not affordable care. Preserve Indigenous jurisdiction, disability rights, informed consent, privacy, cultural safety, rural and remote reach, and appeal.

## Acute, specialty, behavioral, and life-course care

Trace emergency response, transport, triage, boarding, transfer, surgery, diagnostics, medicines, staffing, adverse events, discharge, readmission, rehabilitation, home care, and palliative care. Include mental health, substance use, harm reduction, reproductive, maternal, newborn, child, youth, adult, and elder care. An encounter is not a health outcome; volume cannot substitute for safety, function, autonomy, relief, continuity, or lived experience.

## Public health, surveillance, exposure, and rights

Separate laboratory, clinical, syndromic, genomic, wastewater, environmental, and community signals from verified cases and diagnoses. Define denominators, sampling, missingness, uncertainty, false positives, false negatives, bias, drift, lawful purpose, privacy, correction, deletion, and limits on secondary use. A surveillance signal is not a diagnosis. Exposure findings require enforcement, clinical follow-up, income support, compensation, remediation, and burden reduction.

## Disability, preparedness, recovery, and wellbeing

Preserve legal capacity, supported decision-making, accessible communication, personal assistance, home and community supports, rehabilitation, technology, independent living, safety, participation, and remedy. An emergency declaration is not readiness; require plans, exercises, stockpiles, surge, accessible warning and evacuation, continuity, mutual aid, after-action review, and correction. Service reopening is not population recovery; measure backlog, workforce and caregiver recovery, morbidity, mortality, function, pain, autonomy, belonging, trust, equity, and intergenerational wellbeing.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 85 predecessor, adopted health-access baseline, eligibility or coverage decision, diagnosis, triage decision, clinical quality or safety finding, surveillance or exposure finding, restriction or emergency authorization, disability classification, preparedness conclusion, recovery or population-wellbeing finding, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance, or operating-outcome change.
