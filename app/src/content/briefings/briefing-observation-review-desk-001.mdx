## Why review begins after intake

Phase 69 defines what a future packet must contain. Phase 70 defines how humans review that packet. Structural completeness is only permission to begin review; it is not evidence acceptance.

All thirty-two review dockets remain **Awaiting Submission**. No artifact, value, reviewer, decision, receipt, observation identifier, or revision version exists.

## Twelve review dimensions

| Review | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Specification and cohort | One exact measurement and cohort identity |
| Entity and scope | Same facility, project, service, geography, population, and stage |
| Source and authority | Public source and responsible issuing or acceptance authority |
| Artifact provenance | Stable locator, capture, version, and custody trail |
| Date and period | Observed date, coverage period, cadence, gaps, and partial periods |
| Numerator | Source-declared quantity and unit |
| Denominator | Source-declared denominator, unit, or explicit not-applicable basis |
| Method | Collection, calculation, test, threshold, and version |
| Acceptance | Receiving authority and same-entity recurrence basis |
| Exceptions | Failures, outages, exclusions, rejections, and adverse observations |
| Revision and breaks | Corrections, supersessions, withdrawals, restatements, and breaks |
| Receipt and propagation | Bounded decision receipt and every assigned reader surface |

## Dual-control decision path

1. A real artifact enters one exact Phase 69 envelope.
2. Structural validation checks all eighteen required fields.
3. The first reviewer decides all twelve review dimensions.
4. A different authorized reviewer repeats the decision and records conflicts or recusal.
5. Any discrepancy is held for adjudication; it cannot be silently resolved.
6. A bounded decision may reject, hold, or accept an observation.
7. Only after the decision can a receipt and complete propagation be recorded.

## Revision lineage

Each review docket has an empty lineage register. A correction never overwrites the earlier observation silently. The register must preserve the prior version, changed fields, issuing authority, reason, effective periods, reviewer decision, supersession or withdrawal state, and downstream propagation.

## Reject and hold rules

Reject a packet that changes entity, specification, source-declared units, or evidence identity without a new contract. Hold a packet with incomplete authority, provenance, period, method, denominator, exception, revision, break, dual-review, or propagation records. Never impute a missing value or convert reviewer silence into agreement.

## Publication boundary

The [review registry](/evidence/review/) and [public export](/data/observation-review-series-admission.json) publish empty workflow contracts. They contain no real submission, review, decision, receipt, revision event, accepted observation, series point, score, comparison, or outcome.
