## The missing middle

Public records often jump from authorization to completion language. The missing middle is the commitment: who will deliver what, for whom, with which resources, by what date, under which dependencies, and against what acceptance test.

Phase 75 creates thirty-two ledgers, one for each named measure lineage. Every ledger remains **Inactive - No Authorized Decision** and **Inactive - No Implementation Commitment**.

## A commitment is a versioned artifact

The commitment identifies its owner, scope, beneficiaries, authorization, amendment history, budget, procurement path, workforce, assets, institutional capacity, dependencies, milestones, and completion criteria. A press release, appropriation, contract award, construction start, installed input, or forecast cannot substitute for the exact commitment.

When scope changes, the old version remains visible. The ledger does not overwrite an original promise with a later, easier target.

## Baselines before activity

Resource, capacity, and outcome baselines are registered before implementation claims. The baseline records the date, unit, denominator, method, coverage, missingness, known limitations, and revision rule.

Without a baseline, later activity can still be described, but improvement, savings, avoided harm, contribution, and attribution remain unavailable.

## Milestones and acceptance

Each milestone separates scheduled, initiated, physically complete, technically tested, accepted, available for use, and repeatedly operating states. The acceptance actor, criteria, evidence, date, exceptions, and receipt stay attached.

Percent-complete summaries do not replace named milestones. Money spent does not establish delivered capacity, and installed equipment does not establish accepted service.

## Dependencies and safeguards

Permits, interconnections, suppliers, workforce, data, counterparties, facilities, financing, and operating approvals are mapped as dependencies. A dependency can hold delivery without changing the authorization record.

Safeguards define thresholds for warning, corrective action, pause, stop-work, rollback, remedy, and restoration. They are controls with named owners and evidence duties, not editorial caveats.

## Public contract

The [accountability registry](/evidence/accountability/) exposes every empty commitment and the [implementation map](/atlas/dependency-maps/recommendation-is-not-authorization-or-implementation/) shows the handoffs. No Phase 75 ledger contains a commitment, budget, baseline, milestone, accepted output, benefit, harm, reviewer, or receipt.
