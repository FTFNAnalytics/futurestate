## Why acceptance does not admit a series

An accepted observation is one reviewed point. Series admission asks a different question: can an exact set of observations remain comparable without erasing changes in identity, scope, units, denominator, period, method, authority, revision, exceptions, or attribution?

No accepted observation exists in Phase 70. Every admission docket therefore remains **Not Ready - No Reviewed Observations** and **Not Admitted**.

## Eight admission gates

1. Exact cohort, file, entity, and scope identity.
2. A set of independently reviewed observations.
3. Accepted recurring operation for the same entity.
4. Compatible measure and source-declared units.
5. Compatible denominator or exposure.
6. Compatible periods, methods, and revision history.
7. Complete adjudication of every series break.
8. Independent human admission, bounded receipt, and complete propagation.

The identity contract is present for all eight files. The remaining seven gates are Not Ready because no reviewed observations exist.

## Admission decisions

The desk may retain Not Ready, hold for compatibility, declare a packet eligible for an admission decision, admit a bounded series, or reject the proposed series. No threshold count is preselected in the empty state. The evidence, cadence, lifecycle, and receiving authority determine the human-reviewed admissibility basis.

## Breaks remain visible

A bridge can preserve two segments without making them comparable. When a break cannot be reconciled publicly, the correct result is segmentation, hold, or rejection. Forced continuity is prohibited.

## Outcome boundary

An admitted series may still be too short, incomplete, unstable, differently attributed, or unsupported for an outcome claim. Admission never creates a score, rank, cross-entity benchmark, forecast, causal interpretation, or realized outcome automatically.

## Publication boundary

The [series-admission dockets](/evidence/review/) contain eight identity contracts and zero reviewed observation sets. All decision, reviewer, receipt, series-definition, series-membership, and propagation fields remain empty.
