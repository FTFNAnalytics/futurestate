## Conversion stack

Autonomy and eVTOL evidence spans laboratory and road testing, policy, certification work, pilot selection, operating authority, and limited service. These are different states. Testing miles describe exposure. A conforming aircraft flight advances a certification program. A pilot selection creates a controlled integration lane. A service authorization defines permitted activity.

Persistent service requires additional proof: operating availability, completed rides or missions, safety exposure and events, human interventions, accessibility, customer experience, cost, geographic coverage, weather limits, complaints, and repeat performance under stable definitions.

## Boundary

Testing is not certification. Certification is not local operating permission. Permission is not availability. Availability is not adoption. A one-period operating count is not a durable safety or service trend. Cross-operator comparison remains held until identities, exposure, definitions, periods, and outcome measures align.
