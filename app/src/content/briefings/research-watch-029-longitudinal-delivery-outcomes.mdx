## What Phase 56Y adds

The batch carries stable Phase 56X identities into fifteen later-stage records while preserving every series break needed for honest longitudinal analysis.

## Funding and service delivery

DOT's May 2026 statement separates authority, obligation, and outlay for the full formula-plus-discretionary IIJA universe. NTIA's dashboard narrows 56 submitted BEAD proposals to 44 signed award agreements, while the performance notice defines how operational service must later be tested. None of these stages is a completed-project or outcome measure.

## Sustained operations and cleanup delivery

Hanford reports a completed inspection cohort, two months of on-time preventive maintenance, and a five-year laboratory turnaround improvement. Savannah River and Idaho add processed risk, inspection use cases, authorized storage capacity, and treated volume, with units and attribution boundaries intact.

## Project implementation

LAP4's current planning basis reflects approved 30 Diamond changes but awaits complete subproject baselining and acceptance. SRPPF procurement records add a leased warehouse and forecast design inputs without converting those inputs into delivered equipment or operating capability.

## Evidence boundary

No Phase 56Y record changes directive scope, implementation, closure, or the one Closed / twenty-one Partially Closed / two Open entity ledger. No ranking, composite, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or unsupported causal inference is supported.
