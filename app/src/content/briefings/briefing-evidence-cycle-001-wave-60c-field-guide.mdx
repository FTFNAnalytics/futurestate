## The next operating window

Wave 60C is the September 1–15 decision window inside Evidence Cycle 001. It is not an acquisition forecast and it does not say that any target artifact currently exists. This guide names what the editorial desk will accept, what it must reject, and which reader surfaces can change after a real review.

Six checks belong to the thirteen-item Evidence Cycle. Two more are separate Phase 61 project-file rechecks: Toronto application `24 254930` on September 9 and the Shuttle Landing Facility licence rail on September 15. Sharing a date does not merge their identities, receipts, or outcomes.

## Decision grammar

Every review ends in one of five visible states:

1. **Change Note** when an official artifact changes the bounded evidence record;
2. **No Material Change** when the correct official surfaces were reviewed and the decisive artifact was not supplied;
3. **Watch Note** when the authority, artifact, cadence, or next date changes without resolving the gate;
4. **Correction** when an existing public record requires a traceable repair;
5. **Blocked Check** when access or authority prevents a decision and the attempt, limitation, and next action can be published safely.

None of these states may be inferred from an empty search result. A negative or unavailable source check is bounded to the surfaces actually reviewed.

## What moves

A material decision can change the checked source profile, the existing underlying signal, its assigned dossier, reader pathway, dependency map, Outcomes Watch, Evidence Cycle digest, public update, and any same-entity gap, local system, conversion event, gate, or stage cell supported by the returned artifact.

The source-to-release chain remains:

`official source → receipt → signal decision → canonical dossier → pathway → dependency map → Outcomes Watch → Evidence Cycle → update and export → release verification`

## What stays held

- Availability is not adoption.
- Deployment is not accepted closeout.
- Partial flows are not a complete material balance.
- Agency plans are not an independently closed baseline.
- Reporting instructions are not completed quarterly outcomes.
- Council adoption is not enactment, permitting, construction, completion, or occupancy.
- A displayed expiry date or unchanged page is not a formal licence disposition.
- No Wave 60C artifact transfers into an unrelated Phase 61 named file.
