## Conversion stack

The post-quantum rail now has final algorithm standards, federal planning requirements, preliminary credential and interoperability work, and procurement guidance. Those are necessary layers, but they describe obligations and mechanisms rather than completed institutional change.

The decisive downstream evidence is attached to a named institution and system: inventory scope, selected implementation, procurement, integration test, validated product, cutover, operational acceptance, rollback readiness, and legacy retirement. Until those records appear, policy deadlines and standards should not be counted as deployed protection.

## Boundary

Algorithm selection is not a finished implementation. A draft credential specification is not an issued credential. A procurement path is not an award or deployed product. A migration plan is not a tested cutover. Institution-level conclusions remain held until those stages are source-explicit.
