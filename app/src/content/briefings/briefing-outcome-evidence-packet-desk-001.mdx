## Why claim language needs a contract

The same evidence can be described with verbs of very different strength. “Was authorized,” “was operating,” “increased,” “met a target,” “was associated with,” “contributed to,” and “caused” are not interchangeable. Phase 72 makes the language class visible before a reviewer decides the evidence.

All thirty-two packets remain **Empty - No Eligible Panel**. No panel has an admitted series, so no outcome question, claim text, class, verb, calculation, baseline, follow-up, uncertainty statement, reviewer, decision, or receipt exists.

## Seven claim classes

| Class | Maximum bounded use |
| --- | --- |
| Structural status | Existence, authority, acceptance, or workflow stage; not an outcome |
| Descriptive level | One reviewed same-entity level under one exact contract |
| Descriptive change | Direction and magnitude across compatible same-entity periods |
| Target attainment | A result against one compatible authoritative target |
| Association | A bounded relationship without contribution or causation language |
| Contribution or attribution | A named intervention with alternatives, comparison basis, and explicit limits |
| Causal effect | A registered counterfactual design and separately authorized result |

The taxonomy is not an automatic classifier. Reviewers must choose the proposed class, inspect the exact sentence, and reject verbs that exceed the evidence ceiling.

## Twelve packet gates

Every future packet must resolve an eligible panel and complete the exact outcome question, claim class, calculation, timing, continuity, adverse evidence, alternative explanations, attribution boundary, uncertainty, reproducibility bundle, dual review, receipt, and propagation decisions.

A structurally complete packet is eligible for human review only. Completeness does not establish truth, materiality, independence, publication fitness, or a stronger claim class.

## Adverse evidence and alternatives

The packet cannot curate only favorable periods. Failures, outages, reversals, corrections, exclusions, rejected units, missing periods, and disconfirming observations remain attached. Silence is not evidence that no alternative explanation exists.

Each packet binds one cohort-level alternative-explanation register. Reviewers record evidence for, evidence against, uncertainty, and disposition for every applicable category.

## Uncertainty and reproducibility

The proposed result must be reproducible from public, versioned inputs. The packet retains calculation rules, transformations, exclusions, corrections, provenance, and a durable result snapshot. Coverage, measurement uncertainty, sampling or model uncertainty, and sensitivity to definitions and assumptions accompany the claim.

Reproducibility does not prove validity. It lets another reviewer inspect exactly what was done.

## Publication boundary

The [Outcome Evidence And Counterfactual Registry](/evidence/claims/) and [public export](/data/outcome-evidence-counterfactual-designs.json) expose empty contracts. They contain no eligible panel, selected class, proposed sentence, calculation, result, attribution, causal design, comparison, score, rank, decision, or receipt.
