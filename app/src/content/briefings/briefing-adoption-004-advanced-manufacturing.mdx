## Conversion stack

The advanced-manufacturing corpus now spans federal roadmaps, final awards, research infrastructure, data-exchange and metrology work, workforce programs, and named construction milestones. This is a substantial implementation stack, but the records occupy different stages and cannot be summarized as one capacity number.

The decisive transition occurs after funding and construction: installed equipment, completed tests, qualified processes and products, customer acceptance, stable workforce, yield, downtime, rework, throughput, and repeated output. These measures must remain attached to the same facility, line, product, period, method, and denominator.

## Boundary

An award is not installed capability. A testbed artifact is not production interoperability. A graduate count is not workforce sufficiency. Topping out is not a qualified line. A company-reported output change is not automatically comparable across lines or attributable to one intervention.
