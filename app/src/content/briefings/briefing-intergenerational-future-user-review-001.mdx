## Absence is not consent

Future residents, users, workers, taxpayers, rights holders, ecosystems, and institutions cannot submit comments on current decisions. Yet long-lived infrastructure, land conversion, debt, data architecture, safety regimes, resource use, environmental change, technical standards, and decommissioning duties can constrain their options.

Phase 77 creates a future-user constituency class so these effects cannot be dismissed for lack of attendance. The record must explain who is representing the interest, what horizon and population are considered, what evidence and scenarios are used, and what conflicts or uncertainties limit the claim.

## Separate forecast from obligation

Long-range models and scenarios can inform risk without predicting a single future. Phase 77 records assumptions, ranges, shocks, missingness, revision cadence, and alternative pathways. A forecast does not create a duty by itself, and uncertainty does not eliminate existing duties.

The decision maker distinguishes evidence about likely effects from normative choices about precaution, fairness, resilience, debt, restoration, and acceptable irreversibility. Those choices must appear in the public reasons rather than being hidden inside a discount rate, scenario weight, or technical threshold.

## Examine lock-in and option value

Some choices are expensive but reversible. Others create path dependence through land, infrastructure, contracts, skills, standards, suppliers, data, institutional capacity, or environmental change. Phase 77 asks what options the decision closes, what future alternatives remain, who controls them, and what it would cost to pause, amend, exit, restore, or retire.

Option value is not an automatic reason to defer. It is a material consideration that should be weighed against current needs, harms, benefits, and opportunity costs. The public response explains that balance.

## Carry residual duties forward

A project may end while monitoring, remediation, data protection, service continuity, pension, workforce, asset, environmental, or treaty obligations persist. A policy may be superseded while its harms, dissent, and legal effects remain. Phase 77 links the future-user review to Phase 76 retirement and permanent-archive controls.

No successor generation should have to reconstruct why a decision was made, what conditions were imposed, which risks were accepted, what evidence was missing, or who remains responsible. Archive permanence is part of adaptive mandate, not an administrative afterthought.

## Build reopening into the mandate

Scheduled review and sunset dates protect against silent permanence. Event triggers address incidents, threshold failures, changed hazards, new technology, authority changes, distributional shifts, unmet conditions, and material new evidence. Reopening refreshes standing and notice because the affected public may have changed.

The resulting decision may continue, condition, amend, pause, reverse, sunset, or retire the mandate. A time trigger only opens review; it does not determine the outcome.

## What remains empty

Phase 77 contains no future-user representative, scenario record, irreversibility finding, residual-duty decision, sunset, trigger, adaptive review, or mandate change. The guide supplies a bounded future record while leaving every current case inactive.
