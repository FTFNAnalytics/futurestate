## Adoption stage

GSA's buyer's guide maps migration activities to acquisition support and contract vehicles. Federal policy supplies obligations and dates. The combined record makes procurement possible to trace but does not identify a completed agency migration.

## Boundary and next record

A guide is not an order; an order is not a validated integration; a test is not an accepted cutover. Reopen this case when an official agency or GSA record names the system, procurement, test, acceptance, rollback, or legacy retirement it supports.

## Phase 62 conversion timeline

- `2025-06-01` — `62-EVENT-PQC-GSA-BUYERS-GUIDE`: GSA maps post-quantum migration work to named acquisition paths.
- `2026-07-24` — `62-EVENT-PQC-FEDERAL-MIGRATION-POLICY`: federal policy establishes migration plans and dated transitions without establishing an agency cutover.

These are backfilled evidence events, not historical decision receipts. The [conversion-events export](/data/conversion-events.json) preserves the boundary between a procurement mechanism, an agency order, a tested cutover, acceptance, and retirement.
