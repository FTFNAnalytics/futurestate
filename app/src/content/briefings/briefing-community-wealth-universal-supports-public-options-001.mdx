Universal supports and community wealth require clear rights, governance, financing, access, quality, ownership, distribution, public capacity, and accountability.

## Evidence architecture

Phase 89 begins only after verified work, worker power, livelihood security, and just transition. It separates household income and fiscal resources; wealth, assets, liabilities, debt and inheritance; poverty, deprivation, social protection and benefit access; then distribution, shocks, mobility, shared prosperity and long-horizon economic security. Every transition requires lawful authority, affected-person standing, complete denominators, accessible administration, distribution review, independent challenge, human decision and a dated receipt.

## Income, taxes, transfers, and costs

Trace every earned and unearned source, tax, contribution, credit, transfer, service, essential cost, debt obligation, household composition, timing and uncertainty. An income transfer is not freedom from poverty. Earnings are not household wealth. Gross amounts cannot establish disposable resources, control, resilience, dignity or agency.

## Wealth, debt, ownership, and inheritance

Separate legal title, beneficial ownership, control, access, liquidity, valuation, encumbrance, liabilities, collective wealth, inheritance and intergenerational transfer. Asset ownership is not economic security. A home, pension, business share or account balance may be illiquid, inaccessible, indebted, volatile, controlled by another party or exposed to shock.

## Poverty, protection, and benefit access

Keep poverty thresholds, depth, persistence, recurrence and material deprivation separate from program rules, eligibility, application, take-up, adequacy, continuity, denial, sanction, appeal, restoration and remedy. Benefit eligibility is not benefit access. An administrative record cannot erase nonfilers, excluded populations or unmet need.

## Distribution, shocks, mobility, and shared prosperity

Trace growth, productivity, income, wealth, costs, services, power, place, shocks, stabilizers, recovery, upward and downward mobility, persistence and future-generation conditions. Aggregate growth is not shared prosperity. Poverty exit is not durable mobility without longitudinal security, assets, rights, public capacity and agency.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 88 predecessor, household-resource finding, tax or transfer decision, wealth or debt finding, poverty or deprivation conclusion, benefit-access decision, shared-prosperity or mobility finding, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
