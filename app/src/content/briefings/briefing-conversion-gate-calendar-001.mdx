## The dated queue

Toronto is next on September 9. The Space Coast formal-disposition recheck follows on September 15, TSMC Arizona on September 22, and Northern Virginia on October 1. Each date names a review obligation, not an expected project result. A completed check needs a real source review and one of the allowed bounded receipts. The Space Coast date follows the August 15 No Material Change receipt and does not pre-complete a second decision.

## The trigger queue

Nevada lithium, GSA post-quantum migration, NIST ARIA assurance, and Waymo California reopen only when a source-explicit downstream artifact appears. Phase 63 does not invent dates for those files. Their triggers preserve the named institution, project, operator, or receiving-system stage that the new record must reach.

## What can append

The Phase 60 Space Coast licence and Arizona wastewater checks bind directly to matching files. Loudoun standards binds conditionally to Northern Virginia only if the receipt materially changes the same receiving-system stage. The other ten cycle items cannot transfer into these files merely because they share a sector.

The complete calendar is available in the [conversion-gates export](/data/conversion-gates.json). Scheduled and trigger-based records remain open until an actual receipt and full propagation decision exist.
