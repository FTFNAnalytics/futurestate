## What changed

Phase 55Y asks a harder question than whether a policy, program, agreement, or permit exists: what evidence appears after the upstream gate?

The answer is uneven but useful. NIST has exercised a multi-level AI evaluation procedure. Arizona has operating technical-training and cleanroom assets, plus an aggregate graduate count linked qualitatively to an operating manufacturer. Chandler has completed reclamation infrastructure and measured systemwide operation. California has issued named fared passenger-service authority and publishes public-road testing exposure.

These are real stage advances. None closes its journey.

## AI assurance

ARIA 0.1 evaluated seven applications submitted by five organizations through model testing, red teaming, and field testing. The AI Metrology Center and the TEVV program expose methods and measurement resources. The proposed standard, however, remains a zero-draft outline.

The publishable conclusion is `completed pilot plus operating method resources`. It is not `certified systems`, `institutional adoption`, or `validated production performance`.

## Manufacturing workforce

ASML's Phoenix technical academy is operating, and the University of Arizona nano-fabrication expansion is open with installed tools and teaching capability. Arizona also reports more than 2,000 Drive48 graduates and says many work at Lucid.

That is stronger than an announced partnership. It still does not provide completion denominators, placement rates, retention, wage outcomes, vacancies, or training-to-productivity evidence. A separate Maricopa semiconductor accelerator remains a 2027 plan.

## Industrial water

Chandler reports an operating reclaimed-water network treating roughly 11 billion gallons annually. Its Ocotillo facility is completed and rated for 18 million gallons per day. Intel separately reports Arizona conservation and restoration metrics.

These records show what an operating receiving system can look like. They do not establish customer-level allocation, spare capacity, a complete facility water balance, or the Phoenix TSMC reuse milestones. The TSMC project-specific hold remains in force.

## Autonomous service

CPUC authorized Waymo to begin fared driverless passenger service in specified Los Angeles and San Francisco Peninsula areas effective March 1, 2024. California DMV separately reports 4.5 million autonomous testing miles for the latest reviewed period.

The first record closes a named legal service gate. The second supplies measured testing exposure. Neither proves trip volume, comparative safety, accessibility, service quality, cost, or scale. NHTSA's Zoox exemption remains demonstration-only.

## Publication decision

Eight signals publish because they establish a completed evaluation, operating asset, delivered aggregate output, measured system record, or effective service authority with explicit limits. Four remain In Review because they are a zero-draft outline, a planned training facility, a proposed capital budget, or a demonstration exemption.

That split is the point of the phase: content expands most credibly when every new record is placed at the stage it actually proves.
