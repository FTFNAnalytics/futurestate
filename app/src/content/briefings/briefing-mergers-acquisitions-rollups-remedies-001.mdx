Approval, prohibition, abandonment, conditions, structural remedies, monitoring, post-merger results, recurrence, and public value are separate decisions.

## Evidence architecture

Phase 90 begins only after verified household economic security, distribution, resilience, and mobility. It separates firm identity and governance; market structure, competition, pricing and conduct; corporate, platform, supply-chain, financial and political power; then democratic governance, rights, remedies, public value and long-horizon market health. Every transition requires lawful authority, affected-party standing, complete denominators, independent challenge, human decision and a dated receipt.

## Firms, ownership, control, and lifecycle

Trace legal entities, establishments, enterprise groups, beneficial owners, voting and economic rights, boards, executives, workers, creditors, public obligations, related parties, restructuring, insolvency, closure, succession and restoration. A firm count is not a competitive market. Legal title alone cannot establish control, accountability, resilience or public value.

## Markets, competition, prices, quality, and labor

Define the actual product, service, labor, input, procurement, geographic, temporal, digital, data and innovation arena. A lower price is not complete consumer welfare. Market concentration is not proven abuse. Shares, markups and profits cannot substitute for evidence about entry, switching, quality, safety, privacy, accessibility, labor power, innovation, conduct and distribution.

## Corporate power, platforms, supply chains, and public support

Trace ownership networks, creditors, platforms, data, algorithms, standards, lead firms, buyers, logistics, franchises, financialization, lobbying, political spending, subsidies, tax expenditures, procurement, infrastructure, conditions and public returns. Profit is not productive investment. Public support is not public value without additionality, enforceable conditions, distribution, accountability and durable results.

## Democratic economic governance, rights, remedies, and public value

Keep participation, public reason, enforcement, merger decisions, behavioral and structural remedies, restitution, divestiture, interoperability, public options, worker voice, consumer rights, supplier fairness, community benefit, crisis resolution and long-horizon review separate. Merger approval is not public benefit. Corporate disclosure is not democratic accountability.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 89 predecessor, firm classification, ownership or control finding, market definition, competition or conduct finding, corporate-power conclusion, subsidy or tax decision, merger or remedy decision, public-value finding, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
