Separate minimum continuity from recovery, just reconstruction, transformation and institutional renewal.

## Governed whole-system futures chain

Phase 101 begins only after a verified Phase 100 shared-human-futures record. It separates scenario governance; cross-domain dependency and polycrisis stress testing; preparedness, continuity, recovery and transformation portfolios; then civilizational resilience, renewal and future-generations stewardship.

## Scenario governance

Trace purpose, identities, evidence, assumptions, boundaries, uncertainty, models, branching and prohibited uses. A scenario is not a forecast.

## Polycrisis and stress testing

Trace dependencies, cascades, compound shocks, exercises, observations and corrective action. A risk register is not readiness, and a foresight exercise is not a decision.

## Readiness and transformation portfolios

Trace authority, resources, options, reserves, continuity, mutual aid, recovery and transformation. Redundancy is not resilience, and continuity is not renewal.

## Civilizational resilience and future generations

Trace dignified floors, ecological foundations, knowledge, institutions, shared capacity, irreversible harm, renewal and future-generation standing. A long-term goal is not protection of future generations.

## Decision boundary

The registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 100 predecessor, scenario, stress-test, readiness, resilience, renewal or future-generations decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
