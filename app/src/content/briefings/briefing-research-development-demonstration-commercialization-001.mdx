Research claims, demonstrations, readiness, validation, scale-up, commercialization and operational adoption remain separate records.

## Evidence architecture

Phase 93 begins only after verified revenue, budget, debt, trade, external-balance, supply-resilience and macroeconomic-coordination records. It separates missions, sector strategies and production ecosystems; research, diffusion, commercialization and standards; regional clusters, suppliers, workforce, infrastructure and convergence; then diversification, decarbonization, resilience, shared prosperity and long-horizon productive transformation. Every transition requires lawful authority, complete identities, affected-party standing, distribution, alternatives, uncertainty, independent challenge, a human decision and a dated receipt.

## Missions, strategies, production ecosystems, and additionality

Trace public problems, sectors, value chains, capacities, constraints, instruments, conditions, milestones, firms, suppliers, workers, places and public returns. Subsidy volume is not additional productive capacity. Infrastructure spending is not an executable production ecosystem.

## Innovation, diffusion, standards, and commercialization

Keep research, development, demonstration, validation, readiness, standards, transfer, diffusion, scale-up, commercialization and adoption separate. Patent count is not innovation diffusion.

## Clusters, suppliers, workforce, and regional convergence

Trace place, ownership, supplier capability, skills, job quality, infrastructure, finance, anchor demand, community benefit, displacement, distribution and resilience. Firm attraction is not rooted regional development.

## Productive transformation and shared prosperity

Trace capabilities, productivity, quality, domestic value, diversification, decarbonization, circularity, resilience, worker adjustment, community outcomes, convergence and democratic legitimacy. Export complexity is not shared prosperity. Output growth is not a just productive transformation.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 92 predecessor, mission, sector, firm, subsidy, procurement, innovation, readiness, standard, adoption, cluster, supplier, workforce, convergence, productivity, transition or shared-prosperity decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
