## Why panels begin empty

Phase 71 publishes the destination before any evidence arrives. Each Phase 69 measurement specification has one longitudinal panel shell, but no shell contains a series, period, value, direction, magnitude, or trend.

The empty state is part of the evidence record. It prevents an upstream authorization, launch, acceptance statement, source check, or measurement contract from appearing as a longitudinal result.

## Thirty-two exact contracts

Each panel preserves one named entity, one Phase 68 cohort, one Phase 69 specification, one Phase 70 review docket, and one Phase 70 admission docket. Numerator, denominator, scope, unit, period, method, exception, revision, and break boundaries remain attached to the panel.

No panel may borrow a value from another entity, facility, geography, service, lifecycle stage, reporting period, method version, or receiving authority.

## What an admitted series may populate

A real admitted series may populate only its declared series identifier, accepted observation identifiers, series-point identifiers, period axis, source-declared values, revisions, breaks, and uncertainty notes. The admission receipt and full observation lineage must remain inspectable.

Phase 71 does not choose a universal minimum number of periods. Sufficiency depends on the measure, cadence, question, missingness, breaks, and uncertainty, and requires a recorded human decision.

## Breaks, revisions, and missing periods

Missing periods remain missing. Revisions append to lineage. A method, unit, denominator, identity, scope, authority, attribution, or lifecycle change may segment the panel or stop continuity. No interpolation, normalization, reconciliation, or continuity bridge is automatic.

Adverse observations are part of the panel contract. A favorable direction cannot hide outages, failures, exclusions, rejected units, corrections, or restatements.

## Trend boundary

An admitted series is not automatically a trend. Direction and magnitude require an explicit calculation contract; uncertainty and sensitivity must travel with the result. A trend is still not proof of attribution, counterfactual difference, causation, or policy effect.

## Publication boundary

The [Longitudinal Panel Registry](/evidence/outcomes/) and [public export](/data/longitudinal-panels-outcome-claims.json) expose empty shells and control fields. They contain no real series, points, values, trend labels, outcome claims, comparisons, scores, rankings, or decision receipts.

## Phase 72 evidence packet and design control

[Outcome Evidence Packet Desk 001](/briefings/outcome-evidence-packet-desk-001/) and the [Counterfactual Design Desk](/briefings/counterfactual-design-desk-001/) add thirty-two empty claim packets, eight empty alternative-explanation registers, and eight inactive causal-design dockets. They create zero eligible panels, proposed claims, assessments, registered designs, inspected results, receipts, scores, rankings, or stage changes.

## Phase 73 registered execution and adjudication control

[Registered Analysis Execution Desk 001](/briefings/registered-analysis-execution-desk-001/) and the [Result Adjudication Desk](/briefings/result-adjudication-desk-001/) add thirty-two inactive execution dockets, eight empty deviation registers, eight inactive adjudication dockets, and eight empty correction-withdrawal registers. They create zero registered-design receipts, executions, deviations, unblinded results, replications, claims, corrections, withdrawals, scores, rankings, receipts, or stage changes.
