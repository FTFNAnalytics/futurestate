## Current project stage

Phoenix records Fab 1 in volume production, Fab 2 construction complete, and Fab 3 at a structural milestone. A separate Phoenix agreement defines an interim wastewater path. These records establish real movement, but they do not place every facility at the same operating stage.

## Decision boundary

Utility territory is not accepted capacity. An agreement is not a completed asset. Construction completion is not equipment qualification, and a bounded production statement is not a compatible recurring output series. The file advances only when a named downstream artifact reaches the facility, service, qualification, or acceptance stage it claims.

## Next conversion record

The September 22 water gate seeks accepted infrastructure or measured operation. Separate project records should capture energization, metered demand, inspections, occupancy, tool installation, qualified lots, customer acceptance, and repeat output without collapsing them into one readiness claim.
