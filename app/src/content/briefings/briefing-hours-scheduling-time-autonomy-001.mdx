Recorded hours do not reveal whether work supplies adequate income, safe rest, predictable schedules, leave, care compatibility, recovery, and worker control over time.

## Evidence architecture

The Phase 88 chain begins only after verified human development, public knowledge, cultural capability, and community-learning recovery. It separates actual job access and fair hiring; job quality, compensation, hours, safety, dignity, accessibility and continuity; worker voice, organizing, bargaining, co-determination and shared ownership; then household livelihood security, displacement protection, regional transition and long-horizon economic agency. Every transition needs lawful authority, worker standing, complete denominators, accessible participation, distribution review, independent challenge, human decision and a dated receipt.

## Job access, matching, and hiring

Trace the actual employer, funded vacancy, location, start date, schedule, pay range, terms, accessible application, matching, screening, interview, selection, rejection, accommodation, appeal, offer, acceptance, start and retention. A job posting is not an available job. Posting or application volume cannot establish labor shortage, hiring access, nondiscrimination or a durable work pathway.

## Job quality, compensation, safety, and dignity

Separate employment status from wages, hours, benefits, safety, dignity, stability, voice, accessibility, progression and continuity. Employment is not decent work. A wage rate is not household livelihood security because actual hours, schedules, benefits, deductions, costs, debt, care, dependents, volatility and shocks remain separate evidence.

## Worker voice, bargaining power, and economic democracy

Preserve freedom of association, independent organization, recognition, communication, information, bargaining scope, agreement coverage, implementation, grievance, arbitration, enforcement, nonretaliation, strike rights, co-determination, cooperatives and employee ownership. Worker consultation is not bargaining power, and an ownership share is not economic democracy without durable worker rights and governance.

## Livelihood security, displacement, and just transition

Track income, hours, benefits, costs, debt, savings, social insurance, layoffs, closures, automation, climate transition, retraining, placement, relocation, reemployment, job quality, community continuity, diversification and future-worker evidence. Training completion is not durable career mobility. Reemployment is not a just transition unless work and livelihood quality, power, place, rights and long-term security are restored or improved.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 87 predecessor, actual-job decision, hiring finding, job-quality or compensation finding, health or safety conclusion, organizing or bargaining finding, ownership or economic-democracy conclusion, livelihood-security finding, displacement or just-transition decision, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
