## The editorial series

Outcomes Watch is the reader-facing layer above the dated evidence queue. It answers four questions for every monitored result: what artifact was checked, what the artifact supports, what it does not support, and when the next decision is due.

The series uses four receipts. A **Change Note** records evidence that changes a reviewed record. A **Watch Note** changes the expected artifact or watch posture. A **Correction** preserves and replaces an inaccurate claim. A **No Material Change** receipt records a completed check that did not satisfy the exact gate.

## Current state

Nine of the ten inherited outcome signals remain In Review. DARPA's official result now supports a Published measured competition result and prize decision, while remaining upstream of certification, production, service, recurring operation, and operating outcomes. The Shuttle Landing Facility licence signal also remains In Review after its separate August 15 bounded source check.

The remaining gates cover Amtrak program closeout and named-asset reliability; Louisiana broadband adoption; a completed Montana project quarter; a complete Hanford material balance; and NNSA baseline, accepted-capacity, and recurring-qualified-output evidence.

## Reader boundary

The absence of a qualifying artifact is not evidence of failure. Conversely, an adjacent announcement, plan, availability statement, template, event, or capacity objective cannot substitute for the exact measured record. Each receipt reports only the reviewed source surface, date, decision, publication effect, and next gate.

## Cadence

The public queue is the schedule of record. Material evidence should update the affected signal, briefing, pathway, map, and local dossier together. A completed no-change check should still remain visible so readers can distinguish editorial inactivity from a reviewed decision.
