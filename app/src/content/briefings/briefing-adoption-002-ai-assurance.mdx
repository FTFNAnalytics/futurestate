## Conversion stack

The federal AI record now shows strategic direction, inventories, purchasing mechanisms, risk-framework development, metrology, and evaluation pilots. These records make adoption inspectable, but they do not demonstrate that a deployed system is effective, secure, fair, reliable, or worth its operating cost.

A defensible adoption trail needs a named system and purpose, accountable users, data and model scope, evaluation criteria, authorization, deployment state, monitoring, incident and override records, independent review, and stable mission outcomes. Inventory growth by itself measures reporting volume, not assured performance.

## Boundary

A framework is not an implemented control. A pilot is not continuous evaluation. Catalog availability is not procurement or use. Use is not benefit. A reported use case is not a comparable outcome series. The dossier keeps those stages separate while identifying the exact records that can advance them.
