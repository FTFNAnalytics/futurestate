Money forms require explicit issuers, claims, reserves, redemption, privacy, access, distribution and legal authority.

## Evidence architecture

Phase 91 begins only after verified democratic economic governance, rights, remedies, public value, and long-horizon market health. It separates money, payments, banking access and settlement; credit, underwriting, affordability, servicing and productive allocation; capital markets, institutional investment, insurance and risk transfer; then monetary policy, systemic risk, resolution, public guarantees and democratic financial governance. Every transition requires lawful authority, affected-party standing, complete denominators, alternative explanations, independent challenge, human decision and a dated receipt.

## Money, payments, banking access, and settlement

Trace monetary forms, issuers, legal claims, reserves, accounts, deposits, wallets, cash, access, fees, identity, privacy, fraud, clearing, custody, settlement, continuity and redress. Account access is not financial inclusion. A payment count cannot establish affordability, safety, accessibility, privacy or resilience.

## Credit, underwriting, affordability, servicing, and allocation

Trace borrowers, creditors, brokers, servicers, income, cash flow, collateral, guarantees, prices, terms, models, adverse actions, servicing, collections, insolvency and fresh-start rights. Credit availability is not productive allocation. Approval cannot establish affordability, fairness, additionality or durable security.

## Capital markets, institutional investment, insurance, and risk transfer

Trace issuance, ownership, valuation, liquidity, leverage, stewardship, pensions, beneficiaries, insurance, claims, reinsurance, derivatives, public support, distribution and residual risk. Asset-price growth is not durable wealth. Capitalization is not resilience.

## Monetary systems, financial stability, resolution, and democratic finance

Keep mandates, instruments, transmission, inflation, employment, housing, systemic importance, stress, prudential tools, guarantees, recovery, resolution, public control, fiscal interaction, distribution, emergency powers and normalization separate. Central-bank intervention is not distributional legitimacy. Financial innovation is not financial stability.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 90 predecessor, monetary classification, banking-access finding, payment decision, credit decision, investment or insurance decision, monetary-policy action, systemic designation, guarantee, resolution, loss allocation, public-value finding, review, receipt, score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
