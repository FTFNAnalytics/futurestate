Govern delivery models, competition, packaging, contractor capacity, risk transfer and public return.

## Governed territorial-delivery chain

Phase 95 begins only after a verified Phase 94 physical-economy record. It separates spatial planning, land assembly, rights of way and site readiness; project definition, engineering, estimates, permits and procurement; construction, trades, materials, safety and inspection; then commissioning, accessibility, handover, operations, maintenance, adaptation, reconstruction, community recovery and place value. Each transition requires authority, exact identities, baselines, dependencies, distribution, alternatives, stress tests, independent review, a human decision and a dated receipt.

## Territorial readiness

Trace plan hierarchy, parcels, title, tenure, easements, site control, utilities, environmental review, Indigenous rights, consent, anti-displacement and community benefit. A capital plan is not an executable project.

## Project definition and procurement

Trace purpose, scope, design maturity, engineering, estimates, schedules, permits, delivery models, competition, contracts and market capacity. A permit is not a construction start.

## Construction delivery

Trace mobilization, contractors, trades, materials, equipment, logistics, progress, costs, changes, safety, quality, inspection, accessibility and as-built records. Construction spending is not a commissioned asset.

## Commissioned service and territorial value

Trace tests, deficiencies, occupancy, accessibility, handover, service, operations, maintenance, adaptation, public space, Indigenous stewardship, reconstruction, return and community recovery. Substantial completion is not safe accessible service. Floor area is not place value. Reconstruction output is not community recovery.

## Decision boundary

The registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 94 predecessor, territorial, project, procurement, construction, commissioning, service, stewardship, reconstruction, recovery or place-value decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
