## Why a result is not a claim

An analysis output is a technical artifact. It becomes evidence only after reviewers confirm that it matches the registered question, survived the execution contract, includes null and adverse outputs, and remains interpretable under its deviations, assumptions, uncertainty, and replication record.

All eight dockets remain **Inactive - No Blinded Result**. No blind break, effect, interval, robustness result, falsification test, deviation, replication, proposed sentence, reviewer, decision, or receipt exists.

## Fourteen adjudication gates

The desk reviews execution authorization, blind-break timing, the primary estimand, uncertainty, robustness, falsification, adverse and null results, subgroups and multiplicity, deviations, independent replication, alternative explanations, claim strength, correction readiness, and dual-control publication.

A structurally complete result becomes eligible for human adjudication only. No gate automatically upgrades a descriptive result to attribution or causation.

## Robustness, falsification, and adverse results

All predeclared sensitivity specifications and failure tests remain visible, including reversals. Placebos, negative controls, pre-trends, balance, discontinuity, manipulation, nulls, harms, warnings, exclusions, and disconfirming results cannot be removed because they weaken the preferred story.

## Deviations and replication

The result binds its complete deviation register: what changed, why, when it was discovered, whether reviewers authorized an amendment, and how the estimand changed. Post-result choices carry a different risk than pre-execution amendments.

Independent replication records agreements, disagreements, environmental differences, and unresolved failures. One successful rerun by the same operator is not independent replication.

## Claim strength and publication

Reviewers return to the seven Phase 72 claim classes after seeing the entire record. They may hold, reject, narrow, or downgrade the sentence. Automatic claim upgrades, causal publication, scores, and rankings remain prohibited.

## Correction and withdrawal

Every named file has an empty correction-withdrawal register covering source, code, environment, method, interpretation, attribution, disclosure, and withdrawal events. Corrections are append-only and reader-visible; no silent correction or automatic withdrawal is allowed.

## Publication boundary

The [Analysis Execution And Result Registry](/evidence/analysis/) exposes research controls, not findings. No Phase 73 page establishes a result, effect, attribution, causal claim, comparison, score, rank, correction, withdrawal, or operating outcome.

## Phase 74 evidence synthesis and decision translation

[Evidence Synthesis Desk 001](/briefings/evidence-synthesis-desk-001/) and the [Challenge And Decision Translation Desk](/briefings/challenge-decision-translation-desk-001/) add thirty-two inactive synthesis-input dockets and eight each of synthesis-contradiction, external-challenge, and decision-translation/reevaluation controls. They create zero adjudicated-result inputs, syntheses, contradictions, evidence grades, challenge decisions, recommendations, authorizations, reevaluations, corrections, withdrawals, scores, rankings, receipts, or stage changes.

## Phase 75 accountability, implementation, and impact audit

The [Decision Accountability Desk](/briefings/decision-accountability-desk-001/), [Implementation Commitment Ledger](/briefings/implementation-commitment-ledger-001/), [Realized Impact Audit](/briefings/realized-impact-audit-001/), and [Reversal And Remediation Desk](/briefings/reversal-remediation-desk-001/) add eight inactive accountability dossiers, thirty-two inactive commitment-and-realization ledgers, and eight inactive audit/remediation registers. They create zero authorized decisions, owners, rationales, commitments, baselines, safeguards, outputs, benefits, harms, distributional findings, audits, reversals, remediations, receipts, scores, rankings, or stage changes.
