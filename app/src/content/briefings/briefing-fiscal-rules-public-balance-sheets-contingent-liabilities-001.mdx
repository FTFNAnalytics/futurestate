Fiscal anchors require complete assets, liabilities, guarantees, escape clauses, correction mechanisms, public reason and audit.

## Evidence architecture

Phase 92 begins only after verified money, credit, capital, insurance, monetary-policy, systemic-risk, resolution, public-guarantee, distribution and democratic-finance records. It separates public revenue and tax administration; budgets, expenditure delivery, stabilizers and public value; sovereign debt, fiscal rules and public balance sheets; then trade, external balance, supply resilience and macroeconomic coordination. Every transition requires lawful authority, complete identities, affected-party standing, distribution, alternatives, uncertainty, independent challenge, a human decision and a dated receipt.

## Public revenue, incidence, compliance, and rights

Trace bases, rates, payers, beneficiaries, incidence, filing, collection, refund, audit, avoidance, enforcement, tax expenditures, intergovernmental allocation and taxpayer rights. Revenue capacity is not distributive justice.

## Budgets, stabilizers, delivery, and public value

Keep proposals, costings, appropriations, commitments, obligations, disbursements, delivery, access, quality, outcomes and public value separate. Expenditure authorization is not delivered public value.

## Sovereign debt, fiscal rules, and public balance sheets

Trace issuers, holders, instruments, purpose, maturity, currency, service, refinancing, assets, liabilities, guarantees, stress, restructuring and future-user obligations. Deficit size is not fiscal sustainability. Sovereign borrowing is not productive public investment.

## Trade, external balance, supply resilience, and macro coordination

Trace goods, services, customs, tariffs, treaties, investment, external accounts, capital flows, exchange, supply chains, adjustment, distribution, policy transmission and cross-border spillovers. Export growth is not resilient development. Aggregate stabilization is not democratic macroeconomic legitimacy.

## Decision boundary

This registry is deliberately empty. It contains no verified Phase 91 predecessor, tax, budget, procurement, expenditure, debt, restructuring, customs, trade, treaty, external-balance, supply-resilience or macroeconomic decision; no review or receipt; and no score, rank, Phase 64 advance or operating-outcome change.
