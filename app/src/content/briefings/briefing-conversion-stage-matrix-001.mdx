## One matrix, eight different systems

Phase 64 asks the same questions of TSMC Arizona, Toronto application `24 254930`, Northern Virginia large-load delivery, the Space Coast authority stack, Nevada lithium, GSA post-quantum procurement, NIST ARIA assurance, and Waymo California service. The columns are questions, not a claim that these files share one legal, technical, or commercial sequence.

## What the cells say

Across sixty-four cells, sixteen contain direct evidence, eight remain partial or held, and forty are not established. Evidence is concentrated in context, authority, agreement, finance, and bounded evaluation stages. Acceptance, compatible recurring operation, and stable outcomes remain much thinner.

Every comparable-outcome cell is `Not Established`. That does not mean no outcome exists anywhere. It means the reviewed event set does not establish a repeated named-file series with stable identity, period, definition, method, denominator, and acceptance.

## How to use the matrix

Read a row to see which exact question a new artifact could answer. Read a column to compare evidence requirements, not project quality. A cell never advances automatically, and evidence cannot transfer into a later stage or another entity.

The [conversion-stage-matrix export](/data/conversion-stage-matrix.json) exposes all sixty-four bounded cells. The companion [dependency map](/atlas/dependency-maps/conversion-stage-is-not-outcome/) makes the no-transfer boundary visible.
