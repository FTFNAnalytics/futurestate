## Interpretation boundary

The ARIA 0.1 pilot is direct evidence of one multi-level evaluation. It does not establish a receiving institution's authorization, continuous monitoring, incident response, correction, repeated independent review, or mission outcome.

## Exact next artifact

A named system evaluation connected to authorization, deployment monitoring, incidents, corrective action, repeat review, and a stable mission or service measure.

The Phase 63 gate remains source-triggered. Do not treat an evaluation pilot, framework, inventory, or purchasing channel as assured operation or measured benefit.
