## Dissent survives the meeting

A process that records only the adopted position destroys information about uncertainty, alternative values, excluded evidence, distributional conflict, rights claims, implementation warnings, and future failure modes. Phase 77 preserves dissent with its author or constituency, evidence, rationale, proposed alternative, requested remedy, response, and unresolved status.

Preservation does not mean every dissenting position is accepted. It means the public can later see what was contested and whether events, corrections, or outcomes support reopening the issue.

## Name the grounds

An appeal should identify why review is warranted. Phase 77 defines grounds covering standing exclusion, inadequate notice or accessibility, record omission, evidence failure, inadequate reasons, rights or consent breach, conflict of interest, procedural departure, disproportionate burden, and material new evidence or changed conditions.

The register records the relevant decision version, appellant standing, filing date, requested relief, authority, timeliness, evidence, response, reviewer, disposition, and further route. An invalid or out-of-scope appeal remains visible with reasons; it does not disappear from the history.

## Separate the procedural tools

An appeal challenges a decision through an authorized review path. Reconsideration asks the original or another competent decision maker to reopen it. A stay pauses effect. A correction repairs the record. A remand returns an issue for further process. A remedy addresses a proven violation or harm. A reversal changes the decision. An adaptive review responds to a scheduled or triggered change.

These actions can interact but are not synonyms. Phase 77 prohibits a successful procedural challenge from being silently recorded as a substantive policy reversal, and it prohibits a corrected document from being presented as completed remedy.

## Independence matters most after conflict

The body that designed a process may be poorly positioned to judge allegations about its own exclusion, bias, record integrity, or reasons. The appeal register identifies reviewer authority, independence, conflicts, information access, standard of review, and ability to order relief.

Where independent review is unavailable, that limitation remains public. A second signature does not create independence when both reviewers share the same unresolved conflict or institutional incentive.

## Make relief proportionate and traceable

A finding should connect to a remedy within authority: add a constituency, extend notice, provide access, reopen comments, correct evidence, commission independent review, add a condition, stay implementation, compensate harm, restore an asset, amend the mandate, or initiate retirement. The response states what the remedy addresses and what it leaves unresolved.

Implementation of relief is tracked separately. An ordered hearing is not a completed hearing, and a promised correction is not a corrected public record.

## Keep the mandate adaptable

Appeal records feed Phase 77 adaptive triggers. A material challenge can reopen standing, notice, evidence, reasons, conditions, or the entire mandate. Reopening still requires an authorized decision and renewed participation appropriate to the change.

No Phase 77 dissent, appeal, stay, reconsideration, remedy, reviewer decision, or receipt currently exists. The framework preserves a future path without inventing conflict or resolution.
