## What an event means

Phase 61 described the current state of eight named project and adoption files. Phase 62 adds history. Each event preserves a stable file identity, exact date basis, evidence artifact, source and signal IDs, prior and current stage, materiality, boundary, and next gate.

An event is deliberately smaller than a project conclusion. Toronto Council adoption advances one regulatory stage. Golden-Mars conditional authority advances one infrastructure stage. ARIA completes a pilot. Waymo receives service authority. None of those events establishes the later delivery or outcome stages that remain open.

## Backfill is not a receipt

The first seventeen events are reconstructed from evidence already reviewed and assigned to the Phase 61 files. Their date basis is explicit: an official decision or publication date when the reviewed record supplies one, otherwise the FTFN capture date. Backfill does not manufacture a historical editorial receipt.

## Future append contract

A future Phase 60 decision may append to a named ledger only after a real source check, dated receipt, and complete propagation decision. The cycle target must share the same entity or receiving-system stage. DARPA evidence, for example, cannot advance the Waymo file merely because both sit inside a broader autonomy dossier.

Corrections append a new correction event rather than rewriting the identity, date basis, or prior-stage account of a Published event.
