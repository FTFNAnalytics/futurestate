## What changed

The preliminary practice-guide volume documents protocol-specific test profiles and the availability of collaborator implementations. Its QUIC section records that only one implementation was available during initial testing, preventing completed interoperability testing for that protocol.

## Evidence stage

This supports `preliminary controlled laboratory testing and a documented interoperability limit`. It does not support `final guidance`, `production interoperability`, `vendor-wide support`, `institutional procurement`, or `completed migration`.

## What to watch next

Track later NCCoE drafts, final practice guidance, protocol standards, independent implementations, product support, validation, procurement, institutional inventories, migration milestones, and operating evidence.
