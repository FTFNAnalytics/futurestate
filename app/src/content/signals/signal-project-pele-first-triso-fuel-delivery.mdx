## What Changed

INL reported that the first batch of TRISO fuel produced for Project Pele had been delivered to the Transient Reactor Test Facility. The release identifies the Strategic Capabilities Office, Army, BWXT, DOE, and INL as collaborators.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `fuel produced + delivered to demonstration site`. It does not support `reactor complete`, `authorized`, `fuel loaded`, `first criticality`, `power generated`, or `demonstration successful`.

## Why It Matters

Physical material at the receiving facility narrows the distance between program intent and operation. It also exposes the remaining stack: assembly, authorization, handling, loading, startup, test execution, performance, and transition.

## What To Watch Next

Follow official project records for reactor assembly, licensing or authorization, fuel loading, startup testing, first criticality, first power, transport tests, safety results, operating performance, cost, and any follow-on procurement or commercial program.
