## What Changed

Phoenix Agenda Item 73 authorizes a development agreement with TSMC Arizona Corporation for wastewater conveyance improvements supporting up to three fabs. The work includes improvements to two existing lift stations, a new force main, and new gravity sewer lines, with TSMC responsible for the project costs.

After completion of the improvements, the City agrees to accept an increase in wastewater flow from approximately 333,000 to 755,000 gallons per hour for the contract term. Before additional flows beyond the first three fabs, TSMC must complete its first industrial reclaimed water plant. Later plants are tied to every third fab building receiving a certificate of occupancy and manufacturing chips.

## Why It Matters

This converts a generic industrial-water question into a named service and infrastructure record. It shows that wastewater capacity is being sequenced through customer-funded conveyance improvements, reclaimed-water infrastructure, occupancy milestones, and a future municipal reclamation plant.

## Dependency Stack

- The development agreement must be executed and remain in force
- Lift-station, force-main, and gravity-sewer improvements must be built and accepted
- TSMC funds the specified improvements and additional capacity work
- The initial industrial reclaimed water plant must be operational by June 30, 2028
- Later reclaimed-water plants follow fab occupancy and manufacturing milestones
- The interim arrangement eventually depends on the North Gateway Wastewater Reclamation Plant

## Evidence Limits

The agreement concerns wastewater conveyance and reclaimed-water infrastructure. It does not provide a complete water balance for TSMC's campus, quantify potable or raw-water withdrawals, disclose process consumption, or establish drought resilience.

Authorization is not proof that the infrastructure or reclaimed-water plants are complete or operating. The accepted-flow figure is conditional on completion of the specified improvements.

## What To Watch Next

Track the executed agreement, project permits, construction and acceptance of the conveyance work, operation of the first industrial reclaimed water plant by June 30, 2028, fab certificates of occupancy, and the North Gateway Wastewater Reclamation Plant.
