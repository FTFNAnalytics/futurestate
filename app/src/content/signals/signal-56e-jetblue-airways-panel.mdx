## Named-entity panel

The annual operating-carrier rail remains separate from the later monthly on-time, baggage, wheelchair, complaint, and commitment denominators.

## Observations

1. 2023: 5,763 of 274,852 scheduled operations cancelled, 2.10 percent
2. 2024: 3,735 of 240,282 cancelled, 1.55 percent
3. 2025: 3,825 of 231,413 cancelled, 1.65 percent

## Reporting breaks

- Scheduled operations declined across the three annual observations.
- May on-time, baggage, wheelchair, complaint, and commitment records use different denominators.

## What remains unresolved

- Later full-year cancellation and schedule denominator
- Route, airport, weather, cause, maintenance, and crew controls
- Complaint rates and verified remedy delivery
