## Resolution

Held — one-to-many. The candidates are GAO-25-106874 Recommendation 1: Develop a reliable life-cycle cost estimate for the full electronic health record modernization program. | GAO-25-106874 Recommendation 2: Develop an integrated master schedule for the full electronic health record modernization program..

## Agency response

VA concurred in principle but limited its planned artifacts to the current Oracle contract. GAO said that scope was insufficient for either full life-cycle recommendation.

## Evidence boundary

An agency response is not implementation. GAO recommendation status is not the Phase 56F evidence state, and recommendation closure would not by itself establish operating performance or outcome.
