## Current Stage

The county published September 2026 proposed redline and clean amendment packages for the 2019 Comprehensive Plan and Zoning Ordinance, together with a proposal summary. This advances the policy record to a public proposed-policy package. It does not establish that staff made a final recommendation, that a public hearing or board vote occurred, or that the proposed text is adopted or effective.

The county's October 1 page schedules a Zoning Ordinance Committee meeting for October 2026, a Board check-in in December 2026, Planning Commission hearing and work-session steps in February and May 2027, and Board public-hearing and business-meeting steps in July 2027. The checked page does not post a Phase 2 staff report, hearing result, Board action, adopted ordinance or plan text, or effective date.

## Stage Boundary

Project initiation and draft-policy publication are complete upstream stages. Recommendation, hearing, Board action, adoption, effective rule, project application, permit, construction, service, and operation remain separate, unproven stages.

## Next Check

Recheck after the scheduled October 2026 ZOC meeting for an official agenda, staff record, recommendation, hearing action, or revised schedule.
