## What Changed

The Office of Strategic Capital announced execution of its first direct loan: $150 million to MP Materials for heavy rare-earth separation capability at the company's existing Mountain Pass, California processing facility.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `executed financing + named facility capability`. It does not support `facility complete`, `equipment installed`, `commissioned`, `qualified product`, or `sustained output`.

## Why It Matters

Heavy rare-earth separation is a more specific industrial dependency than a broad critical-minerals strategy. The transaction identifies where the capacity is intended to sit and which processing step the capital is meant to support.

## What To Watch Next

Follow official loan, permit, construction, equipment, commissioning, product-qualification, feedstock, offtake, volume, and operating records. Distinguish facility milestones from company targets and policy claims.
