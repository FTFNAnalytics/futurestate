## Phase 57A panel

DOT reports 23.1 percent of transit revenue vehicles exceeding their useful-life benchmark in FY 2025, compared with a 22.0 percent target.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Fixed-cohort system outcome.** Dedicated revenue vehicles reported through the National Transit Database; spare and non-dedicated vehicles are excluded under the stated method.

## Evidence boundaries

- The percentage is an asset-condition proxy, not a count of riders or service failures.
- A result above target does not identify the cause or the responsible agency or operator.
- Different vehicle mixes, ages, and reporting practices can affect the system-wide share.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track the same NTD vehicle universe, useful-life method, fleet composition, replacements, reliability, availability, and service delivered.
