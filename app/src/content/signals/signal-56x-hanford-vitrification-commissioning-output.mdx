DOE reports that the Low-Activity Waste Facility had immobilized more than 100,000 gallons of tank waste in glass since hot commissioning began in October 2025. The agency says commissioning will continue while the team develops production consistency and sustained operations.

**Operating output.** Cumulative gallons of Hanford tank waste vitrified during hot commissioning through May 26, 2026.
