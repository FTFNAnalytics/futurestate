## Evidence Stage

NIST reports third-party survey estimates from 8,771 MEP clients: $15 billion in new or retained sales, $5 billion in investment, $2.6 billion in savings, and more than 108,300 jobs created or retained.

## Boundary

Publish with client-attribution and survey-method labels. Do not treat the figures as audited causal effects.

## What To Watch Next

Track survey response, sector and region splits, productivity measures, repeated-client effects, and independent validation.
