## Repeat-outcome and alternative-explanation test

The bounded record can publish as a denominator warning, but it does not test persistence of the reported line-output change.

## Tested alternatives

- Demand and product mix
- Quality-control and supply conditions
- Equipment and layout
- Lean intervention

## Later observations

1. FDA's portal showed 193 manufacturer-attributed device reports for 2025 at capture.
2. The company's ISO 14001 page supplies control objectives but no quantitative surveillance or environmental outcome.

## Compatibility and attribution

**Compatibility:** Same manufacturer; product-event counts and environmental management controls do not identify the MEP production line or units distributed.

**Attribution:** FDA exposes the event-report dataset; environmental-control claims remain company-attributed.

**Validation status:** The event count is independently accessible; defect rate, line identity, and causal interpretation are not validated.

## What remains unresolved

Add units distributed, product identity, same-line output, labor, yield, scrap, downtime, delivery, demand, waste, emissions, and surveillance findings.

## Publication boundary

The bounded observation may publish; causation, ranking, composite scoring, readiness scoring, and denominator mixing remain outside the claim.
