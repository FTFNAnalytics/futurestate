## What Changed

FAA's AAM page gives FTFN a primary source for treating air taxis as a system-integration problem. The source supports the idea that aircraft capability is only one layer of AAM deployment.

## Why It Matters

Aircraft are only one layer. The deployment path also depends on certification, airspace, vertiports, insurance, weather, and public trust.

## Dependency Stack

- Powered-lift regulation
- Aircraft certification
- National Airspace System integration
- Ground infrastructure and local permitting
- Insurance and public acceptance

## What To Watch Next

Pair FAA guidance with city, airport, emergency response, and operator sources before treating any market as deployment-ready.
