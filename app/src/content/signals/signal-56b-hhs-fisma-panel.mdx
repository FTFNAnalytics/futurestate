## Entity-level read

HHS OIG rated the department information-security program Not Effective in FY 2022, FY 2023, and FY 2024. The repeated rating reflects a department-level threshold and does not erase variation among operating divisions.

## Measurement contract

**Entity ID:** `agency-hhs`

**Indicator:** Inspector-general FISMA program effectiveness

**Unit:** Annual effectiveness determination

**Denominator:** HHS department information-security program under the stated fiscal-year IG metrics

**Period:** Fiscal years 2022-2024

**Geography:** United States federal health department

**Method:** Annual independent FISMA audit using core and applicable supplemental IG metrics

**Attribution:** HHS Office of Inspector General and its independent auditor

## Comparison boundary

Publish the repeated department rating only. Do not equate the headline result with identical component risk, incidents, health-service disruption, or patient harm.

## What to watch next

Track operating-division maturity, tested coverage, inventory accuracy, corrective-action closure, incident exposure, service disruption, and later metric changes.
