## Phase 56Z panel

DOE reports about 41,000 gallons transferred from single-shell Tank A-102 to a double-shell tank, bringing cumulative retrieved waste across 23 tanks to about 3.4 million gallons.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted cleanup operation.** Completed Hanford single-shell tank retrievals through Tank A-102 and DOE's cumulative retrieved-gallon total as of May 1, 2026.

## Evidence boundaries

- Tank-specific and cumulative gallons use approximate values.
- Transfer to another tank is not treatment, permanent disposal, or operational closure.
- The 24th tank was in progress and is excluded from the completed cohort.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
