## What Changed

FTFN now treats Federal Register and Regulations.gov access as a cross-cutting regulatory watch rail. Federal Register API results can surface notices, proposed rules, rules, agencies, publication dates, and document metadata. Regulations.gov adds the docket and comment-document layer that often explains how a rulemaking is being contested, supported, limited, or implemented.

## Why It Matters

Frontier-domain signals rarely move only through press releases. Energy, mobility, aviation, cybersecurity, water, agriculture, space, and standards work often pass through official notices, proposed rules, dockets, supporting documents, and public comments before local systems respond.

## Dependency Stack

- Agency rulemaking authority
- Federal Register publication metadata
- Regulations.gov docket and document records
- Official legal editions and agency files
- Public comment and supporting materials
- State, local, utility, and sector implementation

## Evidence Limits

This signal identifies a watch rail, not a specific regulatory outcome. FederalRegister.gov should be treated as an access and discovery surface, while legal interpretation still requires the official edition, agency context, and downstream implementation evidence. Regulations.gov comments and attachments require careful handling because public comments are evidence of participation, not verified factual claims.

## What To Watch Next

Select a specific FAA, NHTSA, DOE, FERC, CISA, BIS, NOAA, USDA, or NASA document and pair the Federal Register notice with the related docket record before promoting this into a publication candidate.
