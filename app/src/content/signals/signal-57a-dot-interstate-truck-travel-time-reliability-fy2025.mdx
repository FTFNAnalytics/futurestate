## Phase 57A panel

DOT reports a 1.36 Interstate Truck Travel Time Reliability index in FY 2025, better than the 1.40 target; lower values indicate greater reliability.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Fixed-cohort system outcome.** Interstate travel-time segments using the 95th-to-50th percentile truck travel-time ratio from National Performance Management Research Data Set probe observations, including roughly 700,000 freight vehicles.

## Evidence boundaries

- Probe coverage and sample composition can affect the index.
- A national ratio can mask route, time-of-day, commodity, and disruption variation.
- The movement does not establish which infrastructure or operating action caused the result.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
