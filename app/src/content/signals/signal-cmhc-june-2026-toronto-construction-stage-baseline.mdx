## What Changed

CMHC's July 16, 2026 release puts several Toronto CMA construction stages in one current record:

- approved units awaiting a housing start increased from 16,195 in May to 16,992 in June,
- units under construction increased from 91,382 to 92,217,
- monthly completions fell from 2,224 to 1,329,
- June starts increased from 1,701 in June 2025 to 2,132 in June 2026,
- and January-to-June starts increased from 12,575 in 2025 to 13,225 in 2026.

## Evidence Stage

This supports `current metropolitan construction-stage baseline`.

It does not support `same-project permit-to-start conversion`, `same-project start-to-completion conversion`, `municipal processing time`, `application 24 254930 permit`, `project financing`, `project start`, `project completion`, or `occupancy`.

## Why It Matters

The release improves the Ontario dossier by separating approved-but-not-started units, active construction, completions, and starts. Those measures reveal different stages of the system, but they are not a single cohort and should not be turned into a conversion ratio without project-level linkage.
