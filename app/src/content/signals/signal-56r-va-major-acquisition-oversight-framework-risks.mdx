## Implementation state

Partially addressed. The public notice establishes the framework, but GAO said VA still needed to finalize and implement its management structure and address the identified risks.

## Artifact and milestone

**Artifact:** VA Notice 24-08 establishing the Acquisition Lifecycle Framework. **Availability:** Public official artifact located; notice later expired without proving full implementation. **Milestone:** None stated (No current completion date).

## Official review and remaining gap

GAO classifies the recommendation Open – Partially Addressed. Remaining gap: A finalized and operating governance structure that addresses cost data, workforce, process alignment, and compliance.

## Evidence boundary

A promise is a monitor, not evidence. An artifact is not implementation until its scope satisfies the recommendation and the official record supports that conclusion. Recommendation status, Phase 56F entity evidence state, closure, and operating outcome remain separate.
