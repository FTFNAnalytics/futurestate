## Evidence Stage

GAO found 126 active IRS AI use cases in June 2025, but 77 were categorized as in development and more than one-quarter lacked intended-benefit information.

## Boundary

Publish as an audited inventory-quality and maturity finding, not as a judgment on the effectiveness of IRS AI systems.

## What To Watch Next

Track how many cases reach operation, disclose measured benefits, complete impact assessment, or are retired.
