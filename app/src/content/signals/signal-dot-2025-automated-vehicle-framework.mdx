## Current state

The framework establishes an administration policy direction for automated vehicles and points toward regulatory and program changes.

## Evidence boundary

It does not itself finalize each action, certify a vehicle, validate safety, authorize local service, or demonstrate commercial scale.

## Next records

Track final rules, exemptions, safety-standard changes, reporting requirements, recalls, investigations, state actions, local permits, and operating outcomes.
