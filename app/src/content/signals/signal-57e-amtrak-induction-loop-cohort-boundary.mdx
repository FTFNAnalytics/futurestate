## Phase 57E panel

Amtrak plans twenty additional induction-loop installations in fiscal 2026, which it says would bring the total to 118 stations, with remaining staffed stations planned for fiscal 2027.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Asset-quality reconciliation.** The stated 20-addition and 118-total planning cohort; the report does not separately state the current installed, accepted, available, or used count.

## Evidence boundaries

- The implied prior inventory is not treated as an exact reported observation.
- A planned installation cohort is not accepted service or operating availability.
- No staffed-station denominator, uptime, maintenance, use, complaint, or hearing-access outcome is disclosed.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish the named staffed-station cohort with installed, accepted, available, maintained, used, complained, and remediated status by period.
