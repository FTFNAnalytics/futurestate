## Current state

EPA states that the national inventory estimates economy-wide emissions and sinks, while GHGRP supplies detailed reported information for covered facilities and suppliers.

## Evidence boundary

Neither dataset alone proves causation, compliance, reduction, or a complete local emissions balance.

Track annual inventory releases, GHGRP revisions, reporting thresholds, facility filings, sector methods, local inventories, permits, and verified reduction projects.
