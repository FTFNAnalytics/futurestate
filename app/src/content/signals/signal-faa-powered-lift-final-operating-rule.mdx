## Current state

The FAA has a final powered-lift framework for pilot certification, training, and operations.

## Evidence boundary

The rule does not grant a type certificate, production certificate, operator certificate, route authority, vertiport approval, local acceptance, or commercial service.

## Next records

Track aircraft certificates, production approvals, qualified pilots, operator certificates, airspace procedures, vertiports, emergency plans, routes, flights, safety performance, and service.
