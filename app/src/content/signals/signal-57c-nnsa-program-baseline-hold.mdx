## Phase 57C panel

GAO reports that NNSA still lacked a lifecycle cost estimate aligned with best practices as of April 2026 and separately reported project-level baselines and preliminary estimates, leaving the enterprise recommendation open.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Recurring output and baseline hold.** The complete plutonium-modernization program across sites and supporting activities, evaluated against GAO integrated-master-schedule and lifecycle-cost best practices.

## Evidence boundaries

- Component project estimates do not sum to a validated enterprise lifecycle cost.
- A December 2026 forecast is not a completed or GAO-sufficient artifact.
- The open recommendation does not establish qualified output, accepted rate, schedule confidence, total cost, or readiness.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen only when NNSA publishes the complete integrated master schedule and lifecycle cost estimate, GAO records sufficiency, and recurring qualified output is reported under stable site-period denominators.

## Authority boundary

Planning, design, construction, turnover, service inventory, operating availability, use, adoption, test performance, accepted operation, repeat output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, project baseline, program baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or causal attribution.
