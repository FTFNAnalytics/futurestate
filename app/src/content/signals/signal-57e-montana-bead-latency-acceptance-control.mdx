## Phase 57E panel

ConnectMT requires 95 percent of measured latency observations to be at or below 100 milliseconds.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Adoption and retention acceptance control.** The compliant active-subscriber performance-test sample for each relevant speed tier and technology.

- No awardee latency distribution, exception, retest, or corrective-action record is public.
- Latency compliance alone does not establish adoption, retention, price, complaints, or service availability.
