## What Changed

Toronto's June 22, 2026 decision report moves application `24 254930 ESC 20 OZ` beyond the initial notice stage. Community Planning recommends that City Council amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law for a revised proposal of two mixed-use buildings containing 510 rental units.

The report also states that Development Engineering reviewed the submitted Functional Servicing, Stormwater Management, and Hydrogeological Report and accepted the analysis as demonstrating available servicing-infrastructure capacity to accommodate the proposal.

## Why It Matters

This is the first record in the Ontario dossier that connects a named planning application to a formal staff recommendation and a project-level servicing review. It also shows why application counts alone are weak evidence: the proposal changed from the initial 578-unit notice to a 510-unit staff-recommended form.

## Evidence Limits

The report recommends approval; it is not itself proof that City Council approved the amendments or that the required by-laws came into force.

The servicing statement records staff acceptance of applicant-submitted analysis at the planning stage. It does not replace detailed design, later permit review, construction inspection, system operation, or evidence that every condition has been satisfied.

The record does not prove financing, a building permit, a construction start, completion, occupancy, affordability, or delivery of 510 units.
