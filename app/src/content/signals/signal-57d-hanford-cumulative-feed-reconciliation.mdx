## Phase 57D panel

The cumulative feed total moves from more than 85,604 gallons through April 28, 2026, to 115,361 gallons through May 26, while the July report separately states 29,757 gallons processed during May.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Monthly material-flow series.** Two cumulative cutoffs and one reported monthly May observation for the same LAW Facility; the May value equals the difference between the stated numerical thresholds but the earlier cumulative value remains a lower bound.

## Evidence boundaries

- The earlier greater-than threshold prevents treating the arithmetic difference as an independently exact reconciliation.
- Cutoff dates are not full calendar-month endpoints.
- Cumulative feed does not disclose quality-released glass, accepted containers, disposal, downtime, compliance, or cost.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Retain cutoff dates and threshold operators in every row and reconcile feed to glass, containers, laboratory release, shipment, acceptance, disposal, rejects, rework, and residual inventory.
