## What Changed

Scarborough Community Council considered Toronto agenda item `2026.SC33.9` on July 9, 2026 and recommended that City Council amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law for the Victoria Park Avenue and Thora Avenue proposal. The official tracking page now says City Council will consider the item on July 29, 30 and 31, 2026.

The committee action follows the June 22 staff report and keeps the revised proposal at 510 rental units in two mixed-use buildings of 17 and 35 storeys.

The amended recommendation also directs the City Solicitor to withhold the amendment bills until the applicant supplies a revised pedestrian wind study with wind-tunnel work, enters into and completes the land exchange involving 6 Thora Avenue, and obtains Council approval for permanent closure of the abutting laneway and ownership of that land.

## Why It Matters

The application now has a traceable path from initial notice, to revised staff recommendation and servicing review, to a community-council recommendation and a dated Council gate. The bill-withholding direction shows why Council adoption, amendment enactment, and construction readiness are distinct conversion stages.

## Evidence Limits

A community-council recommendation and a scheduled Council date are not final City Council adoption and do not enact the required amendments.

The July 23 Building Permit portal searches for all eight addresses named in the item returned `Application Not Found`. Those address markers are negative search results, not proof that no application exists. The records do not establish a building permit, construction start, financing close, completion, occupancy, affordability outcome, or delivery date for the 510 units.

## What To Watch Next

Recheck the item immediately after the July 29-31 Council meeting. Capture the disposition, any amended recommendations, bill status and enacted by-law numbers, then track the wind, land-exchange and laneway conditions, first above-grade building permit, CMHC start, construction progress, completion, and occupancy.
