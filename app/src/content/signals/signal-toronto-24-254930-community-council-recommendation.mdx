## What Changed

Scarborough Community Council considered Toronto agenda item `2026.SC33.9` on July 9, 2026 and recommended that City Council amend the Official Plan and Zoning By-law for the Victoria Park Avenue and Thora Avenue proposal.

The committee action follows the June 22 staff report and keeps the revised proposal at 510 rental units in two mixed-use buildings of 17 and 35 storeys.

## Why It Matters

The application now has a traceable path from initial notice, to revised staff recommendation and servicing review, to a community-council recommendation. That sequence is more useful than treating the original application count as an approval or future completion.

## Dependency Stack

- Scarborough Community Council recommendation
- Toronto City Council consideration
- Official Plan and Zoning By-law enactment
- Satisfaction of land, laneway, parkland, and servicing conditions
- Building permits and financing
- Construction, completion, and occupancy

## Evidence Limits

A community-council recommendation is not final City Council adoption and does not itself enact the required amendments.

The record does not establish a building permit, construction start, financing close, completion, occupancy, affordability outcome, or delivery date for the 510 units.
