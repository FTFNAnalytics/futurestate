## What Changed

Toronto City Council adopted agenda item `2026.SC33.9` without amendments and without debate during its July 29-30, 2026 meeting. The action advances the Victoria Park Avenue and Thora Avenue proposal beyond the earlier Scarborough Community Council recommendation.

The committee action follows the June 22 staff report and keeps the revised proposal at 510 rental units in two mixed-use buildings of 17 and 35 storeys.

The adopted recommendation still directs the City Solicitor to withhold the amendment bills until the applicant supplies an accepted revised pedestrian wind study with wind-tunnel work, enters into and completes the land exchange involving 6 Thora Avenue, and obtains Council approval for permanent closure of the abutting laneway and ownership of that land.

## Why It Matters

The application now has a traceable path from initial notice, to revised staff recommendation and servicing review, to community-council recommendation and City Council adoption. The bill-withholding direction shows why Council adoption, amendment enactment, and construction readiness remain distinct conversion stages.

## Evidence Limits

City Council adoption does not itself enact the required amendments while the City Solicitor's bill-withholding direction remains active.

The July 23 Building Permit portal searches for all eight addresses named in the item returned `Application Not Found`. Those address markers are negative search results, not proof that no application exists. The records do not establish a building permit, construction start, financing close, completion, occupancy, affordability outcome, or delivery date for the 510 units.
