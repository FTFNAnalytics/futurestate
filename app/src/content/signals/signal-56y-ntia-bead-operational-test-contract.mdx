## Phase 56Y result

NTIA requires annual testing during the period of performance and before closeout, with at least 100/20 Mbps for funded broadband locations, 1 Gbps symmetric for community anchor institutions, at least 95 percent of latency tests at or below 100 milliseconds, and average outages no greater than 48 hours over 365 days excluding force majeure.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Award-to-service contract.** Operational BEAD-funded service by eligible entity, technology, speed tier, and random active-subscriber sample; sample floors are five locations at 50 or fewer subscribers, 10 percent at 51-500, and 50 at 500 or more.

## Evidence boundaries

- These are required measures, not observed results.
- Testing applies to operational service and is not construction validation.
- A passing sample does not establish universal adoption, affordability, or economic outcomes.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish eligible-entity results only when the tested cohort, technology, speed tier, sample, period, exclusions, and closeout status are disclosed.
