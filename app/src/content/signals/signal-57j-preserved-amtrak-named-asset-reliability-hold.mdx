## Evidence stage and denominator

**Measured reliability outcome hold.** Each stable station and accessibility asset by reporting period, including installed, accepted, available, failed, repaired, used, complained, resolved, and rider-outcome fields.

## Evidence boundaries

- Construction status does not establish reliability.
- Aggregate engagement and remediation statements cannot supply asset-period outcomes.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen with stable asset identifiers and compatible asset-period reliability, use, complaint, and rider-outcome tables.
