## What Changed

FTFN selected USAspending award `DEMS0000003`, described as Project Double Play, an advanced domestic battery-minerals processing facility award to Talon Nickel (USA) LLC. The record advances the evidence posture from a broad funding rail to a named recipient and federal award trail.

On the checked record, USAspending reported $114.85 million obligated, $954,321.45 outlayed, and $250.88 million in non-federal funding. The award period began on November 1, 2023, and the current end date was October 31, 2026.

Phase 55L preserves the award API response inside the implementation-evidence download, making the amounts and award metadata used for this signal independently inspectable as a captured source record.

## Why It Matters

Critical-minerals policy becomes more analytically useful when FTFN can distinguish an announced funding opportunity from an actual award, then distinguish an award from money outlayed, construction progress, commissioning, and production. This record supplies the first two layers of that chain.

## Dependency Stack

## Evidence Limits

The award does not prove that the proposed facility is permitted, financed through completion, under construction, commissioned, technically successful, supplied with feedstock, or producing battery materials. Obligations and outlays are administrative funding evidence, not physical-progress measures.

The USAspending place, recipient, and award fields also do not establish local infrastructure capacity or economic impact. Those questions require project-site, permitting, power, water, workforce, contractor, and operating records.

## What To Watch Next

Watch later USAspending transactions, DOE project updates, recipient disclosures, site and permit records, construction milestones, equipment installation, commissioning, feedstock agreements, offtake, and production evidence.
