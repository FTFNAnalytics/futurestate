## Phase 57E panel

Washington Ecology's final response records a June 25, 2026 effective date for the WTP permit modification enabling EMF concentrate to be grouted and shipped out of state for commercial disposal.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted-output closure.** The final regulatory authorization for the EMF concentrate route, kept separate from actual batches grouted, shipped, accepted, and disposed.

## Evidence boundaries

- Permit effectiveness is not proof that a concentrate batch completed the new route.
- The record does not quantify actual grouted, shipped, accepted, or disposed volume.
- A process change does not establish cost savings or environmental outcomes.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
