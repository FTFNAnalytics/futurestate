## Phase 57C panel

ConnectLA's 104-location Bossier Parish service-availability cohort remains public without a stable location list, installations, subscribers, test distribution, latency, uptime, affordability, adoption, complaints, or accepted closeout.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Adoption and activation hold.** The same 104 BEAD locations reported as serviceable, with eligible, serviceable, installed, subscribed, tested, and retained denominators kept separate.

## Evidence boundaries

- Serviceable locations are not subscribers.
- Advertised gigabit capability is not a measured test distribution.
- Adoption, affordability, reliability, complaints, and closeout cannot be inferred from tower activation.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen with a stable location list and disclosed eligible, serviceable, installed, subscribed, tested, retained, price, latency, uptime, complaint, and closeout fields.

## Authority boundary

Planning, design, construction, turnover, service inventory, operating availability, use, adoption, test performance, accepted operation, repeat output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, project baseline, program baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or causal attribution.
