## Entity dossier read

HHS's FY 2025 FISMA review extends the panel with a sixth consecutive Not Effective result. GAO's 82 open CIO recommendations identify a broad remediation workload but do not function as a severity score or explain the annual rating.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** HHS remained Not Effective across FY 2022-FY 2024.
2. **Named intervention or input:** HHS continued department and component remediation under the CIO and FISMA programs.
3. **Constraint:** GAO identified 82 open CIO recommendations, while HHS OIG found supplemental metrics at Ad Hoc maturity.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** The FY 2025 evaluation again rated the HHS information-security program Not Effective.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** HHS OIG supplies the annual rating; GAO independently inventories open recommendations.

**Independent validation:** Independent oversight confirms both conditions, but open-recommendation counts do not validate the cause of the FISMA result.

## Alternative explanations

- Federated operating-division maturity
- Annual metric weighting
- Recommendation age and severity
- Control areas outside the open GAO set

## What to watch next

Track FY 2026 FISMA results, recommendation closures, component control testing, incidents, recovery, affected services, and health-program outcomes.
