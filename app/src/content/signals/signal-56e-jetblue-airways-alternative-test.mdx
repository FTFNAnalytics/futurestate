## Alternative-explanation test

The May record shows a bounded later operating observation but does not isolate why the annual cancellation rate changed.

## Named alternatives

- Schedule reduction
- Route and airport mix
- Weather
- Maintenance or staffing
- Late inbound aircraft

## Later observations

1. DOT reported 81.0 percent on-time arrivals for JetBlue in May 2026.
2. DOT also reported distinct baggage and wheelchair or scooter denominators that cannot be merged with cancellation performance.

## Compatibility

Stable carrier identity; annual cancellation and monthly service measures remain separate.

## Validation status

Official DOT compilation; carrier reporting is not an independent causal determination.

## What remains unresolved

- Later full-year cancellation and schedule denominator
- Route, airport, weather, cause, maintenance, and crew controls
- Complaint rates and verified remedy delivery
