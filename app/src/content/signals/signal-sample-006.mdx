## What Changed

NASA's Artemis program page gives FTFN a stronger official anchor for treating lunar exploration as a mission-cadence and infrastructure-stack question. It connects crewed exploration, SLS, Orion, ground systems, lunar landers, surface systems, science objectives, and the Moon-to-Mars pathway.

## Why It Matters

Space travel is not only a launch story. Artemis depends on terrestrial manufacturing, human-rated systems, launch cadence, surface power, communications, funding, procurement, science priorities, international coordination, and safety.

This signal does not prove that a specific mission, supplier, lander, or surface system is on schedule. It marks the official program stack that future dated Artemis updates should be compared against.
