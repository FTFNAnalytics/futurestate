## What Changed

NASA reported in July 2026 that Artemis III booster segments were being secured and stacked at Kennedy Space Center. The update also describes work on the core stage and engines, Orion heat shield, and service-module acoustic testing.

## Why It Matters

The record converts a general program page into a dated integration milestone. It shows how a mission depends on manufacturing, transport, stacking, testing, ground systems, crew systems, funding, procurement, and safety before launch.

The update does not prove that Artemis III is fully integrated, launch-ready, on schedule, or successful. It also is not a lunar-landing milestone: NASA currently describes Artemis III as a 2027 low-Earth-orbit rendezvous and docking test, with the landing mission assigned to Artemis IV in 2028.
