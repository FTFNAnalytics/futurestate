## What Changed

Arizona's official water agency source gives FTFN a stronger foundation for treating chip-corridor growth as a water-governance problem, not only an industrial investment story. ADWR's assured and adequate water supply material makes the criteria layer more explicit by pointing to long-term availability, legal rights, water quality, delivery capability, and related requirements.

## Why It Matters

Industrial growth in water-constrained regions depends on supply, demand, treatment, reuse, permits, provider obligations, drought conditions, and public legitimacy.

## Dependency Stack

- State water governance
- Assured or adequate water supply criteria
- Provider-level water records
- Industrial water demand estimates
- Reuse and treatment infrastructure
- Permitting and local planning
- Public trust

## Evidence Limits

This signal does not quantify facility-level water use, provider obligations, discharge requirements, or reuse capacity. The assured and adequate supply source explains review criteria, but FTFN still needs provider, facility, permit, and reuse records before making project-level conclusions.

## What To Watch Next

Watch water provider records, facility disclosures, reuse plans, permitting documents, designated provider materials, and community responses around industrial water demand.
