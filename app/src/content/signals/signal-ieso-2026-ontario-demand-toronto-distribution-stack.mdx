## What Changed

IESO's 2026 Annual Planning Outlook provides a new provincial demand and adequacy baseline. Its reference outlook forecasts Ontario electricity demand growing 65% over the long term and says data centres could account for 8.6% of provincial demand by 2050.

At the local layer, the Ontario Energy Board's `EB-2023-0195` record preserves Toronto Hydro's 2025-2029 distribution-rate application, distribution-system-plan evidence, and Board decisions.

## Why It Matters

These records separate three layers that are often collapsed:

- Provincial demand, supply, and adequacy
- Toronto distribution planning, investment, and rates
- A named project's connection capacity, timing, and conditions

The first two are now documented. The third remains open.

## Power Delivery Stack

- Provincial demand scenarios
- Resource procurement and generation
- Bulk transmission and regional planning
- Toronto distribution investment
- Local station, feeder, and transformer capacity
- Customer study, agreement, construction, and energization

## Evidence Limits

The 65% and 8.6% figures are long-term scenario results, not guaranteed demand. They do not identify application 24 254930 or establish its load.

The Toronto Hydro proceeding establishes a regulated planning and rate record. It does not prove spare capacity, connection timing, cost, or a service commitment at a named address. Applicant evidence also remains interested-party material unless reflected in a Board decision.

## What To Watch Next

Watch later IESO forecast revisions, procurements, transmission projects, Toronto Hydro plan implementation, Board rate updates, and project-specific connection or capacity evidence.
