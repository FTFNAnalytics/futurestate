## What changed

NIST opened an ongoing profile-development process and framed critical-infrastructure AI across IT, OT, ICS, autonomous cyber response, facility monitoring, industrial robotics, digital twins, diagnostic systems, and optimization.

## Evidence stage

This supports `profile-development concept and stated assurance agenda`. It does not support `draft profile`, `final guidance`, `binding requirement`, `operator adoption`, or `deployed capability`.

## What to watch next

Track community workshops, requests for information, public drafts, sector profiles, final guidance, testing methods, procurement language, operator implementation, incident evidence, and independent assurance results.
