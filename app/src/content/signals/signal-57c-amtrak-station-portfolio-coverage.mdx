## Phase 57C panel

Amtrak reports 205 of 385 active stations reached substantial completion: 159 with full Amtrak responsibility met and 46 complete except for platforms outside Amtrak's responsibility.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Service inventory and reliability boundary.** Amtrak's 385 active-station ADA program denominator, split into 159 full-responsibility completions, 46 platform-excluded completions, and 180 stations not represented as substantially complete.

## Evidence boundaries

- Portfolio completion status does not measure feature uptime after turnover.
- The 46 platform-excluded stations are not equivalent to full-scope completion.
- No station-level use, outage, maintenance, rider-experience, or causal outcome series is disclosed.
