## Phase 57E panel

ConnectMT requires a provider to show average outage time across its locations below 48 hours per calendar year.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Adoption and retention acceptance control.** The provider's tested BEAD locations over a calendar year under the required availability method.

## Evidence boundaries

- The requirement is not a disclosed provider result.
- An average can conceal location-level outage concentration without the underlying distribution.
- Availability compliance does not establish subscription, retention, affordability, speed, latency, complaints, or accepted closeout.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish location-level outage periods, exclusions, averages, distributions, corrective actions, and acceptance decisions.
