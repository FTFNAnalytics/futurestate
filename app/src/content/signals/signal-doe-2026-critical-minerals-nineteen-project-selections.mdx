## What changed

DOE announced more than $45.7 million for 19 selected projects on May 19, 2026. The portfolio is intended to support pilot-scale processing and recovery work, including magnesium and rare-earth elements.

## Why it matters

The selection supplies the named project layer needed to move from a general program or funding opportunity toward inspectable implementation. Each project can now develop a separate record across award execution, obligation, location, cost share, equipment, validation, milestone, and production stages.

## Evidence limits

Selection is not necessarily an executed award or federal obligation. Pilot intent does not prove that a process works at design throughput, meets cost or environmental targets, secures feedstock or customers, obtains permits, or reaches commercial production.

## What to watch next

Watch DOE award notices, recipient disclosures, USAspending records, project locations, environmental and operating permits, equipment commissioning, technical milestones, validation results, offtake agreements, output, and trade effects.
