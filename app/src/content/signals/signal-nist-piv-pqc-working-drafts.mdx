## What changed

NIST published a working set covering PIV data representation, card command interfaces, and allowed algorithms. The proposed approach retains classical objects while adding references and containers for ML-DSA and ML-KEM.

## Evidence stage

This supports `preliminary specification work`. It does not support `formal standard`, `validated product`, `agency pilot`, `issued credential`, or `completed migration`.

## Why it matters

The record moves the federal PQC trail from executive direction and agency planning requirements into one technical identity-system dependency.

## What to watch next

Follow formal drafts, public comments, final revisions, vendor support, module validation, interoperability testing, agency pilots, procurement, credential issuance, and migration outcomes.
