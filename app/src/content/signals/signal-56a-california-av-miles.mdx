## Longitudinal read

DMV reported about 5.7 million autonomous-mode public-road test miles in the 2021-2022 period, 9,068,861 miles in 2022-2023, and 4,498,066 miles in 2023-2024.

## Comparison boundary

Publish as reported testing exposure, with safety-driver and driverless modes separated where available. Testing miles are not passenger service, deployment, normalized safety, technological capability, or a company ranking.

## What to watch next

Track permit-holder exits and entries, mode, public versus private testing, operating domain, service miles, fleet, incidents, reporting-rule changes, and the 2025 series break.
