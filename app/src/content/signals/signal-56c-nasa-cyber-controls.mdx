## Entity dossier read

NASA's FY 2025 Level 3 rating extends the panel by one compatible annual observation. A separate 2025 audit documents corporate-system zero-trust progress and delayed mission and JPL implementation, but does not establish that zero-trust work caused the maturity result.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** NASA remained at FISMA Level 3 across FY 2022-FY 2024.
2. **Named intervention or input:** NASA assigned zero-trust leadership, planned phased corporate implementation, and funded corporate initiatives while mission and JPL owners retained separate responsibilities.
3. **Constraint:** Non-corporate implementation had not begun; legacy systems, distributed ownership, and unknown non-corporate costs remained open.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** The later FY 2025 FISMA evaluation again reported Level 3, Consistently Implemented.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** NASA OIG independently reports both the implementation condition and the annual maturity result.

**Independent validation:** Independent oversight supplies two different official records; neither report validates a causal link between them.

## Alternative explanations

- Annual metric design
- Control scope and tested systems
- Other remediation work
- Legacy-system and mission-environment differences

## What to watch next

Track FY 2026 FISMA results, pillar-level implementation across corporate and non-corporate systems, tested-control operation, incidents, recovery, service effects, and mission outcomes.
