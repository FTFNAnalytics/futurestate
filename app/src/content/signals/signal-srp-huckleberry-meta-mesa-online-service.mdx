## What Changed

SRP's Project Huckleberry page identifies Meta's Mesa Data Center as the customer for a dedicated electrical-infrastructure project. SRP says it used existing 69 kV infrastructure to meet the customer's December 2022 in-service date, then placed the Prickly Pear Substation and 230 kV transmission line in service in May 2024. The page now labels the project online.

SRP also says Meta, as the primary beneficiary, funded the electrical infrastructure needed to serve the data center.

## Why It Matters

Earlier dossier records showed system planning and the E-67 framework for large-load service. Project Huckleberry adds a named customer, location, infrastructure path, funding responsibility, and completed in-service milestone.

## Dependency Stack

- Meta requested service for a named Mesa site
- SRP used existing 69 kV infrastructure for the initial schedule
- The project received the required transmission-siting approval
- SRP built the Prickly Pear Substation and a 230 kV transmission connection
- Meta funded the infrastructure as primary beneficiary
- The project reached the recorded in-service and online state

## Evidence Limits

The page does not disclose the data center's peak or actual load, energy consumption, tariff charges, executed service agreement, reliability performance, or operating economics.

This is evidence for one Meta project. It does not prove spare regional capacity, AI-specific workload demand, or electric-service readiness for TSMC, suppliers, future data centers, or any other site.

## What To Watch Next

Track customer load disclosures, executed service or facilities agreements, tariff treatment, operating consumption, later transmission additions, and comparable named semiconductor service records.
