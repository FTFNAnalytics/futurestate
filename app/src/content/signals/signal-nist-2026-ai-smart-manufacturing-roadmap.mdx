## What changed

The July 2026 roadmap consolidates twenty-one research areas spanning industrial big-data analytics, sensing, autonomous manufacturing, additive and laser processes, digital twins, logistics, robotics, sustainable manufacturing, physics-informed learning, generative and semantic AI, reliability, safety, and data-centric metrology.

## Evidence stage

This supports `current research roadmap and named deployment constraints`. It does not support `validated factory deployment`, `commercial performance`, `multi-site scale`, or `economic impact`.

## What to watch next

Track NIST measurement methods, testbed results, industrial standards, interoperability artifacts, named factory pilots, independent performance validation, workforce changes, and repeatable production outcomes.
