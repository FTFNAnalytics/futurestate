## What Changed

The Department's June 16, 2025 contract notice identifies OpenAI Public Sector LLC as the performer on prototype other transaction agreement `HQ0883-25-9-0012`. The notice gives the agreement a $200 million value and records $1,999,998 in fiscal 2025 RDT&E funds obligated at award.

GAO's September 2025 department-wide review found that DOD could not systematically link every prototype OTA to a later FAR production contract. GAO did not review or make a finding about this OpenAI agreement.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `named prototype agreement + initial obligation + system-level transition evidence limit`. It does not support `full ceiling obligated`, `prototype delivered`, `accepted`, `operationally used`, or `follow-on production`.

## Why It Matters

The record makes federal AI procurement more legible by separating instrument value, obligated funding, scope, performer, and estimated completion. Those fields are necessary to avoid converting a large ceiling number into an unsupported deployment claim.

## What To Watch Next

Track later obligations, specific tasking, prototype delivery, evaluation criteria, assurance and security controls, user acceptance, infrastructure dependencies, completion status, follow-on award identifiers, and any production or enterprise adoption decision.
