## What changed

GSA now exposes a single federal purchasing surface for multiple AI offerings and identifies pricing windows, eligibility, acquisition paths, and procurement guidance.

## Evidence stage

This supports `available government-wide purchasing channel`. It does not support `agency acquisition`, `active users`, `approved data use`, `productivity gain`, `cost saving`, or `mission outcome`.

## Why it matters

The record is downstream of AI policy and separate from the CDAO prototype agreement. It shows a civilian acquisition pathway that can be monitored through task orders, agency adoption, security authorization, renewal, and results.

## What to watch next

Track agency-specific orders, authorities to operate, user counts, permitted data classes, training, measured task outcomes, usage cost, renewal pricing, and operational dependence.
