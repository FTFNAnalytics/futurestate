## What changed

WASDE 673 lowered projected 2026/27 world total-grain ending stocks by 8.97 million metric tons and U.S. total-grain ending stocks by 5.18 million metric tons from the June vintage. For U.S. wheat, projected ending stocks moved from 744 million to 722 million bushels while the projected average farm price remained $6.00 per bushel.

## Evidence stage

This supports `official forecast revision`. It does not support `realized production`, `observed shortage`, `realized price`, `farm outcome`, or `causal attribution`.

## What to watch next

Follow weekly crop condition, subsequent production estimates, trade, demand, stocks, price realization, and the August WASDE vintage.
