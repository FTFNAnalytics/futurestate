## What changed

BLS's fourth-quarter 2025 county release reports 2,318,500 covered jobs in Maricopa County in December, 0.1 percent below December 2024. The county's average weekly wage was $1,512, 2.3 percent above the prior-year quarter.

## Why it matters

Phoenix-area industrial growth is often described through announced jobs or training programs. QCEW adds a broad administrative baseline for employment, establishments, and wages and can be narrowed by industry in later extracts.

## Evidence limits

The headline county values do not isolate semiconductor manufacturing, construction trades, utilities, engineering services, suppliers, occupations, vacancies, skills, shifts, attrition, or training outcomes. Quarterly data are lagged and detailed records can be suppressed or revised.

## What to watch next

Build Maricopa industry extracts for semiconductor manufacturing, construction, utilities, engineering, and technical services; connect them to OEWS occupations, job postings, employer disclosures, apprenticeships, college completions, retention, and project schedules.
