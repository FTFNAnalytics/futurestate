## Repeat-outcome and alternative-explanation test

The test remains held because public records do not expose a compatible repeat output or input series.

## Tested alternatives

- Labor input
- Tooling and layout
- Product mix
- Demand and backlog
- Unrecorded private capital investment

## Later observations

1. The current company page identifies manufacturing, engineering, quality, procurement, tooling, and information-system roles.
2. The independently attributable OSHA inspection predates the line intervention and is not a later production observation.

## Compatibility and attribution

**Compatibility:** The company identity is stable; neither record uses the same line, output unit, labor denominator, or post-intervention window.

**Attribution:** Operating roles are company-attributed; the historical inspection is OSHA-attributed.

**Validation status:** Entity continuity is partially supported; line performance is not independently validated.

## What remains unresolved

Obtain same-line output, labor hours, WIP, yield, scrap, downtime, cost, delivery, demand, and dated capital records.

## Publication boundary

Hold interpretation until a compatible later outcome, common denominator, and sufficient alternative-explanation controls are independently observable.
