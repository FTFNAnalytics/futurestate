## Named-entity panel

DOE's later records show continuing FISMA and access-control work alongside a bounded 2026 statutory determination that the reviewed sharing requirements were implemented.

## Observations

1. FY 2023: continuing program weaknesses
2. FY 2024: 44 prior recommendations remained and 79 new recommendations were issued
3. FY 2025 management letter: 33 cyber findings, including 13 repeat findings
4. April 2026: reviewed Cybersecurity Act information-sharing actions were implemented

## Reporting breaks

- The FY 2025 management letter is a financial-audit record, not the annual FISMA metric set.
- The 2026 information-sharing review covers a narrower statutory control domain.

## What remains unresolved

- FY 2025 FISMA evaluation
- Finding-level closure and repeat-finding resolution
- System incidents, recovery, mission and laboratory service effects
