## Longitudinal read

The 2023, 2024, and 2025 Mineral Commodity Summaries maintain an annual commodity-by-commodity record of production, trade, and import reliance. The 2024 edition reported $105 billion in 2023 U.S. nonfuel mineral production value.

## Comparison boundary

Publish as annual commodity statistics. Nominal value is not physical output, refining capacity, qualified supply, price resilience, inventory, or facility availability; commodity definitions and withheld values can change.

## What to watch next

Track revised commodity tables, physical quantity, real value, mine and processing capacity, trade, price, import reliance, withheld data, qualification, substitution, recycling, and disruptions.
