## What Changed

Statistics Canada's Building Permits Survey gives FTFN a formal monthly source for construction intentions in Canada. CMHC's construction tables add the next conversion layer by tracking starts, completions, and units under construction.

## Why It Matters

Permits can be an early indicator of construction momentum, but they are not completed supply. Projects still depend on capital, labor, servicing, market absorption, and local approvals.

## Dependency Stack

- Permit applications and approvals
- Starts, completions, and units under construction
- Project financing
- Labor and materials
- Servicing and infrastructure capacity
- Starts, completions, and occupancy

## Evidence Limits

This signal does not prove that permitted units will be completed, affordable, or delivered on schedule. Treat permits as an upstream indicator, and use CMHC starts/completions tables plus municipal infrastructure and financing evidence to test whether intentions convert into delivered housing.

## What To Watch Next

Watch monthly permit changes alongside housing starts, completions, municipal servicing capacity, and financing conditions.
