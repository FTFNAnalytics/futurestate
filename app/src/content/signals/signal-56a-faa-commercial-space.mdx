## Longitudinal read

FAA's FY 2025 Agency Financial Report gives a consistent annual series of 74 licensed commercial space operations in FY 2022, 113 in FY 2023, and 148 in FY 2024.

## Comparison boundary

Publish as national licensed-operation counts. The series does not distinguish launch from reentry, site, vehicle, mission success, anomaly, payload, cost, delay, environmental compliance, or local benefit.

## What to watch next

Track final revised counts, launch versus reentry, site, vehicle, license, success, anomaly, delay, mission type, payload, closure duration, and local operating outcomes.
