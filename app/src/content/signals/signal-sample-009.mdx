## What Changed

AI electricity demand analysis now has a stronger evidence stack in FTFN: IEA provides international analysis of AI and electricity demand, while EIA electricity data provides an official U.S. electricity data anchor.

## Why It Matters

AI scaling depends on physical systems: generation, transmission, distribution, interconnection, cooling, water, land, permitting, and local legitimacy. Compute expansion may be constrained by infrastructure long before it is constrained only by model design.

## Dependency Stack

- Power supply and grid reliability
- Interconnection and utility planning
- Data center construction and cooling
- Semiconductor supply
- Water and land-use approvals
- Public legitimacy in host communities

## Evidence Limits

This signal should not be used to claim that a specific region or utility can or cannot absorb new demand. That requires local utility planning, interconnection, rate-case, water, land-use, and project evidence.

## What To Watch Next

Track specific electricity datasets, utility filings, data-center siting decisions, and local permitting signals that turn global AI demand into local infrastructure pressure.
