## Phase 56Z panel

DOE reports that two planning changes are expected to reduce mission demand by about 300 high-level-waste canisters, including 150 fewer from a process change that allows 5.5 percent more waste per canister.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Comparison hold.** Liquid Waste System Plan Revision 24.1's forecast mission canister count and two modeled 150-canister changes.

## Evidence boundaries

- The 300-canister reduction is an expected plan result, not a final realized count.
- One component follows a process change and one follows a program decision, so attribution differs.
- No final cost, safety, environmental, or mission-completion outcome is established.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track actual waste loading, canisters poured, revised mission total, quality, downtime, accepted storage, cost, and final disposal before promotion.

## Authority boundary

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
