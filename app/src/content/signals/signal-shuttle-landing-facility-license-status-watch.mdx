## August 15, 2026 Dated Hold

The FAA Shuttle Landing Facility page, linked LRSO 18-018 license PDF, and FAA spaceport inventory were rechecked on August 15, 2026. The inventory still labels the site FAA Licensed while displaying a January 15, 2026 expiration date; the page and PDF do not supply a later formal renewal, replacement, lapse, surrender, or other disposition.

This is a site-license hold only. NASA's current description of Space Florida's management of the physical facility, a proposed vehicle or operator application, an environmental record, a mission, or any measure of facility use does not resolve the FAA site-license disposition.

## Stop Condition

Hold this record until the FAA publishes or provides a formal current disposition for LRSO 18-018. Do not assign a renewed, replaced, lapsed, surrendered, operational, or utilized status from the unchanged public display.
