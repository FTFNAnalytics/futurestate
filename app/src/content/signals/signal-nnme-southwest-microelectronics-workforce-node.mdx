## What Changed

The Arizona Commerce Authority's May 27, 2026 record establishes NNME Southwest as a regional node spanning Arizona, Colorado, Utah, New Mexico, and Southern California. It identifies 47 members across employers, education, workforce, government, K-12, STEM, and community organizations.

Named industry participants include TSMC, Intel, Amkor, Micron, Applied Materials, Lam Research, Nikon Precision, Synopsys, Arm, Northrop Grumman, RTX, Teledyne Technologies, and HRL Laboratories. The announced work includes aligned curriculum, stackable credentials, cleanroom access, pre-apprenticeships, internships, fellowships, and broader access to training.

## Evidence Stage

This supports `named regional workforce coordination structure`.

It does not support `funds delivered`, `training capacity installed`, `participants enrolled`, `credentials completed`, `workers placed`, `workers retained`, `vacancies filled`, or `regional workforce sufficiency`.

## Why It Matters

The record expands the workforce evidence beyond one TSMC apprenticeship into a multi-employer and multi-institution system. That improves the actor map and the list of records FTFN can now follow, while keeping the outcome question open.

## What To Watch Next

Track executed partner agreements, budgets, curriculum delivery, cleanroom capacity, enrollment, completions, credentials, placements, retention, employer demand, vacancies, supplier hiring, and geographic distribution.
