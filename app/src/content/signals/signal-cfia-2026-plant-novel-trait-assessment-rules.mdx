## What Changed

The Canadian Food Inspection Agency revised Directive 94-08 on May 20, 2026.

The directive describes the environmental safety assessment criteria and application path for plants with novel traits. It also points to separate decision documents for authorized plants and novel feeds.

## Why It Matters

Agricultural biotechnology stories often compress several stages into one claim. CFIA's structure provides a better evidence stack:

- Determine whether the plant is regulated as novel
- Assess environmental safety and intended release
- Issue a product-specific authorization and conditions
- Complete feed and food reviews where required
- Maintain stewardship and compliance
- Establish commercial availability, adoption, and field performance separately

## Evidence Limits

The directive is regulatory infrastructure. It does not authorize a named product and does not prove safety or performance for every plant produced with a particular breeding method.

Environmental authorization is also not the same as food authorization, feed authorization, seed availability, acreage, yield, environmental benefit, or market acceptance.

## What To Watch Next

Watch specific decision documents, notices under review, stewardship conditions, food and feed decisions, commercial release, adoption, and field-performance evidence.
