## Phase 57G registry panel

FTFN now maintains 14 ordered DFLAW lifecycle nodes from tank retrieval through TSCR, AP-106, laboratory certification, LAW processing, two named melters, container handling, shipment, IDF staging, and final disposal.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Batch-container-stage registry.** Fourteen public process or custody nodes derived from DOE and regulator records, each with a stable stage key and explicit input/output relation.

Structured registry: `phase-57g-hanford-batch-container-stage-registry.json`.

## Evidence boundaries

- A stage key is not a public batch or container ID.
- The process path does not prove every transfer occurred for the same material cohort.
- Custody and acceptance authority remain distinct.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Attach source-reported batch and container identities without inferring missing transitions.

## Authority boundary

A registry row is an identity, lineage, cohort, stage, or work-breakdown object—not reliability, adoption, yield, readiness, output, implementation, or closure. Historical and current revisions, site and facility, project and subproject, BSL and CAI, feed and glass, container stages, capacity and qualified output, agency assertions and independent acceptance remain separate. No record supports a ranking, composite, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or unsupported causal attribution.
