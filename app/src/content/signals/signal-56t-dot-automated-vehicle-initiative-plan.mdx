## Decision

Public candidate matrix complete; authoritative sufficiency unresolved. Target artifact: DOT Automated Vehicle Framework and related initiatives. Likely custodian: DOT Office of the Secretary or the named operating administration.

## Directive matrix

- **One department-wide automated-vehicle plan:** Partly supported by located public record. DOT publishes a framework, while GAO says it is not the comprehensive plan required. Locator: DOT April 2025 Automated Vehicle Framework release: three principles and initial initiatives, but not one department-wide comprehensive plan.
- **Goals, priorities, and implementation steps:** Partly supported by located public record. The framework identifies policy directions and initiatives. Locator: GAO-18-132 Recommendation 1: as of March 2026 DOT still lacks priorities, milestones, and other leading planning principles.
- **Milestones and performance measures:** Not established by located public record. GAO reports these comprehensive-plan elements remain insufficient. Locator: No page or section in the release establishes a complete milestone and performance-measure system for departmental AV initiatives.
