## Phase 56Z panel

FTA obligations rose from $37.280 billion to $56.710 billion and outlays from $18.595 billion to $34.573 billion; the obligation share moved from 48.2 to 57.3 percent and the outlay share from 24.0 to 34.9 percent.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Repeat funding measurement.** FTA formula-plus-discretionary IIJA funding; adjusted authority changed from $77.356 billion to $98.931 billion.

## Evidence boundaries

- The authority base is not fixed.
- An outlay is a payment, not an accepted transit asset or operating result.
- The panel does not establish ridership, reliability, accessibility, safety, or emissions outcomes.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
