## Longitudinal read

The 2022, 2023, and 2024 Air Travel Consumer Report shelves preserve recurring flight cancellation, on-time, baggage, complaint, and accessibility measures. The 2024 full-year cancellation rate was 1.4 percent versus 1.3 percent in 2023.

## Comparison boundary

Publish individual measures with the reporting-carrier denominator and revision date attached. Do not combine service reliability, complaints, baggage, accessibility, price, or safety into one carrier score.

## What to watch next

Track revised tables, reporting-carrier coverage, flights operated, cancellations, delay causes, complaints, accessibility, baggage, airport mix, and weather.
