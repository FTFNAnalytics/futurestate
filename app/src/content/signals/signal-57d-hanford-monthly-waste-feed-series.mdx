## Phase 57D panel

The consent-decree reports state that the LAW Facility processed more than 18,550 gallons of waste feed in April 2026 and 29,757 gallons in May 2026.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Monthly material-flow series.** Two named calendar-month feed observations for the same LAW Facility and reporting authority; April is a lower-bound value and May is reported as an exact value.

## Evidence boundaries

- A two-month series is not steady-state performance.
- The April greater-than threshold prevents an exact month-over-month percentage calculation.
- Feed gallons are not glass mass, filled containers, accepted output, disposed output, quality yield, uptime, or cost.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Append each monthly report using the same feed field and add glass mass, containers by stage, quality yield, rejects, rework, downtime, acceptance, disposal, compliance, and cost.
