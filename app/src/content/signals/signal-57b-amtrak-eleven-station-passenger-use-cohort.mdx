## Phase 57B panel

Amtrak reports that eleven named stations reached substantial completion between November 1, 2025 and April 30, 2026; its definition says primary scope was complete and the facilities were turned over for passenger use.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted-service cohort.** Eleven named station projects in Amtrak's November 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026 construction cohort: Camden, Detroit Lakes, Du Quoin, Fort Morgan, Granby, Hamlet, Havre, Miami, Pomona, Rocklin, and Rugby.

## Evidence boundaries

- Substantial completion can leave punch-list or minor work outstanding.
- Passenger-use turnover does not establish station-feature uptime or trip-level accessibility.
- The report does not attribute ridership, safety, reliability, or customer outcomes to the projects.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track each named station through final completion, compliant features, entrance and platform availability, outages, service use, and rider experience.
