## Phase 57C panel

Amtrak reports 240 station construction projects completed, 74 in progress, and 132 upcoming in the June 2026 program inventory.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Service inventory and reliability boundary.** The report's 446 construction-project status entries, preserved as 240 completed, 74 in progress, and 132 upcoming rather than collapsed into a completion rate for stations.

## Evidence boundaries

- Projects are not unique stations and can represent different scopes.
- Upcoming work is a pipeline state rather than accepted service.
- The inventory does not establish feature availability, reliability, use, cost outcome, or rider experience.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
