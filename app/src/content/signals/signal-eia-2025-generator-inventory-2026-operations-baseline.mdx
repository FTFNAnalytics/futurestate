## What changed

EIA released early 2025 Form 860 generator data on June 9, 2026 and updated monthly Form 923 plant-operation files through May 2026 on July 23.

Form 860 distinguishes operable, proposed, retired, canceled, and postponed generators. Form 923 reports observed generation, fuel use, receipts, stocks, and related plant data. The datasets answer different questions and should be used together only with explicit vintages, fields, geographies, and status filters.

## Evidence limits

The Form 860 release is preliminary. A proposed unit is not a committed or operating resource. Plant production does not establish transmission headroom, interconnection availability, distribution service, reliability, emissions compliance, or price for a named customer.

## What to watch next

Build bounded Arizona extracts, reconcile facility identifiers, preserve revisions, connect balancing-authority data from Form 930, and then add utility, transmission, interconnection, tariff, service-agreement, and project-load evidence.
