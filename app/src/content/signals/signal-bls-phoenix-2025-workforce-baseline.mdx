## What Changed

BLS's May 2025 Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler release gives the Southwest dossier a current metropolitan occupational baseline.

The survey reports an all-occupation mean hourly wage of $33.48. Construction and extraction accounted for 5.6% of area employment with a mean hourly wage of $29.42; production accounted for 4.1% at $25.08; and architecture and engineering accounted for 1.6% at $50.95.

## Why It Matters

Semiconductor expansion depends on several labor systems at once: engineers, technicians, construction trades, maintenance, production, logistics, utilities, and suppliers.

The BLS record helps establish the surrounding labor-market scale and wage structure before FTFN evaluates company hiring or training claims.

## Evidence Limits

These are cross-industry estimates for a metropolitan area that includes Maricopa and Pinal counties. They do not isolate semiconductor establishments or a named employer.

They also do not show vacancies, hiring demand, attrition, training completions, credential match, shift premiums, contractor capacity, or workforce sufficiency. The release was reissued to correct sample-size and response-rate information, reinforcing the need to preserve the release vintage.

## What To Watch Next

Watch detailed occupation estimates, QCEW industry employment, semiconductor employer filings, apprenticeship outcomes, community-college completions, vacancies, retention, construction-trade demand, and supplier hiring.
