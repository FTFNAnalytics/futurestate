## Phase 57E panel

Amtrak's ADA report lists 93 completed PIDS deployments while its Accessible Canada Act report lists 117 completed PIDS installations for the same April 2026 reporting horizon.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Asset-quality reconciliation.** Two Amtrak accessibility reports dated June 2026, preserving the 93-deployment and 117-installation labels as separate unresolved inventories rather than merging them.

## Evidence boundaries

- The reports do not define whether deployments and installations cover different scopes, stations, devices, or closeout states.
- Neither count establishes accepted FRA closeout, operating availability, uptime, actual use, or rider benefit.
- The 24-record difference prevents a single PIDS completion denominator without a published crosswalk.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish a station- and device-level crosswalk explaining scope, revision, closeout, operational status, uptime, defects, maintenance, actual use, complaints, and rider outcomes.
