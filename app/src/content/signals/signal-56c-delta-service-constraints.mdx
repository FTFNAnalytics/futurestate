## Entity dossier read

Delta's operating-carrier cancellation rate moved from 0.91% in 2024 to 1.08% in 2025. DOT's service dashboard adds controllable-disruption commitments, but it does not explain the movement, measure remedy delivery, or authorize a cross-carrier comparison.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** Delta reported 9,147 cancellations among 1,009,194 scheduled operations in 2024.
2. **Named intervention or input:** Delta publishes customer-service commitments for specified controllable cancellations and delays through DOT's dashboard.
3. **Constraint:** Schedule mix, branded network identity, airports, weather, maintenance, crews, and recovery conditions remain separate.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** Delta reported 11,114 cancellations among 1,026,332 scheduled operations in 2025, a 1.08% rate.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** Carrier-filed operational data are published by DOT; commitments are carrier representations compiled by DOT.

**Independent validation:** DOT validates the reporting rails, not a causal effect, a service-quality score, or universal delivery of remedies.

## Alternative explanations

- Weather and airport exposure
- Schedule and branded-network mix
- Maintenance and staffing
- Air traffic constraints
- Recovery decisions

## What to watch next

Track 2026 full-year operations, delay causes, complaints, accessibility, schedule mix, airport exposure, commitment compliance, and revisions.
