## What Changed

NIST's PQC project page gives FTFN a primary source for treating post-quantum cryptography as infrastructure migration rather than distant theory. The practical work is inventory, standards adoption, software updates, validation, procurement, and vendor readiness; standards availability does not prove institutional migration is complete.

## Why It Matters

Quantum risk is not only about when quantum computers arrive. It is also about whether existing institutions can migrate critical systems in time.

## Dependency Stack

- Final standards
- Cryptographic inventory
- Vendor implementation
- Validation and procurement
- Migration planning
- Security workforce capacity

## What To Watch Next

Track standards adoption, procurement rules, vendor readiness, and cryptographic inventory work.
