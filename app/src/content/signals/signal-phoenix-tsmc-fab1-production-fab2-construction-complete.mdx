## What Changed

The City of Phoenix's July 16, 2026 TSMC update says the first Arizona fab has been in volume production of N4 technology since late 2024. It also says construction of the second fab is complete, with volume production expected in 2027, and that TSMC Arizona currently employs more than 3,500 people.

The same release announces a much larger future investment plan. This signal uses only the stated current operating, construction, and employment milestones; it does not treat the future plan as delivered capacity.

## Why It Matters

The North Phoenix PUD established the campus planning envelope. This later record adds a claimed operating state for the first fab, a completed-construction state for the second, and a current facility-employment count.

## Dependency Stack

- Fab construction must be completed
- Tools and production systems must be installed and commissioned
- Processes must be qualified for volume production
- Power, water, wastewater, materials, equipment, and labor must remain available
- The second fab must move from completed construction through installation and qualification
- Later operating disclosures must distinguish output from announced capacity

## Evidence Limits

The City release relays TSMC's claims. It is not an audited production, yield, employment, energy, water, or supplier disclosure.

It does not provide a named building permit, inspection, certificate of occupancy, production volume, workforce occupation mix, utility service agreement, or proof that announced future fabs will be built.

## What To Watch Next

Track named permits and certificates of occupancy, regulatory or audited production evidence, Fab 2 commissioning and qualification, occupational employment data, supplier operating records, and facility-level power and water evidence.
