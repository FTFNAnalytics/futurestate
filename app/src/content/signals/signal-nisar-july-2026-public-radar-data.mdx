## Current state

NISAR has crossed a concrete operating gate: processed science data is publicly available, and additional observations are expected to be released as processing continues.

## Evidence boundary

Data availability is not complete coverage, a validated domain model, institutional use, or a measured local decision outcome.

## Next records

Track product coverage, calibration, archive completion, documented data quality, validated applications, user adoption, operational decision products, and local outcomes.
