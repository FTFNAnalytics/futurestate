## Evidence Stage

USGS reports $112 billion in U.S. nonfuel mineral production in 2025, up 5.6 percent, while China remained a major source for 14 of the 33 critical minerals on which the United States was most import reliant.

## Boundary

Publish as national commodity data. Do not convert production value into physical supply security or facility availability.

## What To Watch Next

Track commodity-level output, refining, trade, prices, qualification, inventory, substitution, recycling, and disruption.
