## Phase 57F panel

The LEO quarterly schema requires the number of customer premises equipment units shipped to project broadband serviceable locations during the reporting period.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Observed adoption measurement contract.** The approved project BSL cohort and quarter, separating CPE shipped from delivered, installed, activated, subscribed, tested, retained, or accepted.

## Evidence boundaries

- Shipment is not delivery or installation.
- One unit cannot be assumed to equal one active subscriber.
- The instructions provide no awardee shipment or activation result.
