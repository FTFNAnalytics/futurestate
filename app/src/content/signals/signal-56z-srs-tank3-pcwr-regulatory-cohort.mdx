## Phase 56Z panel

DOE reports regulator concurrence on preliminary cease waste removal for Tank 3, the seventh old-style tank to reach PCWR since 2024 and one of seven completed 7 to 27 months ahead of Federal Facility Agreement deadlines.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted cleanup operation.** Seven named old-style SRS tanks receiving PCWR concurrence since 2024 under the Federal Facility Agreement process.

## Evidence boundaries

- PCWR is preliminary reasonable assurance, not final closure.
- Sampling, analysis, and final residual-volume determination follow the concurrence.
- Schedule performance does not establish final stabilization, isolation, risk outcome, or cost performance.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track residual determinations, grouting, isolation, regulatory closure, and post-closure monitoring for the same seven tanks.

## Authority boundary

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
