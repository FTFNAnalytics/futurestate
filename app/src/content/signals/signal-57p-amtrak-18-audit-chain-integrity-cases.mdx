## Evidence stage and denominator

**Amtrak append-only audit, adjudication, and publication-receipt controls.** 18 fixture-only audit cases across 2 contracts; zero failures and zero actual audit events.

Structured registry: phase-57p-append-only-dual-review-audit-chains.json.

## Evidence boundaries

- Passing fixtures do not create evidence or reviewer history.
- A valid hash link does not validate the underlying claim.
- Supersession preserves rather than rewrites the earlier decision.

Actual evidence receipts, publication receipts, adjudications, escalations, and manual release authorizations: **Zero**. Trigger, closure, and publication events: **Zero**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Require contiguous sequence, increasing time, prior-event digest, payload digest, and immutable event digest for every actual append.
