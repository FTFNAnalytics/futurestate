## Named-entity panel

The records preserve the annual program findings and the separate continuous-monitoring scope without making recommendation counts a readiness score.

## Observations

1. FY 2023: two resolved-but-open recommendations
2. FY 2024: ten resolved-but-open recommendations
3. FY 2025: Level 2, Defined, with seven recommendations
4. September 2024: 62 of 85 reviewed NAS cyber-management systems lacked near-real-time monitoring

## Reporting breaks

- Annual metric sets and recommendation scopes changed.
- The 62-of-85 denominator applies to reviewed NAS Cyber Management Systems, not all DOT systems.

## What remains unresolved

- FY 2026 annual review
- Closure evidence for the monitoring recommendations
- System incidents, recovery, affected transportation services, and mission outcomes
