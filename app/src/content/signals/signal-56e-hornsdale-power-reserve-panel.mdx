## Named-entity panel

Event response and installed capacity remain distinct from offered service capability, compliance, availability, and value.

## Observations

1. November 2019: rapid response supported frequency arrest during separation
2. Final report: service response varied by category and settings were adjusted
3. 2022: court-enforced capability and offer violations for a 2019 period
4. Current state profile: 150 MW after the 2020 expansion

## Reporting breaks

- Event-specific response and multi-month offer compliance use different intervals.
- Installed power capacity does not describe energy duration or delivered service.

## What remains unresolved

- Annual availability and dispatch
- Service enablement and delivery by interval
- Cycling, degradation, revenue, safety, and customer outcomes
