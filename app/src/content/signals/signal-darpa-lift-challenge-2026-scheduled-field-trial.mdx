## What Changed

DARPA's official August 11 results close the exact measured-results gate. AVIDrone placed first in the objective category at a 3.84:1 payload-to-weight ratio and received $1.25 million. MTech Operations placed second at 3.64:1 and received $750,000; Xtreme Aerial Concepts placed third at 3.45:1 and received $500,000.

DARPA also published three $500,000 subjective-category awards: the University of Maryland Autonomous Micro Air Vehicle Team for aerodynamic design, Portal Aircraft Company for powertrain design, and DefendTex for most promising technology. DefendTex's 9.63:1 demonstration was not a completed scored run and is not substituted for the objective-category results.

## Evidence Stage

This record now supports `official measured competition result + named winners + prize decisions`. DARPA states that no official score reached the 4:1 target. The result does not yet support `certified service`, `production scale`, `recurring qualified operation`, `military or commercial deployment`, or `comparable operating outcome`.

## Why It Matters

The result converts a scheduled field trial into a bounded, measured empirical record. It also shows why the stage boundary matters: an official competition result is stronger than an event announcement but remains upstream of transition, manufacture, certification, procurement, service, and repeat performance.
