## What Changed

DARPA moved the Lift Challenge from an application pool to a named first group of 72 invited teams. Official challenge materials identify a live Dayton, Ohio event from August 2-9, 2026 and an August 9 awards ceremony. The canonical page reviewed on August 11 links an event guide and scoreboard surface, but the reviewed DARPA page does not publish a final results table, winner, measured scores, or prize decisions.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `named performers + dated event window`. It does not yet support `official measured result`, `target achieved`, `winner selected`, `prize awarded`, `technology transitioned`, or `service deployed`.
