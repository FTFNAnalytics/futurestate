## What Changed

DARPA moved the Lift Challenge from an application pool to a named first group of 72 invited teams. Official challenge materials identify a live Dayton, Ohio event from August 2-9, 2026 and an August 9 awards ceremony. The August 10 recheck did not locate an official final-results table or measured team-score release.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `named performers + dated event window`. It does not yet support `official measured result`, `target achieved`, `winner selected`, `prize awarded`, `technology transitioned`, or `service deployed`.
