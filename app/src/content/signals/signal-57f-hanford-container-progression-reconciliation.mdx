## Phase 57F panel

Official records separately report 19 containers sent to IDF at the February meeting, 34 filled as of a February regulator snapshot, 66 shipped by the May meeting, and about 30 staged at the first-disposal event.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Full output reconciliation.** Four dated observations under different authorities and lifecycle labels, preserved as sent, filled, shipped, staged, and disposed states without assuming one-to-one identity.

## Evidence boundaries

- The dates, verbs, and reporting authorities differ.
- The records do not prove the same containers appear in every observation.
- No exact disposed total or container-level lineage is public.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish a regulator-verifiable container register with stable IDs, stage dates, quality disposition, mass, acceptance, and disposal status.

## Authority boundary

Historical cohorts, current inventories, assets, requirements, serviceability, adoption, retention, feed, glass, nominal specifications, measured mass, container stages, capacity, qualified output, forecasts, completed baselines, implementation, and independent closure remain separate. Operator, regulator, state, federal, contractor, and independent evidence retain distinct attribution. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or unsupported causal attribution.
