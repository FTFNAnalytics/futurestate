## What changed

GAO published report GAO-26-108687 on July 22, 2026. The review compares selected substitution and recycling approaches for battery and semiconductor materials and asks how quickly those approaches might reduce U.S. supply-chain risk.

The report gives battery recycling a potentially nearer path for some materials, roughly two to three years in the conditions GAO reviewed. Several semiconductor approaches face longer adoption paths because recovered or substituted materials must satisfy demanding performance requirements and move through collection, separation, processing, qualification, and manufacturing change.

## Why it matters

Critical-minerals coverage can become misleading when a promising chemistry, recycling yield, or research result is treated as domestic capacity. The GAO record identifies the missing middle: infrastructure, standards, economics, manufacturer acceptance, and commercial throughput.

## Evidence limits

The report is not a project forecast. It does not establish operating capacity, material volumes, facility locations, project finance, permits, customers, production, shipments, or reduced import shares. Its battery findings should not be generalized to semiconductor inputs or across commodities.

## What to watch next

Watch recipient-specific DOE awards, USAspending obligations, pilot locations, state and local permits, equipment installation, feedstock and offtake agreements, qualification records, production volumes, trade statistics, and import-share changes by commodity.
