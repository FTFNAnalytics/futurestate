## Current state

NOAA's annual report provides official global temperature, ocean heat, sea ice, snow, and tropical-cyclone indicators for 2025.

## Evidence boundary

The report is an observation baseline. It does not establish causal attribution for a named event or the impact of climate conditions on a specific local system.

## Next records

Pair the annual baseline with regional analyses, hazard records, asset exposure, sector data, losses, adaptation decisions, and measured outcomes.
