## Longitudinal read

GAO reported about 900 open cybersecurity recommendations in November 2021, nearly 21 percent of 712 recommendations open in one federal-systems challenge area in December 2022, and 567 of 1,610 recommendations open across four challenge areas in May 2024.

## Comparison boundary

Publish the persistence of a material backlog, not a numeric improvement rate. The recommendation universes and challenge-area scopes differ across the three reports.

## What to watch next

Track a stable recommendation cohort, implementation dates, verified closure, recurrence, incident exposure, and the scope of each GAO synthesis.
