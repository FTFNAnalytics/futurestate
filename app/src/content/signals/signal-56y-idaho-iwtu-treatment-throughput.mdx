## Phase 56Y result

DOE reports more than 130,000 gallons treated in the current processing run and nearly 400,000 gallons treated since operations began in 2023, against an approximately 900,000-gallon total mission.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Cleanup delivery and outcome.** Current-run gallons, cumulative gallons since 2023, and approximately 900,000 gallons of original inventory; the state plan separately requires at least 15 percent annually on a rolling three-year average.

## Evidence boundaries

- Current-run, cumulative, total-inventory, and rolling-average percentages are different measures.
- Surpassing a state milestone does not establish full treatment or permanent disposal.
- DOE estimates five to seven years to complete treatment including maintenance, which is a forecast.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track accepted treated volume, downtime, maintenance, canister storage, rolling-plan compliance, residual inventory, and final disposal under stable denominators.

## Authority boundary

Funding authority, selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, and closure remain separate. Agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or GAO acceptance.
