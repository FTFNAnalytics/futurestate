## Entity dossier read

United's operating-carrier cancellation rate moved from 1.64% in 2024 to 0.82% in 2025. DOT's service dashboard adds controllable-disruption commitments, but the commitments do not explain the movement or prove passenger receipt.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** United reported 12,478 cancellations among 760,451 scheduled operations in 2024.
2. **Named intervention or input:** United publishes customer-service commitments for specified controllable cancellations and delays through DOT's dashboard.
3. **Constraint:** Schedule mix, airports, weather, maintenance, crews, network recovery, and marketing-versus-operating identity remain separate.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** United reported 6,527 cancellations among 795,271 scheduled operations in 2025, a 0.82% rate.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** Carrier-filed operational data are published by DOT; commitments are carrier representations compiled by DOT.

**Independent validation:** DOT validates the reporting rails, not a causal effect of commitments or universal delivery of remedies.

## Alternative explanations

- Weather and airport exposure
- Schedule and network mix
- Maintenance and staffing
- Air traffic constraints
- Recovery decisions

## What to watch next

Track 2026 full-year operations, delay causes, complaints, accessibility, schedule mix, airport exposure, commitment compliance, and revisions.
