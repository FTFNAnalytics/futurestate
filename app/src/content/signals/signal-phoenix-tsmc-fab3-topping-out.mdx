## What changed

Phoenix records that the final and highest structural beam was installed on TSMC's third Arizona fab. The page also says initial construction had begun on a fourth fab and a first advanced-packaging plant.

## Evidence stage

This supports `physical superstructure milestone + reported start of later facilities`. It does not support `building complete`, `certificate of occupancy`, `tools installed`, `qualified process`, `volume production`, or `measured capacity`.

## Why it matters

The record advances the local dossier from capital commitments and planning envelopes into visible facility construction. It also increases the importance of the still-open power, water, wastewater, workforce, and supplier trails.

## What to watch next

Follow named permits, inspections, occupancy records, tool installation, utility agreements, reclaimed-water milestones, hiring outcomes, qualification, customers, and production for each facility.
