## What Changed

NHTSA's Standing General Order requires named manufacturers and operators to report certain crashes involving automated driving systems and Level 2 advanced driver assistance systems. The agency's current page identifies a third amended order and provides data through June 15, 2026.

## Why It Matters

The reporting rail creates a continuing federal evidence source for safety oversight. It also demonstrates why autonomous-mobility analysis needs more than a raw incident count: reporting requirements differ by system type, and exposure, operating conditions, duplication, redaction, and reporting awareness all affect interpretation.
