## Named-entity panel

The three annual audits and the later GAO inventory preserve continuing oversight work without turning unlike recommendation scopes into a performance trend.

## Observations

1. FY 2022: annual FISMA audit baseline
2. FY 2023: 25 recommendations after testing 45 systems
3. FY 2024: 23 recommendations after testing 49 applications and systems
4. January 2026: 38 open CIO recommendations across subjects and vintages

## Reporting breaks

- Tested systems increased from 45 to 49 between the stated FY 2023 and FY 2024 records.
- GAO's CIO inventory is not the annual FISMA recommendation denominator.

## What remains unresolved

- FY 2025 or FY 2026 FISMA results
- Recommendation-level closures and tested control operation
- Incidents, recovery, affected services, and mission effects
