## Comparison boundary

Publish as a recurring federal activity and appendix series. Narrative accomplishments, budgets, launches, missions, and research outputs use different denominators and do not form one aerospace performance score.

## What to watch next

Track stable appendix tables, launch definitions, mission outcome, anomalies, payload, budget basis, agency coverage, publication lag, and revisions.
