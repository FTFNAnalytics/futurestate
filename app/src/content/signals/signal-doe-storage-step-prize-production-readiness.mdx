## What Changed

DOE's Office of Electricity launched the Storage Technology Elevation Prize in July 2026. The agency describes a two-phase, $500,000 competition intended to help next-generation storage teams address manufacturability, material, and supply-chain barriers before production.

## Why It Matters

The record points at a common conversion gap. A storage chemistry or architecture can show technical promise and still fail to scale because the production process, components, safety case, supplier network, cost, warranty, customer qualification, or project finance is not ready.

## Dependency Stack

- Competition eligibility and selection
- Manufacturing process and tooling
- Materials and component supply
- Safety and performance validation
- Production partners and quality systems
- Customer qualification and bankability
- Project permits and interconnection

## Evidence Limits

The prize launch is not a technology result, award outcome, production record, cost curve, safety validation, or grid deployment. The $500,000 figure describes the competition purse across its phases, not a demonstrated manufacturing investment.

## What To Watch Next

Track selected teams, phase results, test evidence, manufacturing partners, pilot production, customer qualification, cost disclosures, safety records, permits, and project deployments.
