## Current record

Toronto Hydro applied for an incentive mechanism covering eligible non-wires solutions proposed for May 1, 2026 through December 31, 2029. The OEB acknowledged the application, issued procedural orders, and published interrogatory records in July.

## Why it matters

Non-wires solutions sit between grid planning, customer demand, distributed resources, capital allocation, and regulation. The docket can show what evidence and incentive structure the regulator accepts.

## Evidence limits

The current record is an application and active evidentiary process. It is not a substantive approval, selected project, deployed non-wires solution, avoided capital project, measured capacity gain, customer benefit, or cost result.

## What to watch next

Watch responses to interrogatories, staff and intervenor submissions, additional procedural orders, hearing materials, the OEB decision and order, approved program rules, project selections, spending, measurement and verification, system performance, and customer outcomes.
