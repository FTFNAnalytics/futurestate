## Entity dossier read

Florida PSC filings add a 900 MWh energy figure, approximately 2.2 hours of duration, 0.84 conversion efficiency, and stated dispatch uses to the stable 409 MW panel. They describe asset design and intended operation, not realized performance or customer benefit.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** EIA plant code 60014 remained at 409 MW across final 2021-2023 inventories.
2. **Named intervention or input:** The utility placed a 409 MW, approximately 900 MWh battery in service in December 2021 near solar and transmission assets.
3. **Constraint:** Conversion losses, duration, system conditions, cycling, degradation, and rate recovery shape operation.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** PSC filings state uses for high load, reserves, transmission constraints, frequency response, and off-peak or solar charging.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** The utility supplies the operating descriptions inside Florida PSC proceedings.

**Independent validation:** The regulator docket validates the filing and asset characteristics, but not realized dispatch frequency, value, reliability effect, or cost effectiveness.
