## What Changed

NIST's project page identifies an active 5G Open Testbed, open-source automation software for O-RAN testbed deployment, and multiple 2025 change requests accepted or incorporated into O-RAN test and security specifications. The software page now identifies version 1.7 and documents multi-user-equipment connections, split CU-DU configurations, handover, slicing, metrics, and visualization features.

## Evidence Stage

This record supports `versioned test software + laboratory infrastructure + accepted standards contributions`. It does not support `completed 6G standard`, `certified interoperable system`, `commercial 6G network`, or `federal deployment`.

## Why It Matters

Next-generation wireless policy often arrives years before concrete artifacts. Here, the artifacts are visible: code, a testbed, change requests, incorporated specification text, and a named standards forum.

## What To Watch Next

Track new software releases, published test results, multi-vendor interoperability, certification and badging, accepted 3GPP or O-RAN specifications, post-quantum controls, spectrum decisions, federal pilots, and commercial deployments.
