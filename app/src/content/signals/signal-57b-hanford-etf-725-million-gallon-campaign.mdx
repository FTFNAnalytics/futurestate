## Phase 57B panel

DOE reports that the Effluent Treatment Facility processed more than 7.25 million gallons of contaminated wastewater over six months and lowered one storage-basin level by almost 17 feet.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Observed operating output.** One six-month ETF campaign, measured as treated wastewater volume and the separate level change in one named storage basin.

## Evidence boundaries

- Treated volume is not contaminant mass removed or restored environmental condition.
- The basin-level change is not a sitewide inventory or capacity measure.
- No influent and effluent concentration, quality yield, downtime, energy, unit cost, or independent validation is disclosed.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
