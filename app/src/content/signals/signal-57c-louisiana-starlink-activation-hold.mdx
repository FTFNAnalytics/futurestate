## Phase 57C panel

The signed Starlink BEAD agreement covers 10,635 locations, but no compatible activation, installation, subscriber, capacity-reservation, test, affordability, adoption, complaint, or closeout result is public in the reviewed record.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Adoption and activation hold.** The same 10,635-location agreement cohort across more than 50 parishes, kept separate from activated, installed, subscribed, tested, retained, or closed-out locations.

## Evidence boundaries

- Agreement execution is not service activation.
- A delivery schedule is not an accepted result.
- No subscriber, capacity, performance, affordability, adoption, complaint, or closeout denominator is disclosed.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen with location-level activation, installation, subscribers, capacity reservation, tests, latency, uptime, price, adoption, complaints, and accepted closeout.
