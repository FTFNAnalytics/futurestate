## What Changed

The Arizona Corporation Commission's February 4, 2026 open-meeting summary records a unanimous vote approving a Certificate of Environmental Compatibility for Baccara Power, LLC in docket `L-21369A-25-0222-00253`.

The proposed Project Baccara configuration includes two data-center buildings and approximately 700 MW of on-site natural-gas generation in western Maricopa County. The Commission says the generation would serve the data center and support the regional grid.

The same record relays the developer's plan to use chilled rather than evaporative cooling and to use wastewater from a nearby brewery in a reclaimed-water system. It also says Maricopa County and Luke Air Force Base approvals remained pending.

## Why It Matters

This record shows a specific local conversion stack: a large compute load, dedicated generation, a state environmental-compatibility decision, cooling and reuse plans, and a set of remaining air, land-use, construction, and military-compatibility gates.

## Evidence Limits

The ACC explicitly distinguishes its CEC jurisdiction from approval of the data center itself. The CEC does not prove that the generation plant, interconnection, data-center buildings, or water infrastructure have been financed, permitted at every level, constructed, commissioned, or operated.

The cooling and wastewater statements are developer plans relayed in an official Commission summary. They are not measured water withdrawals, reuse volumes, discharge records, completed infrastructure, or proof of near-zero groundwater consumption.
