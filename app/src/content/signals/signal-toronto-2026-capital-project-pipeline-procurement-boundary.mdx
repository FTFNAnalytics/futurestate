## What changed

Toronto first published and refreshed its capital-project pipeline on May 29, 2026. The dataset covers preliminary City-led projects from engineering and construction, Toronto Water, transportation, and solid-waste divisions that may enter competitive procurement.

## Why it matters

The pipeline creates an upstream record before a solicitation exists. It can help FTFN connect capital planning to approved budgets, procurement, contracts, construction, and infrastructure delivery without waiting for a single project page to tell the whole story.

## Evidence limits

The City states that inclusion does not guarantee a solicitation. Scope, timing, budget, and procurement method can change. A pipeline entry is not an award, start, completion, available capacity, or service commitment.

## What to watch next

Select named projects relevant to development servicing, preserve identifiers and refresh dates, then connect approved budgets, solicitations, addenda, bids, awards, council authorities, construction records, payments, change orders, acceptance, and operating outcomes.
