## Phase 57B panel

GAO reports that, as of April 2026, NNSA had not developed a lifecycle cost estimate aligned with GAO best practices and forecast a December 2026 estimate after SRPPF design work.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Rate, capacity, and baseline hold.** The enterprise program to establish and sustain pit production across Los Alamos and Savannah River, evaluated against GAO schedule and lifecycle-cost best practices.

## Evidence boundaries

- A forecast December estimate is not a completed or GAO-accepted baseline.
- The recommendation status does not measure qualified output or operating rate.
- The record does not support schedule confidence, total cost, cost outcome, capacity acceptance, or readiness.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen when NNSA publishes the integrated master schedule and lifecycle cost estimate, GAO records sufficiency, and recurring accepted output is reported under a stable site and period denominator.

Authorization, construction, turnover, final completion, service availability, subscriber use, tested performance, accepted operation, observed output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate.
