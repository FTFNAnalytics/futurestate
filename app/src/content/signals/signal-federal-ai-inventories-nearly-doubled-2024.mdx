## Evidence Stage

GAO found reported AI use cases across 11 selected agencies increased from 571 in 2023 to 1,110 in 2024, while generative-AI cases increased from 32 to 282.

## Boundary

Publish as a disclosed-inventory trend. Do not translate use-case counts into productivity, mission benefit, or safety.

## What To Watch Next

Track agency-level operational maturity, usage, impact assessments, incidents, corrective actions, and measured benefits.
