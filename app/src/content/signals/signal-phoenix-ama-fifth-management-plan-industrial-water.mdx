## What changed

The fifth plan's conservation requirements became effective January 1, 2025. Its industrial chapter includes large-scale power plants, large-scale cooling facilities, mining, and new large industrial users, while the implementation chapter covers measurement, records, annual reports, audits, inspections, variances, administrative review, and compliance.

## Evidence stage

This supports `effective area-wide conservation and compliance requirements`. It does not support `named-project allocation`, `utility capacity`, `service commitment`, `actual use`, `facility compliance`, `infrastructure acceptance`, or `operation`.
