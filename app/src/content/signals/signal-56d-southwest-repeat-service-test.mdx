## Repeat-outcome and alternative-explanation test

The repeat record tests several alternatives simultaneously but cannot isolate schedule, staffing, weather, air-traffic, or recovery effects.

## Tested alternatives

- Weather and airport exposure
- Schedule and network structure
- Maintenance and staffing
- Air traffic constraints
- Recovery decisions

## Later observations

1. December 2025: 118,075 records, 74.84% on time, 0.55% cancelled, with 7.60% air-carrier, 0.18% extreme-weather, 4.48% national-system, and 12.06% late-aircraft delay.
2. May 2026: 120,646 records, 71.88% on time, 0.39% cancelled, with 8.93% air-carrier, 0.37% extreme-weather, 4.82% national-system, and 13.25% late-aircraft delay; DOT separately recorded 224 complaints.

## Compatibility and attribution

**Compatibility:** Same reporting operating carrier and monthly delay table; complaint counts use a separate denominator.

**Attribution:** DOT publishes carrier-reported operations and separately received complaint cases.

**Validation status:** Official common-table observations; causal attribution and commitment delivery remain unverified.
