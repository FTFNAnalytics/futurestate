**Named-asset registry.** All 30 named train-access cohort memberships in Appendix B, including the 12 rows not reported Complete in the historical snapshot.

Structured registry: `phase-57g-amtrak-named-station-registry.json`.

Historical and current revisions, site and facility, project and subproject, BSL and CAI, feed and glass, container stages, capacity and qualified output, agency assertions and independent acceptance remain separate. No record supports a ranking, composite, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or unsupported causal attribution.
