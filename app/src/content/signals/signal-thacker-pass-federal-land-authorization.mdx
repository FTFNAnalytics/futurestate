## Evidence Stage

The ROD establishes the federal land-decision stage for a named mine and processing project. It does not establish condition satisfaction, construction, production, compliance, or customer acceptance.

## What To Watch Next

Follow state permits, water rights, financing, construction, inspections, commissioning, output, qualification, and operating results.
