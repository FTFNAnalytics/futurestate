## Phase 56Z panel

FRA obligations rose from $35.476 billion to $41.516 billion and outlays from $2.589 billion to $7.279 billion, but the obligation share moved from 67.2 to 63.0 percent as adjusted authority rose from $52.795 billion to $65.856 billion.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Repeat funding measurement.** FRA formula-plus-discretionary IIJA funding across two DOT statements with a changing authority base.

## Evidence boundaries

- The 4.2-point obligation-share decline is a denominator effect and is not evidence that obligations were reversed.
- The 6.2-point outlay-share increase remains a payment measure.
- No project completion, service, capacity, safety, or passenger outcome is established.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Follow stable FRA program cohorts from obligation and payment into construction, acceptance, service, and outcomes.

## Authority boundary

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
