## Phase 56Z panel

DOE reported more than 279,000 gallons treated by January 27, 2026 and nearly 400,000 gallons by June 30, while the state-authorized storage system provided 84 vaults for 1,344 canisters.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted cleanup operation.** Cumulative IWTU sodium-bearing waste treated since radiological operations began in April 2023, paired with separately authorized interim storage capacity.

## Evidence boundaries

- More-than and nearly values do not support an exact five-month delta.
- Treatment volume and storage capacity are separate denominators.
- The approximately 900,000-gallon design mission and final tank closure remain incomplete.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Add exact cumulative and period throughput, downtime, canisters generated and stored, residual inventory, compliance, permanent disposal, and tank closure.

## Authority boundary

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
