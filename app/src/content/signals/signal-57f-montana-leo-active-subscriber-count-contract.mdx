## Phase 57F panel

The LEO instructions require subgrantees to report the total number of active subscribers as of each quarterly reporting period.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Observed adoption measurement contract.** The exact awardee project and quarter, with active subscribers measured against the approved covered-location cohort.

## Evidence boundaries

- Active subscribers alone do not establish installations, price, retention duration, complaints, or acceptance.
- Public reporting must protect subscriber privacy while retaining a stable denominator.
