## Longitudinal read

The FY 2021 through FY 2023 FISMA reports form a recurring federal cybersecurity series. The FY 2023 report says 96 percent of civilian executive-branch agencies increased their Detect-category score from FY 2022, while the reporting program continued shifting toward automated, outcome-focused measures.

## Comparison boundary

Publish as an OMB reporting-series result. Changes in metrics, automation, agency scope, and scoring prevent a simple multi-year incident or effectiveness trend.

## What to watch next

Track stable measures for detection coverage, event logging, remediation time, incidents, recovery, affected people, and reporting-method changes.
