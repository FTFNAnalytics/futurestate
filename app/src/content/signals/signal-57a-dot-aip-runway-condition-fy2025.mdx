## Phase 57A panel

DOT reports 97.3 percent of Airport Improvement Program-eligible paved runways in fair or better condition in FY 2025, above the 93 percent target.

**Fixed-cohort system outcome.** Airport Improvement Program-eligible paved runways evaluated under FAA's runway-condition measure.
