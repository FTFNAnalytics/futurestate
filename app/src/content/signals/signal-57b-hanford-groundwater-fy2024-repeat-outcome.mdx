## Phase 57B panel

DOE reports 2.3 billion gallons treated in FY 2024 and ten consecutive fiscal years above 2 billion gallons, while Washington Ecology later reports more than 38 billion gallons cumulatively across six facilities.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Observed operating output.** FY 2024 sitewide treated groundwater volume across Hanford's six treatment systems, plus a ten-year threshold series; the later regulator cumulative total is a separate denominator.

## Evidence boundaries

- A threshold series does not disclose the exact annual value for every year.
- Treated water volume is not annual contaminant mass removed, plume reduction, or aquifer restoration.
- The 200 West capacity expansion is capability and cannot be counted as realized treatment beyond the reported 2.3 billion gallons.
