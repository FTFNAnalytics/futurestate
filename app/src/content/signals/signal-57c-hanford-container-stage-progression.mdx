## Phase 57C panel

DOE reported more than 20 immobilized-waste containers in December 2025, Washington Ecology reported 34 by February 2026, and May project minutes reported 66 shipped to the Integrated Disposal Facility.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Repeat operating output.** Three dated container observations under distinct produced, regulator-observed, and shipped stages; the counts are not treated as one accepted or disposed series.

## Evidence boundaries

- Produced, filled, shipped, accepted, and disposed containers are distinct stages.
- The more-than-20 and approximately-34 observations are not exact compatible counts.
- Rejects, rework, quality, acceptance, downtime, cost, and final disposal counts are not reported together.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Publish a monthly stage ledger for produced, filled, quality-released, shipped, accepted, disposed, rejected, and reworked containers.

## Authority boundary

Planning, design, construction, turnover, service inventory, operating availability, use, adoption, test performance, accepted operation, repeat output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, project baseline, program baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or causal attribution.
