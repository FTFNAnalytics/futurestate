## Phase 56W result

GAO reports a plan to grout about 24 million gallons of low-activity waste, a $480 million to $1.1 billion estimate that excludes transportation and disposal, a December 2025 solicitation, proposals under review in April 2026, and an award planned later in 2026.

## Material narrowing

The report adds a cost-and-procurement record for a distinct Hanford waste stream while preventing low-activity-waste planning from being used as evidence about the high-level-waste recommendation target.

## Evidence boundaries

- Low-activity waste is not high-level waste.
- The estimate excludes transportation and disposal.
- A planned contract award is not an awarded, operating, or outcome-producing system.
