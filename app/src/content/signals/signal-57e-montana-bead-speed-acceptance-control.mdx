## Phase 57E panel

ConnectMT states that 80 percent of download and upload measurements must reach at least 80 percent of the required speed tier, with upload and download assessed separately.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Adoption and retention acceptance control.** The tested active-subscriber sample for each speed tier and technology type under the BEAD performance-monitoring method.

## Evidence boundaries

- The threshold is an acceptance control, not a published awardee result.
- No project-specific sample, measurement distribution, failure count, or remediation result is public.
- Passing speed does not establish adoption, retention, affordability, complaints, or availability.
