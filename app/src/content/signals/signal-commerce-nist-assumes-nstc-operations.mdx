## What changed

Commerce announced that NIST would take operational responsibility for the NSTC. The same release said a prior agreement granting Natcast up to $7.4 billion was invalid and would not be followed by the Department.

## Evidence stage

This supports `operator change + department funding position`. It does not support `every award canceled`, `facility canceled`, `funds recovered`, or `replacement operating structure complete`.

## Why it matters

The January 2025 NAPMP release presented a four-part $1.4 billion award stack, including a $1.1 billion Natcast component. The later Commerce action means that stack must be followed recipient by recipient instead of treated as one uncomplicated final-award milestone.

## What to watch next

Track NIST or Commerce records identifying current operators, valid agreements, payment status, facility contracts, equipment procurement, staffing, site activity, researcher access, and the disposition of each named award.
