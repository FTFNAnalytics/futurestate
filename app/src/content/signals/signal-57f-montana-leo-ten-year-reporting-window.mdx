## Phase 57F panel

ConnectMT requires LEO subgrantees to file quarterly reports from grant execution through ten years after certifying service availability to every covered location.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Observed adoption measurement contract.** Each LEO award's exact covered-location cohort and quarterly reporting periods through the ten-year post-availability window.

## Evidence boundaries

- A reporting duration is not retention performance.
- The public instructions contain no awardee submission or subscriber outcome.
- A universal-availability certification is not universal adoption.
