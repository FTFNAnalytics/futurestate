## Longitudinal read

NASA's 2022, 2023, and 2024 public AI-use records provide recurring visibility into agency applications and governance. They establish disclosure continuity, but the inventories do not provide one stable denominator for realized mission benefit, reliability, incidents, or cost.

## Comparison boundary

Publish the continuity of annual disclosure. Do not describe inventory entries as deployed systems or convert named use cases into an adoption, productivity, or safety trend.

## What to watch next

Track stable inclusion rules, maturity, operational usage, accuracy, availability, human override, incidents, impact assessments, cost, and mission outcomes.
