## Phase 57A panel

Washington Ecology reports that six Hanford groundwater pump-and-treat facilities have treated more than 38 billion gallons; the largest system can treat up to 2,500 gallons per minute.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Independent operating acceptance.** Cumulative gallons treated across six Hanford pump-and-treat facilities since operations began in the 1990s; capacity for the largest 200 West system is a separate denominator.

## Evidence boundaries

- Cumulative treated volume is not contaminant mass removed or aquifer restoration.
- The period spans multiple facilities, methods, contaminants, and decades.
- The planned 3,750-gallon-per-minute expansion is not treated as realized capacity.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.
