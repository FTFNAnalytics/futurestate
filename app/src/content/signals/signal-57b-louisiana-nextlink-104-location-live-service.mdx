## Phase 57B panel

ConnectLA reports that Nextlink activated a BEAD-funded fixed-wireless tower on May 1, 2026 and made gigabit-speed service available to 104 BEAD locations in Bossier Parish.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted-service cohort.** One tower in southern Bienville Parish and the 104 BEAD locations in Bossier Parish stated as receiving service availability, within a larger 7,460-location Nextlink subgrant.

## Evidence boundaries

- Service availability is not an active-subscriber count or adoption rate.
- The release provides no disclosed location list, test sample, measured speed, latency, uptime, affordability, or independent acceptance result.
- One 104-location cohort does not close the earlier nearly 5,000-location Louisiana authorization cohort.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track the same 104 locations through disclosed availability, requests, installations, subscribers, tests, latency, uptime, affordability, adoption, and closeout.

Authorization, construction, turnover, final completion, service availability, subscriber use, tested performance, accepted operation, observed output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate.
