## Phase 56X result

DOE reports the first transfer of Effluent Management Facility concentrate to a local offsite grouting facility after a regulatory permit was obtained. EMF concentrate is a secondary liquid byproduct of vitrification, and the grouted material is then to be shipped out of Washington for commercial disposal.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Operating output.** First reported EMF-concentrate transfer in July 2026; DOE states that one to three gallons of secondary material can be created per gallon vitrified but does not publish the transfer volume.

## Evidence boundaries

- EMF concentrate is not the 22-tank low-activity-waste stream in GAO-26-108878.
- A first transfer does not establish a stable operating rate, full lifecycle cost, or completed disposal.
- DOE's statement that the approach could increase treatment capacity is a projected effect, not a realized measured outcome.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track transferred and disposed volume, destination, acceptance, cost, schedule, operating cadence, and any verified effect on vitrification throughput.
