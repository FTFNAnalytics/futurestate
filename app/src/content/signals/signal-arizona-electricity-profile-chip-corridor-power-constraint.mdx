## What Changed

The Arizona electricity profile adds an official state-level electricity baseline to the chip-corridor evidence stack. It gives FTFN a stronger source anchor for power-system questions without claiming site-level capacity.

## Why It Matters

The U.S. Southwest chip corridor is not only a semiconductor story. It is also a grid, utility, interconnection, rate, and local infrastructure story.

## Dependency Stack

- State electricity baseline data
- Utility planning and regulation
- Facility-level load estimates
- Interconnection timelines
- Tariffs and rate-case context
- Local permitting and land use

## Evidence Limits

This signal does not show whether a specific fab, supplier, or data center can secure enough power. State-level EIA data must be paired with utility and project-level evidence before local conclusions are defensible.

## What To Watch Next

Watch Arizona utility planning, interconnection, tariff, annual-report, and rate-case materials alongside semiconductor facility milestones.
