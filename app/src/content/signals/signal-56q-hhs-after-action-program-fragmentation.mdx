## Resolution

Held — one-to-many. The candidates are GAO-24-106276 Recommendation 1: Coordinate after-action work across HHS component agencies and integrate existing component programs. | GAO-24-106276 Recommendation 2: Include relevant external stakeholders in each emergency-response after-action process..

## Agency response

HHS developed standard operating procedures. GAO is awaiting evidence from an exercise or response after-action report before determining whether either recommendation is implemented.

## Evidence boundary

An agency response is not implementation. GAO recommendation status is not the Phase 56F evidence state, and recommendation closure would not by itself establish operating performance or outcome.
