## Phase 56X result

GAO reports that the LAP4 30 Base Equipment Installation and Decontamination and Decommissioning projects were replanning baselines under the 30 Diamond strategy, while the definition-phase 30 Reliable project had a 92 percent increase in scoped equipment relative to preliminary estimates. GAO's June 2025 table lists 30 Base at a $1.864 billion baseline and August 2030 completion date, while the definition-phase 30 Reliable current cost and completion date remained to be determined.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Project baseline.** Named LAP4 subprojects, project phase, approved baseline or preliminary range, and status generally as of June 2025.

## Evidence boundaries

- Scope transfers make parent-project and subproject trend lines non-equivalent without change-control history.
- An approved project baseline is not production capability or project completion.
- The series is not the complete multi-site pit-production capability life-cycle estimate.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track each LAP4 subproject's approved scope, baseline revision, current estimate, completion, operational acceptance, and production-capability contribution.
