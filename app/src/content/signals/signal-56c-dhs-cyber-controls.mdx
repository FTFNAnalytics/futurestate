## Entity dossier read

DHS's FY 2024 FISMA evaluation extends the panel with an effective result. GAO's 2025 review separately identifies CDM guidance, data-quality, endpoint, and cloud-asset gaps; the two records describe coexisting control conditions, not cause and effect.

## Temporal order

1. **Baseline:** DHS moved from ineffective in FY 2021 to effective in FY 2022 and FY 2023.
2. **Named intervention or input:** DHS and CISA continued operating the government-wide CDM program for asset visibility and network monitoring.
3. **Constraint:** GAO identified incomplete guidance, data-quality controls, endpoint onboarding, and cloud-asset management.
4. **Observed outcome or later boundary:** DHS OIG rated the FY 2024 department program effective, with function ratings of Level 4 or Level 5.

## Attribution and validation

**Attribution:** DHS OIG reports the department result; GAO independently reports CDM implementation constraints.

**Independent validation:** Two independent oversight bodies support the coexistence of the records, not a causal relationship.

## Alternative explanations

- Selected component and system scope
- Annual FISMA metric changes
- Controls outside CDM
- Component-level variation

## What to watch next

Track FY 2025 department and component ratings, recommendation closure evidence, CDM data quality, endpoint coverage, cloud assets, incidents, recovery, and service effects.
