## Current state

NCA5 Chapter 13 treats transportation climate risk as a system problem spanning emissions, extreme-weather exposure, aging assets, cyber-technology integration, workforce capacity, and planning practice.

## Evidence boundary

The assessment supports a national current-state and dependency frame. It does not prove the condition, adaptation plan, funding, construction, or performance of a named road, rail line, airport, port, fleet, or local network.

## Next records

Track asset inventories, regional hazard studies, engineering criteria, resilience plans, funded projects, construction milestones, maintenance changes, service disruptions, and measured performance.
