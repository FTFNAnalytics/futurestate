## Phase 57A panel

The W87-1 first production unit is qualified and accepted, while GAO's recommendation for a comprehensive lifecycle cost estimate aligned with best practices remains open and the enterprise schedule remains incomplete.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Program baseline hold.** The enterprise capability to establish and sustain at least 80 pits per year across Los Alamos and Savannah River, kept separate from the single accepted W87-1 unit.

## Evidence boundaries

- A qualified FPU is not recurring output or enterprise capacity.
- An agency forecast for a later cost estimate is not a completed GAO-accepted baseline.
- The record does not support a completion rate, schedule confidence, cost outcome, or readiness score.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen when NNSA publishes a comprehensive integrated schedule and lifecycle cost estimate, GAO records its sufficiency, and recurring accepted output is reported under a stable period and site denominator.

## Authority boundary

Target attainment, authorization, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, qualified output, recurring rate, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or causal attribution.
