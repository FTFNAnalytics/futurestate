## What Changed

Joby announced in March 2026 that the first aircraft it describes as FAA-conforming had flown and entered a certification test campaign. The company says the aircraft was built for Type Inspection Authorization work and that FAA pilot testing was expected later in 2026.

## Why It Matters

Moving from a general demonstration to an aircraft intended for certification testing is a meaningful program milestone. It narrows the evidence question from whether an aircraft can fly to whether its conformity, test results, certification basis, production system, operating approvals, and service infrastructure can satisfy regulators and customers.

## Evidence Limits

The selected milestone is reported by Joby, an interested party. It does not prove FAA type certification, production certification, operating approval, commercial service, route viability, vertiport readiness, insurance availability, weather reliability, or unit economics.

## What To Watch Next

Watch FAA type-certificate and Type Inspection Authorization records, regulator-flown testing, production approvals, operating certificates, vertiport permits, route announcements, and independent operational evidence.
