## Phase 57A panel

DOT reports 96 percent of National Highway System bridge-deck area in fair or better condition in FY 2025, above the 95 percent target.

**Fixed-cohort system outcome.** National Highway System bridge-deck area classified under the federal bridge-condition method.

- The national measure does not attribute condition to one funding program.
