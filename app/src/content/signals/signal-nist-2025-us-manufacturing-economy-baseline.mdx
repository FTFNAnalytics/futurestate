## Current state

The NIST report provides a current statistical frame for comparing the U.S. manufacturing economy with other countries and reviewing domestic trends.

## Evidence boundary

National statistics do not prove the capacity, productivity, technology adoption, workforce, supplier depth, or competitiveness of a named facility or region.

## Next records

Track establishment data, production, investment, equipment, technology use, suppliers, workforce, wages, exports, customers, utilities, permits, and operating outcomes.
