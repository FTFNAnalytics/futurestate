## What changed

Phoenix's preliminary 2026-31 Capital Improvement Program includes project WS90320008, described as design and construction of a new North Gateway Water Reclamation Plant near Interstate 17 and Dixileta Drive. The preliminary schedule shows $17,845,489 in 2026-27 across identified funding sources.

## Why it matters

The record turns a broad wastewater-infrastructure need into a named, traceable project. FTFN can now follow the same code through budget adoption, design, environmental review, procurement, contract award, construction, acceptance, commissioning, and operation.

## Evidence limits

This is a preliminary capital-program record. It is not an adopted appropriation, procurement, award, expenditure, construction milestone, completed asset, treatment-capacity statement, customer service agreement, or operating result.

## What to watch next

Watch the adopted CIP, OpenGov solicitations, contract and council records, design and permit files, environmental review, construction schedules, payment and change-order records, acceptance, operating permits, treatment volumes, service-area records, and customer agreements.
