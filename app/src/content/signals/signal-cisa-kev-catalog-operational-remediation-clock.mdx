## What Changed

CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog gives FTFN a better cybersecurity operating rail than undifferentiated vulnerability volume. The catalog is useful because it focuses on vulnerabilities with known exploitation evidence and gives each entry operational action context.

## Why It Matters

Cybersecurity signals become decision-useful when they help teams prioritize what must be fixed first. A known-exploited vulnerability is not just another database row; it can trigger remediation work, patch-window pressure, vendor review, risk acceptance, and executive reporting.

## Dependency Stack

- Vulnerability disclosure and CVE metadata
- Evidence of active exploitation
- CISA catalog inclusion criteria
- Vendor mitigation guidance
- Asset exposure and patch status
- Operational remediation ownership

## Evidence Limits

This signal does not show that a specific company, city, utility, hospital, school district, or infrastructure operator is exposed. FTFN should pair KEV entries with asset inventories, vendor advisories, exploitability context, and local remediation evidence before drawing institution-specific conclusions.
