## Phase 56Z panel

DOE reports completed modifications to all 2,262 original positions in Glass Waste Storage Building 1, enabling interim storage of 4,524 vitrified high-level-waste canisters.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Accepted cleanup operation.** All 2,262 original positions in GWSB 1 modified for two-canister stacking, yielding 4,524 interim positions.

## Evidence boundaries

- Completed storage modification is not use of every position.
- Interim storage is not final disposal in a federal repository.
- DOE's avoided-building savings estimate is not treated as independently audited realized savings.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Track accepted use, canisters stored, GWSB 2 modification, remaining mission demand, capacity margin, and final disposal.
