## Phase 56Z panel

DOE's 2024 record reports more than 15 million gallons processed since 2008 across three facilities and about 7.5 million by SWPF in its first three years; the 2026 record reports more than 10.6 million gallons processed by SWPF since 2022.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Comparison hold.** Two overlapping but non-identical SRS salt-waste reporting universes and start dates.

## Evidence boundaries

- The all-facility total cannot be compared as an SWPF-only series.
- SWPF's first-three-years period and since-2022 period are not identical windows.
- The record remains In Review until compatible periodic or cumulative measures are available.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Recover SWPF-only cumulative observations with the same start date, method, revision history, downtime, and residual-inventory denominator.

## Authority boundary

Selection, agreement, obligation, payment, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation, output, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. Repeated agency reporting does not establish independent causal attribution or a cross-entity ranking.
