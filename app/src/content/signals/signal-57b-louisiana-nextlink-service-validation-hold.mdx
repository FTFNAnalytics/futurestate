## Phase 57B panel

The Nextlink tower establishes live service availability for 104 locations, but the state release does not publish the subscriber, installation, performance-test, uptime, adoption, affordability, or closeout results needed for a validated service outcome.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Closeout or performance hold.** The same 104-location Bossier Parish availability cohort, with required outcome fields treated as missing rather than inferred.

## Evidence boundaries

- A live tower does not prove every listed location requested or received service.
- Advertised gigabit service is not a disclosed test-sample distribution.
- No adoption, affordability, reliability, customer-experience, or program-closeout result is public in this record.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reopen with a stable served-location list, subscriber denominator, installation count, disclosed test sample, speed, latency, uptime, price, adoption, complaints, and accepted closeout.

## Authority boundary

Authorization, construction, turnover, final completion, service availability, subscriber use, tested performance, accepted operation, observed output, recurring rate, analytical capacity, baseline, realized outcome, closeout, implementation, and closure remain separate. No record supports a cross-system ranking, composite score, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or causal attribution.
