## Phase 57A panel

DOT reports 99.4 percent commercial availability for the U.S. portion of the St. Lawrence Seaway in FY 2025, above the 99.0 percent target.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Fixed-cohort system outcome.** Commercially scheduled operating time for the U.S. Seaway system under DOT's availability method; the FY 2021 through FY 2025 series is 99.6, 99.6, 98.7, 99.0, and 99.4 percent.

## Evidence boundaries

- Availability is not cargo throughput, wait time, safety, revenue, or asset condition.
- The five-point series does not identify the cause of annual movement.
- U.S. system availability is not the same as end-to-end Great Lakes–Seaway performance.
