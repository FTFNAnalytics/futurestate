## Current state

NASA has made multiple layers of the lunar communications and navigation design inspectable through public technical documents.

## Evidence boundary

Reference designs and specifications do not prove provider awards, deployed relays, validated coverage, availability, cybersecurity, mission use, or operating performance.

## Next records

Track specification versions, procurement, awards, provider systems, launch, terminal integration, demonstrations, validation, mission use, uptime, and service performance.
