**Highest-value record:** Final regulator investigation and corrective-action closure

**Status:** Partially Closed

**Reopen when:** Reopen when CPUC publishes the formal investigation or audit report, facility response, or closure notice.

### Manatee Solar Energy Center

**Highest-value record:** Interval dispatch and availability for the named battery

**Status:** Open

No compatible public interval dispatch or availability series was identified; another planning or capacity record would not close the gap.

**Reopen when:** Reopen when FPL, Florida PSC, EIA, or a balancing authority publishes asset-specific operating data with a declared interval.

### Gateway Energy Storage System

**Status:** Open

**Reopen when:** Reopen when CPUC, EPA, CAISO, SCE, or the operator publishes final findings or a full-capacity operating record.

**Status:** Open

**Reopen when:** Reopen if ESV publishes a later enforcement or incident record, or AEMO publishes an asset-specific operating series.
