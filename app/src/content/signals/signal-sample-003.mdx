## What Changed

The Department of Commerce announced a definitive CHIPS for America agreement with SandboxAQ in June 2026. NIST describes up to $500 million for an AI-driven materials-discovery effort spanning simulation, candidate identification, and laboratory validation.

## Why It Matters

The agreement broadens the CHIPS evidence stack beyond fab construction. Materials discovery can affect semiconductor manufacturing, battery chemistries, catalysts, critical-mineral substitution, and other advanced-manufacturing pathways if candidates survive testing, qualification, cost, supply-chain, and scale-up gates.

## Dependency Stack

- Definitive federal agreement and milestones
- Simulation and candidate discovery
- Laboratory synthesis and validation
- Industry qualification
- Manufacturing scale-up
- Cost, supply-chain, and customer evidence

## What To Watch Next

Watch for milestone disclosures, validated material candidates, peer-reviewed or test evidence, industry qualification, manufacturing partners, and any named facility or production record.
