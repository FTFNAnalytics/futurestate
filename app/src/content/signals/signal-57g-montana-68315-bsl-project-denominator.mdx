## Phase 57G registry panel

FTFN reconciled all 68,315 Final Proposal BSL location rows to the 32 project IDs and publishes project-level totals plus raw classification and technology-code distributions without exposing individual BSL identifiers.

## Evidence stage and denominator

**Project-cohort registry.** Exactly 68,315 location-file rows; raw classification codes 0 and 1 total 45,401 and 22,914, and raw technology codes total the same 68,315 rows.

Structured registry: `phase-57g-montana-project-cohort-registry.json`.

## Evidence boundaries

- Raw codes remain untranslated until an official schema is joined.
- Location assignment does not establish serviceability or subscription.
- Individual BSL identifiers and sensitive location details are excluded from FTFN public output.

Exact target artifact acquired: **No**. FTFN submitted no agency contact or FOIA request.

Next action: Reconcile future served-location and quarterly files to project-level eligible, serviceable, installed, subscribed, tested, retained, and accepted counts.

## Authority boundary

A registry row is an identity, lineage, cohort, stage, or work-breakdown object—not reliability, adoption, yield, readiness, output, implementation, or closure. Historical and current revisions, site and facility, project and subproject, BSL and CAI, feed and glass, container stages, capacity and qualified output, agency assertions and independent acceptance remain separate. No record supports a ranking, composite, readiness score, generalized savings claim, or unsupported causal attribution.
