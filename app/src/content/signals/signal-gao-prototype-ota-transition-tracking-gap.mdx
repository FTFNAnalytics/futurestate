## What changed

GAO found that DOD systematically tracked production OTAs but lacked a reliable method to identify FAR production contracts that resulted from prototype OTAs. GAO said the gap prevented the Department from assessing the extent to which prototype OTAs delivered capabilities.

## Evidence stage

This supports `verified oversight finding + agreed recommendations`. It does not establish the result of any individual agreement outside GAO's selected sample.

## Why it matters

The OpenAI prototype notice is a valid award record, but neither its ceiling nor its initial obligation establishes prototype delivery. The GAO report supplies the correct system-level warning: the transition from prototype to capability must be proven with agreement-specific records.

## What to watch next

Track DOD implementation of GAO's recommendations, improved contract linkage, later obligations, delivered prototypes, evaluation, acceptance, fielding, follow-on awards, and production.
