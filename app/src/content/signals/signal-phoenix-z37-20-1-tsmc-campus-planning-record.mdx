## What Changed

FTFN selected City of Phoenix case `Z-37-20-1`, the North Phoenix 3,500 Planned Unit Development, as the first named planning record tied to the TSMC campus area.

The January 2026 narrative records City Council adoption on October 21, 2020 and approved minor amendments dated December 11, 2024 and December 16, 2025. It describes the Technology Campus district, infrastructure-planning requirements, and the first TSMC fab phase within the larger planning area.

## Why It Matters

The record creates a traceable municipal entitlement layer between a company investment announcement and construction. It also makes the sequencing visible: a PUD can establish land uses and development standards while later plans, permits, infrastructure work, inspections, and occupancy remain separate evidence gates.

## Dependency Stack

- The adopted PUD and amendments define the planning envelope
- Later phases require the applicable development master planning
- Site, civil, drainage, water, wastewater, and transportation plans require review
- Grading and building permits authorize specific work
- Inspections test compliance during construction
- Certificates of occupancy establish a later operating milestone

## Evidence Limits

The narrative is City-hosted but includes applicant-prepared planning material. It records an entitlement framework and approved amendments; it is not a complete City permit or inspection history.

It does not prove that future fabs are funded, fully permitted, connected to utilities, under construction, complete, or occupied.

## What To Watch Next

Track later PUD amendments, final site plans, named civil and building permits, inspection results, certificates of occupancy, and project-specific utility-service records.
