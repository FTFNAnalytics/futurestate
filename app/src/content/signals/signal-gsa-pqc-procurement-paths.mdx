## What Changed

GSA's June 2025 buyer's guide translates post-quantum migration into acquisition and implementation work. It covers cryptographic inventory, environment assessment, transition planning, implementation, maintenance, training, and example use cases, then identifies acquisition support and contract vehicles agencies can use.

## Evidence Stage

This supports `federal procurement guidance and available acquisition paths`.

It does not support `agency inventory completed`, `solicitation issued`, `order awarded`, `validated product accepted`, `pilot completed`, `production system migrated`, `legacy cryptography retired`, or `operational risk reduced`.

## Why It Matters

The record closes a missing bridge in the policy-to-implementation pathway. Agencies do not have to invent the acquisition categories from scratch, but FTFN still needs institution-level evidence showing that an agency used those paths and moved a system through testing, acceptance, deployment, and retirement.

## What To Watch Next

Track agency solicitations and task orders, cryptographic inventory results, migration budgets, vendor roadmaps, Cryptographic Module Validation Program records, interoperability tests, production deployments, maintenance evidence, and retirement of quantum-vulnerable algorithms.
