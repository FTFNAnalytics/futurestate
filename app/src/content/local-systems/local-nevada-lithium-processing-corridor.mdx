## Named Projects And Records

- Thacker Pass has a federal ROD, named state permit stack, and federal loan close. Those are three distinct stages, not an operating result.
- Rhyolite Ridge has a federal land decision and a DOE loan-related NEPA decision. The latter does not establish financial close.
- Nevada's water-rights system is the required bridge from general aridity to project-specific allocation, pumping, monitoring, and impact evidence.

## Evidence Limits

The dossier does not translate estimated resources into reserves, permits into compliant operation, financial close into completed construction, or projected capacity into qualified sales. Each project must be followed through conditions, water, construction, testing, commissioning, customer qualification, production, compliance, and community outcomes.
