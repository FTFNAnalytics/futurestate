## Named Projects And Records

- Launch Complex 46 has a displayed FAA site-license term through 2030, but a site license does not authorize each mission.
- The Shuttle Landing Facility pages still display a January 2026 expiration date; current status requires a formal FAA record.
- The SLC-40 final environmental review establishes one federal decision layer without substituting for licenses, construction, mitigation, or missions.
- NASA's master plan and programmatic environmental review define system envelopes rather than a completed capital program.

## Evidence Limits

The dossier does not infer a mission license from a site license, built capacity from a master plan, project delivery from a funding program, or resilience from an operating history. Reliable corridor claims need named capital, environmental, license, construction, acceptance, mission, safety, utilization, workforce, utility, and local-outcome records.

## What To Watch Next

- Formal FAA disposition of the Shuttle Landing Facility site license.
- Operator-specific Part 450 licenses and modifications tied to Space Coast missions.
- FDOT and Space Florida project awards, agreements, procurement, construction, acceptance, and utilization.
- KSC master-plan projects and tiered environmental decisions.
- Range capacity, delays, safety events, coastal resilience, utility, workforce, and housing outcomes.
