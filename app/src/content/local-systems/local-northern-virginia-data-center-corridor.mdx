## Named Projects And Records

- The JLARC report supplies the corridor-wide baseline but cannot answer whether a particular site can be served.
- The SCC's GS-5 decision changes the risk and cost framework for large future loads; it is not a service commitment.
- Golden-Mars is a named transmission project with a conditional route trail that can be followed from approval into land, build, energization, and service.
- Loudoun's Phase 2 standards project is an unresolved policy gate with a dated post-September 2026 review.
- Virginia DEQ's registry makes facility and generator permits traceable without treating permits as operating evidence.
- IAD-215, VA10, and permit 74262-4 are named 2026 air-permit trails for later construction, testing, and compliance checks.
- Project Raspberry is retained only as a Virginia multi-permit comparator; it is outside the corridor geography and remains in application review.

## Evidence Limits

The dossier does not infer spare capacity from an operating cluster, project service from a regional forecast, construction from an approved route, operation from an air permit, or water availability from a program page. Corridor readiness requires project-specific agreements, permits, construction, acceptance, energization, metering, compliance, and outcome records.
