The schema does not publish a current count. Active subscribers alone do not establish installations, price, retention duration, complaints, or acceptance. Public reporting must protect subscriber privacy while retaining a stable denominator. The inherited 1 Closed / 21 Partially Closed / 2 Open entity ledger remains unchanged.
