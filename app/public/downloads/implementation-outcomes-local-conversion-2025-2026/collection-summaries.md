# Implementation Outcomes and Local Conversion, 2025-2026

Sixteen reviewed official records showing where federal programs and local projects have moved into obligations, governance changes, physical construction, software, standards drafts, procurement channels, regulatory action, or dated conversion gates.

Captured: 2026-07-23

## Interpretation boundary

A record qualifies only when it names a project, recipient, operator, facility, agreement, physical milestone, software release, standards artifact, procurement channel, regulatory action, or dated decision gate. Announced value, obligation, outlay, award, construction, test, acceptance, operation, and scale remain separate stages. Official claims are attributed, later reversals are preserved, and absence of a downstream record is not converted into a negative outcome.

## 8. GAO Prototype OTA Effectiveness and Transition Tracking Review

**Publisher:** U.S. Government Accountability Office

**Document type:** Oversight Report

**Capture status:** Official page captured

GAO found that DOD could not systematically identify how often prototype OTAs became FAR production contracts and therefore could not fully assess whether prototype OTAs delivered capabilities.

### Key findings

- Fiscal 2024 prototype OTA obligations exceeded $16 billion.
- DOD tracked production OTAs but lacked a reliable method to link prototype OTAs to follow-on FAR production contracts.
- GAO made two recommendations and DOD agreed with both.

### Why it matters

The report establishes a system-level evidence gap between procurement mechanism and delivered capability, reinforcing the need for agreement-specific follow-through.

### Evidence limits

- GAO's 18-system sample was nongeneralizable.
- The report did not assess the OpenAI agreement and cannot establish its outcome.

**Official source:** https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107546/index.html
