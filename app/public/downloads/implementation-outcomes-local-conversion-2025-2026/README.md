## Capture exceptions

Official-link records identify sources that were reviewed but whose hosts suppressed automated export. The bundle preserves those official URLs rather than substituting non-authoritative copies.

## Interpretation boundary

A record qualifies only when it names a project, recipient, operator, facility, agreement, physical milestone, software release, standards artifact, procurement channel, regulatory action, or dated decision gate. Announced value, obligation, outlay, award, construction, test, acceptance, operation, and scale remain separate stages. Official claims are attributed, later reversals are preserved, and absence of a downstream record is not converted into a negative outcome.
