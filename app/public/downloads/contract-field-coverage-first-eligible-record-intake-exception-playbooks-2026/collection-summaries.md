# Contract-Field Coverage, First-Eligible-Record Intake, and Exception Playbooks, 2026

Phase 57L classifies all sixty required fields across nine reopening contracts, defines nine first-eligible-record queues, and assigns sixty human-reviewed resolution actions while preserving every inherited outcome hold.

Captured: 2026-08-08

## Interpretation boundary

Coverage states are not readiness scores. A present field cannot be carried across records, a first-eligible-record definition is not evidence that a candidate exists, and partial coverage cannot fire a trigger. Identity, period, schema, definition, privacy, method, unit, authority, acceptance, custody, implementation, capability, and closure remain distinct.
