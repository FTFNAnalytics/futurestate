## Interpretation boundary

Each record preserves the local action key, official report, recommendation number or hold, affected component, current GAO status, agency response, last visible update period, limitation, and continuation rule. Agency response is not implementation; recommendation status is not the Phase 56F entity evidence ledger or an operating outcome.
