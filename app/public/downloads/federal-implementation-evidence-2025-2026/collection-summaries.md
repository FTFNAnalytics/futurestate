# Federal Research-to-Implementation Evidence, 2025-2026

Eight bounded official records that move the Phase 55K research agenda toward named obligations, awards, contracts, facilities, a scheduled field trial, a delivered production milestone, transmission financing, and test or standards artifacts.

Captured: 2026-07-23

## Interpretation boundary

Implementation is staged as direction, selection, award or obligation, physical milestone, test, acceptance, operation, and scaled outcome. A record advances only to the highest stage directly supported by its official evidence.

## 1. USAspending Award DEMS0000003: Talon Nickel Project Double Play

**Publisher:** U.S. Department of the Treasury, USAspending

**Document type:** Federal Award Record

**Capture status:** Original file captured

The captured USAspending API record identifies Talon Nickel (USA) LLC, award DEMS0000003, a $114.85 million federal obligation, $954,321.45 in reported outlays, $250.88 million in non-federal funding, and an October 31, 2026 current end date for Project Double Play.

### Key findings

- The award is a named DOE project grant for an advanced domestic battery-minerals processing facility.
- The captured record reports $114,846,344 obligated and $954,321.45 outlayed.
- The record reports $250,881,777 in non-federal funding and a performance period from November 1, 2023 through October 31, 2026.

### Why it matters

This is the collection's clearest obligation-and-outlay trail and repairs an existing FTFN signal by preserving the source response used for the amounts.

### Evidence limits

- Obligations and outlays do not prove permitting, construction, equipment installation, commissioning, feedstock, qualification, offtake, or production.
- USAspending values can change with later transactions and reporting updates.

## 2. DARPA Lift Challenge Competitor and Live-Trial Announcement

**Publisher:** Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency

**Document type:** Program Milestone

**Capture status:** Official page captured

DARPA named the first 72 invited Lift Challenge teams after more than 480 applications and scheduled a five-nautical-mile live performance trial in Dayton, Ohio, for August 2-9, 2026, with $6.5 million in prizes.

### Key findings

- More than 200 teams completed critical requirements and milestones before the first 72 invitations were announced.
- The live trial is designed to compare payload-to-weight performance on a five-nautical-mile circuit.
- The competition is scheduled for August 2-9, 2026 at the National Museum of the U.S. Air Force.

### Why it matters

The record converts a broad DARPA logistics and aviation research direction into named performers and a dated field-test gate.

### Evidence limits

- Invitations and a scheduled event do not prove that the trial occurred or that any design met the performance target.
- The announcement does not establish certification, procurement, production readiness, service economics, or operational adoption.

## 4. CHIPS NAPMP Final Advanced-Packaging Awards

**Publisher:** National Institute of Standards and Technology

**Document type:** Federal Award Record

**Capture status:** Official page captured

NIST announced $1.4 billion in final advanced-packaging awards: $100 million each to Absolics, Applied Materials, and Arizona State University, plus $1.1 billion to Natcast for advanced-packaging capabilities at the Tempe prototyping and piloting facility.

### Key findings

- Three $100 million awards target glass-core substrates, silicon-core substrates, and wafer or panel-level processing.
- The $1.1 billion Natcast award is intended to operate advanced-packaging capabilities co-located with NSTC prototyping in Tempe.
- The planned facility includes a baseline advanced-packaging piloting line intended to bridge laboratory research and production.

### Why it matters

The awards identify recipients, locations, facility functions, and targeted manufacturing capabilities behind the Phase 55K semiconductor-equipment and packaging direction.

### Evidence limits

- Final awards do not prove the facilities or piloting lines are operational.
- The source does not establish process yields, qualified products, customers, production volumes, or commercial transition.

## 6. Project Pele First TRISO Fuel Delivery

**Publisher:** Idaho National Laboratory

**Document type:** Program Milestone

**Capture status:** Official link record

Idaho National Laboratory reported production and delivery of Project Pele's first batch of TRISO fuel to the Transient Reactor Test Facility, a physical milestone for the planned mobile microreactor demonstration.

### Key findings

- The fuel was delivered to the Idaho National Laboratory site intended for the demonstration.
- The release identifies INL, the Strategic Capabilities Office, the Army, BWXT, and DOE as collaborators.
- The source characterizes the batch as the first TRISO microreactor fuel delivered to its final destination.

### Why it matters

This is the strongest physical production milestone in the collection and advances the advanced-nuclear lane beyond strategy, funding, and ground-breaking.

### Evidence limits

- Fuel delivery does not prove the reactor is assembled, licensed or authorized, fueled, critical, or generating power.
- The release does not establish demonstration performance, mobility, safety results, cost, commercial readiness, or follow-on deployment.

- The project targets emerging security standards and guidance for 5G/6G Open RAN and core technologies.
