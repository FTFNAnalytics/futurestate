## Interpretation boundary

A transition classification is not evidence that a transition occurred, and a reopening contract is not evidence that its trigger fired. Missing official values stay null; identity, privacy, period, stage, method, unit, denominator, authority, acceptance, custody, implementation, capability, and closure remain distinct.
