# Open-Rail Acquisition and Partial-Closure Deepening, 2026

Fourteen evidence decisions continue all six Open rails and deepen eight high-value Partially Closed records. DOE, F-35, and Hornsdale advance to Partially Closed; three exact rails remain Open.

Captured: 2026-07-24

## Interpretation boundary

Every decision retains its Phase 56F coverage ID and continuation rule. Evidence state measures one selected record, not performance. Adjacent context is never substituted for a missing denominator or final outcome.
