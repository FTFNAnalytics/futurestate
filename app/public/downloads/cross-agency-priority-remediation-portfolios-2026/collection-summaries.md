# Cross-Agency Priority Remediation Portfolios, 2026

Phase 56O publishes seven agency priority-recommendation portfolios and one bounded government-wide benefit model while preserving the 1 Closed / 21 Partially Closed / 2 Open evidence ledger.

Captured: 2026-08-01

## Interpretation boundary

Each record keeps agency, portfolio arithmetic, state definitions, status period, subject scope, limitation, remaining gap, and continuation rule attached. Implemented, added, de-prioritized, current, modeled, and realized states remain distinct.

## 1. Department of Energy: Phase 56O Remediation Follow-Through

**Publisher:** U.S. Government Accountability Office

**Document type:** Oversight Report

**Capture status:** Official link record

GAO reports that DOE implemented five of thirty prior priority recommendations, added one priority recommendation, removed priority status from two, and now has twenty-four priority recommendations. Phase 56O decision: Publish the exact portfolio movement while keeping implemented, newly added, de-prioritized, and currently prioritized states distinct.

### Key findings

- Coverage identity: coverage-56f-doe.
- Exact record: GAO-26-109001 DOE priority recommendation portfolio.
- Status period: April 2025 baseline through July 2026.
- Continuation rule: Reopen when GAO publishes the next DOE priority letter or changes a named current recommendation status.

### Why it matters

The record exposes implementation throughput and remaining remediation workload without converting a portfolio into a performance rate.

### Evidence limits

- The letter reports portfolio movement and priority areas, not the status of every recommendation or a measure of department performance, safety, readiness, or value.
- Action-level identities and closure evidence for the current twenty-four, including DOE insider-threat recommendations 1 and 7 and the separate AI-inventory action.
- No ranking, composite, readiness score, performance rate, or causal claim is supported.

## 8. United States federal government: Phase 56O Remediation Follow-Through

**Publisher:** U.S. Government Accountability Office

**Document type:** Oversight Report

**Capture status:** Official link record

GAO estimates that implementing open recommendations could produce $132 billion to $251 billion in measurable future financial benefits; the range is model-based, excludes many nonquantifiable benefits, and is not realized savings. Phase 56O decision: Publish the modeled range as bounded government-wide context with the method and realization limits attached.

### Key findings

- Coverage identity: context-56o-gao-open-recommendations-benefit-model.
- Exact record: GAO-26-108932 government-wide open-recommendation benefit model.
- Status period: Open recommendations as of October 2025; report published May 2026.
- Continuation rule: Reopen when GAO updates the model, publishes realized benefit attribution, or materially revises the open-recommendation inventory.

### Why it matters

The record exposes implementation throughput and remaining remediation workload without converting a portfolio into a performance rate.

### Evidence limits

- The $132 billion to $251 billion range is a modeled government-wide potential-benefit estimate as of October 2025. It is not a forecast, budget score, agency allocation, or realized saving, and actual benefits depend on whether, how, and when recommendations are implemented.
- Recommendation-level implementation, attribution, timing, realized savings, nonfinancial outcomes, and agency-specific allocation of any benefit.
- No ranking, composite, readiness score, performance rate, or causal claim is supported.
