# Cross-Cohort Coverage and Missing-Record Closure, 2010-2026

Twenty-four entity-specific coverage decisions identify the strongest current operating evidence, one highest-value missing record, a closure state, and an exact reopening rule.

Captured: 2026-07-24

## Interpretation boundary

Coverage status measures evidence closure, not entity performance. Exact closures, partial closures, and open records preserve identity, attribution, unit, denominator, method, observation window, and reopening rule. No ranking, composite score, readiness score, causal effect, or inference from missing disclosure is supported.

**Document type:** Regulatory Decision

**Capture status:** Official link record

BTS Technical Directive 40 names JetBlue as a 2026 reporting carrier and fixes the reporting threshold and airport scope. Phase 56F decision: The reporting denominator is closed; the full-year outcome and cause-controlled interpretation remain unavailable.

### Key findings

- Highest-value missing record: Later full-year cancellation and schedule denominator.
- Closure status: Partially Closed.
- Remaining gap: Full-year cancellations and schedule, route and airport mix, weather, maintenance, crew controls, complaints, and verified remedies.

### Why it matters

The coverage decision prevents broad context from displacing the one operating or closure record that would materially deepen the named entity.

### Evidence limits

- A reporting obligation does not provide a full-year outcome, explain causes, verify remedies, or make carriers comparable across network and identity breaks.
- Full-year cancellations and schedule, route and airport mix, weather, maintenance, crew controls, complaints, and verified remedies.
- No causal effect, ranking, composite score, readiness score, or unsupported cross-entity comparison is supported.
