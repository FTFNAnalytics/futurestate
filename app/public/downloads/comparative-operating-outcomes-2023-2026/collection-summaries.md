# Comparative Operating Outcomes, 2023-2026

Thirty-two official records establish bounded operating-outcome trails across institutional AI and cybersecurity, manufacturing, infrastructure, and mobility, aviation, and space.

Captured: 2026-07-24

## Interpretation boundary

No composite score or ranking is created. Counts, percentages, dollars, hours, miles, megawatts, participants, jobs, missions, and program stages retain their own units, periods, geographies, methods, and attribution.
