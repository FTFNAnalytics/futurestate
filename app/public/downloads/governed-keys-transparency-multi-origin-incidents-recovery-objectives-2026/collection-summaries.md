Phase 57U executes 1,170 synthetic governed-key, key-lifecycle, transparency, multi-origin, incident, and recovery-objective cases across nine contracts while preserving every inherited hold and failing every trust, origin, incident, or recovery divergence closed.

Captured: 2026-08-09

## Interpretation boundary

Every key, signature, lifecycle event, log entry, proof, origin observation, incident, receipt, replay, and recovery drill is synthetic. Unknown or revoked signers, inconsistent logs, divergent origins, incomplete containment, and missed recovery objectives fail closed; reconstructed state never activates automatically.
