## Interpretation boundary

The collection compares evidence stages, not equivalent technologies or markets. A plan is not an appropriation; a permit or site license is not construction or operation; a financial decision is not financial close unless the record says so; financial close is not commissioned capacity; and an analyzed or projected activity level is not measured output, utilization, safety, or local benefit.
