## Terminology requiring human review

## Review procedure and exit

1. Verify the English record/hash and source-to-contract mapping. Decide whether a plain-language translation is the required edition or only a pilot. Record the actual editor and decision; do not infer consent from silence.
2. Compare the overview and all six corresponding sections sentence by sentence. Check negations, uncertainty, dates, actor/asset identity, causal limits, and the distinction between planned capacity, qualified capacity, delivery and acceptance.
3. Have a qualified language reviewer and a subject reviewer record findings against exact draft hashes. Record locale, terminology decisions and substantive corrections. Do not transfer an English review automatically to the translations.
4. Revise and recheck the exact drafts. Only after real review and applicable authority may a dated approval receipt be recorded and the approved text integrated into the public edition, followed by accessibility and build verification.
