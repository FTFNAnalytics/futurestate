### Toronto — clarify adoption versus operative permission

The draft correctly names the application, item and withheld amendment bills; it keeps conditions, enactment, permit, construction and occupancy separate. However, the second section says a favorable planning decision “changes permission.” In this context that shorthand could suggest operative development permission despite the still-withheld instruments described elsewhere.

Proposed correction for a dated article revision: “Council adoption records a planning decision subject to the stated conditions; it does not by itself establish that the withheld amendments have been enacted or that a building permit has been issued.” The retained September 21 receipt supports this limited distinction, not legal advice or a new finding about the application's current status.

The September 21 receipt also records portal/Council discrepancies and eight bounded address searches. The annual article shares its exact source IDs, but the current context helper omits `operations.receipts`, so this later context is not returned for the annual route. This is a concrete follow-through gap to repair after the running candidate has finished validation. Do not alter the original receipt's affected-route list or treat a negative search as proof of no permit.

Next: add the scoped dated clarification and exact receipt link, verify historical text/provenance remains inspectable, and obtain subject review. Preserve the separate September 28 obligation.

### Montana — completed identity reconciliation is not a completed quarter

The 32-ID/768-row reconciliation does not establish a project quarter, construction, accepted service or subscriber outcome. No new spreadsheet analysis was performed in this desk pass.

The annual text's four source IDs are not the exact resource-index ID checked by the September 15 decision. The context helper therefore correctly declines an automatic source-ID join. If a later project-level note is useful, author an explicit scoped association and explain this difference; do not broaden the generic join to similar names or every Montana source.
