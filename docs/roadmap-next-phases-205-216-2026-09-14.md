## Core objective

Build a trusted, source-backed reference for understanding frontier technologies and the systems that turn them into useful capabilities. A reader should be able to answer: **What exists? What could become possible? What must happen first? What has actually been delivered? What benefits or harms have been measured? What remains uncertain?**

FTFN's distinction should be its connection between technical possibility, physical infrastructure, institutions, named delivery and human consequences. The five-layer framework and seventeen topics remain the organizing thesis. Being the best available resource requires useful answers and disciplined updating—not the largest count of pages, packets or source links.

### The twelve-question commissioning list

| Reader question | Required answer boundary |
| --- | --- |
| What observed climate conditions constrain infrastructure in a named place? | Separate observations, projections, local exposure and attributed effects |
| Which agricultural or bioeconomy capability has moved beyond a controlled trial? | Name organism/crop, method, field or production setting, period and limits on transfer |
| What useful quantum capability has actually been demonstrated? | Identify task, hardware, benchmark and resource/error assumptions; do not equate benchmark success with deployment |
| What does a new sensing or discovery instrument establish? | Separate instrument performance, measured data, inference and independent validation |
| Which TSMC Arizona facilities and utilities have reached which stages? | Keep fab identity, issuer claims, construction, accepted services and recurring production separate |
| What can Toronto application 24 254930 legally and physically do now? | Follow enacted instruments, conditions, permits and delivery records for that exact file |
| Has Louisiana broadband availability converted into observed adoption? | Keep Starlink and Nextlink separate; require cohort, numerator, denominator, period and method |
| Has the NNSA enterprise cost/schedule requirement been resolved? | Track each exact recommendation and final artifact; adjacent closures do not substitute |
| What does Hanford's operating record establish about material balance? | Reconcile compatible feed, product, secondary waste, inventories and periods before calculating ratios |
| What did Amtrak PIDS deployment complete—and what remains unproven? | Distinguish installations, final acceptance, uptime and passenger outcomes |
| When does a critical-minerals project become dependable supply? | Separate permissions, finance, construction, commissioning, accepted product and recurring output |
| What has a named battery asset demonstrated beyond installed capacity? | Retain power/duration, dispatch, service, availability, event and period definitions |

These questions are editorial assignments, not preselected factual conclusions. A useful answer may be “not established,” provided it states what is established, the bounded search, and the exact missing evidence. A method-only explanation without artifact-level findings does not count as a completed factual answer.

## Research and editorial quality bar

Every release-selected article needs a direct useful answer, a clear evidence cutoff, claim-level support, attribution, uncertainty, delivery dependencies, and a specific next update trigger. Where relevant it must include adverse or contradictory evidence and distribution, affordability or service-quality consequences.

Use original papers, official records, disclosed methods and measured operating evidence appropriate to the question. Alternative primary sources can fill a real evidential role; a portal, press summary or adjacent project cannot be relabeled as the required artifact. Word counts and source counts are screening aids, not proof of reference quality.

Keep review stages distinct: drafted, internally edited, exact sources checked, independent review complete, locally validated, repository-published, hosted and owner-launched. “Published” as a repository label is not evidence that this candidate is live.
