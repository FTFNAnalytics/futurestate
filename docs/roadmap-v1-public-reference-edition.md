# FTFN v1 Public Reference Edition roadmap

**Planning date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** Phases 181–204  
**State:** Content, routes, and full-program local validation complete; external review, repository publication, hosting, public launch, and v1 promotion remain separate

## Outcome

The v1 Public Reference Edition is FTFN's most ambitious content program to date. It turns the governed evidence corpus into a coherent reference system: one canonical reader entry for every topic, visible research and source backlogs, global jurisdiction and place frameworks, named delivery cases, comparison and longitudinal controls, failure and distributional analysis, correction services, accessible-edition contracts, and a flagship *State of Future Systems 2026* package.

The objective is not simply to publish more pages. It is to make FTFN the best available evidence-bounded resource for understanding what future systems could become, what institutional and physical dependencies govern their delivery, what evidence would demonstrate progress, and where the present record remains incomplete.

This roadmap records completed local content architecture. It does not create a future source check, exact artifact, project identity, stage decision, outcome observation, causal finding, owner disposition, expert review, translation approval, Git publication, hosted deployment, public launch, or v1 promotion.

## Built program inventory

- **24 phases**, covering Phases 181–204.
- **1,048 primary records** across canonical topics, evidence gaps, places, projects, questions, comparisons, outcomes, casebooks, monographs, editions, and promotion controls.
- **3,216 unique public HTML routes** across native phase routes, the immutable `/review/v1ref/` edition, and the stable `/reference/` reader layer.
- **25 JSON exports**: one export for each phase plus the aggregate edition export.
- **24 dated update records**, one for every phase.
- **17 canonical topic editions** and **102 canonical question briefs**.

The route total is a deduplicated public inventory. Native phase routes are retained where declared; `/review/v1ref/` is the immutable edition namespace; `/reference/` is the stable reader entry point. The three route families do not rewrite the authority or status of their underlying records.

## The major content pushes

### 1. Make every topic navigable as a body of knowledge

The canonical resource map and topic editions connect the strongest source, signal, project, place, mission, comparison, and decision-use material for every topic. A reader should be able to enter through a topic and move from orientation to decisive evidence, open questions, delivery cases, and explicit limitations without encountering unsupported synthesis.

### 2. Turn incompleteness into an inspectable research program

The evidence backlog, missing-system missions, authority-rail packets, and exact-artifact slots expose what is unresolved and what would resolve it. Missing evidence remains a research contract, not a negative finding. This makes the resource more useful while preventing breadth from masquerading as certainty.

### 3. Build a genuinely global receiving-system layer

The jurisdiction profiles and place atlases widen FTFN beyond a small set of familiar systems. They model authority, finance, infrastructure, implementation, operation, and public-value questions without transferring a national policy claim to a project, or a project result to an entire place.

### 4. Connect possibility to named delivery

The two delivery atlases and international project cohort make named projects central to the reference system. Exact inherited identities remain exact; open project slots remain open. Stage, conversion, acceptance, and outcome fields advance only through dated evidence and the appropriate decision gate.

### 5. Make comparison and outcomes harder to misuse

The comparative and longitudinal laboratories make entity identity, period, measure, definition, method, denominator, geography, revision lineage, and acceptance explicit. No comparison, series, trend, or outcome is admitted merely because two numbers or sources are available.

### 6. Treat failure and public value as first-class content

The casebooks and distribution observatory ensure the reference does not become a catalogue of announcements and progress narratives. Delay, reversal, correction, affordability, access, distribution, and service quality receive governed research structures—but no classification, cause, correction result, or outcome value is invented.

### 7. Synthesize across systems without creating a score

The constraint monographs and *State of Future Systems 2026* package explain relationships among compute, power, water, materials, institutions, finance, workforce, logistics, safety, and public authority. The synthesis remains source-bound and non-ranking; it does not manufacture causal findings or a universal readiness score.

### 8. Make the reference correctable and broadly usable

The change-intelligence desks require visible challenge, review, propagation, and receipt histories. The accessible-edition contracts specify plain-language, low-bandwidth, French, and Spanish surfaces while preserving human language review, subject review, accessibility review, and publication as separate gates.

## Operating follow-through

The content build and the live operating calendar are separate lanes. The next work proceeds in this order:

1. **Preserve the validated local baseline.** Receipt `204-FULL-LOCAL-VALIDATION-2026-09-01-be92c873cfdb` binds the complete 1,048-record candidate and its 10,230-page / 151-export / 230-update / 830-source whole-site snapshot. Any candidate change must rerun the same full suite and create or match a current digest-bound receipt.
2. **Operate evidence acquisition lawfully.** Work the 285-item backlog, 120 exact-artifact slots, open project/place dockets, and longitudinal candidates through real dated reviews. Use alternative official primary sources when the exact artifact is unavailable, but preserve the original question and record a bounded blocker when authority is insufficient.
3. **Obtain expert and jurisdiction review.** Subject-matter, jurisdictional, comparative-method, and longitudinal reviewers must approve factual or interpretive upgrades before they become reference findings.
4. **Produce accessible editions.** Render plain-language and low-bandwidth English editions first. French and Spanish remain held until human language and subject review are complete.
5. **Reconcile the candidate for repository publication.** A Git commit or push must bind the same validated candidate and remain distinct from hosting or public access.
6. **Run hosted release verification.** Hosting, canonical URLs, indexing, redirects, exports, social metadata, accessibility, and rollback must be verified on the actual deployment.
7. **Make the v1 decision explicitly.** Promotion requires every mandatory Phase 204 gate to be terminal and an owner receipt binding the exact candidate. A passing local build alone cannot promote v1.

Meanwhile, all Phase 165–180 calendar operations continue only on their lawful dates. A later evidence receipt may update the living reference, but it must never rewrite an immutable prior edition or validation receipt.

## Acceptance contract

The Phase 181–204 program is locally complete only when:

- all 1,048 primary identities are unique and resolve through their declared registries;
- all 3,216 public HTML routes, 25 JSON exports, and 24 update records reconcile exactly;
- every canonical topic, question, source, project, place, comparison, outcome, casebook, monograph, correction, edition, and promotion record preserves its upstream evidence boundary;
- missing external evidence remains a visible next-artifact requirement rather than an inferred negative result;
- no synthetic observation, stage, score, rank, comparison, causal finding, translation approval, or owner outcome appears;
- the complete inherited and Phase 181–204 validation suite passes against one candidate digest;
- full local validation, Git publication, hosting, public access, and v1 promotion remain separately receipted decisions.
