The corrected content makes these distinctions explicit:

- Matching a field label does not supply or validate a source value. Only case normalization and outer trimming are allowed; ambiguity cannot be silently coerced.
- Empty/incomplete fixture routing does not acquire or accept evidence, prove that an official packet exists, or create a trigger, closure or publication.
- Role definitions do not assign people; evidence and publication review remain separate.
- Receipt integrity does not establish the truth of evidence or decide the substantive review outcome. Missing fields cannot be borrowed across records.
- No-event statements describe the historical rehearsal, not current external evidence absence. Later dated evidence decisions remain separate.

All eighteen exact historical reopening requests remain visible. Contextual agency sources are preserved without newly verifying their contents or currency. Separate Amtrak, Nextlink, Starlink, Montana, Hanford and NNSA evidence requirements are not merged by shared cohort labels.
