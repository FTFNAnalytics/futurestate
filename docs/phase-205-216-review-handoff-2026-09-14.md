## Subject/editorial reviewer worksheet

For each assigned article, record:

1. Reviewer name, relevant subject/jurisdiction experience, conflicts and actual review date.
2. Exact article ID, route and candidate digest.
3. Claim quoted or located by heading; exact supporting artifact and page/table/paragraph.
4. Whether the artifact establishes an observation, issuer claim, method result, analysis or unresolved question.
5. Entity, event date, reporting period, units and denominator where relevant. Distinguish retrieval date from publication and event dates.
6. Finding: supported as written, needs qualification, unsupported, unresolved, or outside expertise. Record proposed wording and rationale.
7. Editor response and changed claim/version. Reviewer acceptance must refer to the revised version, not be presumed from a change.
