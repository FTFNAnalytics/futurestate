# FTFN v0.6 roadmap — Open Evidence Review

**Version:** 0.6<br>
**Executed:** 2026-08-30<br>
**Phases:** 125–129
**Status:** Complete locally; GitHub publication and hosted deployment remain separate owner-controlled release steps.

## Core objective

FTFN exists to make the path from frontier claim to real-world consequence legible. v0.4 established the canonical public coverage, authority, encyclopedia, project and place architecture. v0.5 turned it into an Evidence Fieldbook. v0.6 performs the first full open review of that fieldbook: every mission-context signal is annotated, every mission is audited against its own requirements, every named project and place receives a conversion biography, every topic gets a state-of-evidence review, and every cross-system dossier receives an explicit compatibility synthesis.

This edition deepens interpretation without manufacturing evidence. A Published signal can support context without answering a mission. A project biography can expose an evidence chain without advancing a conversion stage. A topic review can prioritize the next artifact without ranking projects. A compatibility synthesis can identify a shared constraint without creating a common denominator, causal conclusion, winner or score.

## The twelve acquisition gaps

v0.6 reviews the corpus around the twelve uncovered missions but does not claim that review closes them. Current evidence offers stronger bounded support for advanced-manufacturing outcomes, aviation baseline and outcome, critical-minerals baseline, and mobility baseline. AI-for-science, chips, cybersecurity, discovery technologies, mobility outcome and space outcome remain corpus-bounded gaps. Existing post-quantum migration records are inadmissible adjacency for a quantum-system outcome claim.

The permitted language is that the current FTFN corpus does not contain mission-decisive evidence. The edition may never infer that qualifying evidence does not exist externally.

## Authorship standard

Generated identity and relationship scaffolding does not count as substantive content. Phase assertions must exclude repeated boundary boilerplate from narrative word counts and enforce unique per-record fingerprints.

- Evidence annotations: at least 120 authored words per note.
- Mission audits: at least 350 authored words per mission.
- Project biographies: at least 500 authored words each.
- Place biographies: at least 650 authored words each.
- Topic reviews: at least 900 authored words each.
- Cross-system syntheses: at least 800 authored words each.

The completed edition publishes separate rendered, substantive, exact-passage-deduplicated and repetition metrics where those measures apply. Phase 125 contains 33,235 substantive annotation words; Phase 126 contains 34,104 requirement-analysis words; Phase 127 contains 66,458 project/place biography words with unique section-body assertions; Phase 128 contains 33,592 rendered and 13,598 exact-passage-deduplicated analysis words; and Phase 129 contains 15,804 rendered and 5,137 exact-passage-deduplicated analysis words. Rendered totals are never presented as wholly unique prose.
