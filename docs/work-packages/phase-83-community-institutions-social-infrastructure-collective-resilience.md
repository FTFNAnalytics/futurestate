# Phase 83 Work Package: Community Institutions, Social Infrastructure And Collective Resilience

Date: 2026-08-24

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Create the community-capacity layer after Phase 82. Phase 83 asks whether everyday household capability is supported by trusted and accessible institutions; whether civic associations, cooperatives, community ownership, mutual aid, volunteers, and paid workers form durable capacity; whether local media, public records, community knowledge, and trusted intermediaries produce reachable and correctable public knowledge; and whether community hubs, preparedness, trauma repair, institutional restoration, and democratic normalization create long-horizon collective resilience.

The phase refuses six shortcuts: institution presence is not community access; volunteer count is not mutual-aid capacity; information volume is not public knowledge; consultation is not collective governance; an emergency plan is not community preparedness; and reopening is not collective recovery.

## Activation rule

Phase 83 cannot open from a building inventory, facility licence, membership list, volunteer count, cooperative label, public notice, page view, social-media reach, consultation event, emergency plan, exercise announcement, reopened facility, or recovery declaration. At least one Phase 82 chain must contain real, reviewed, and receipted:

- a household boundary, capability floor, baseline, and adequate service bundle;
- assessed care need, care capacity, protected workforce, provider, quality, access, continuity, and funding;
- complete household resource, expense, time, volatility, debt, arrears, and administrative-burden findings;
- verified benefit access, take-up, protection, stabilization, privacy, and remedy decisions;
- complete neighborhood, rural, disability, and return-trip access tests;
- enforceable displacement, mobility, right-to-remain, and right-to-return safeguards;
- bounded crisis response, service continuity, recovery, distribution, learning, and long-horizon security evidence; and
- independent review and dated propagation receipts.

No Phase 82 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 83 record therefore remains inactive.
