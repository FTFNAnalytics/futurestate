## Strongest findings

- DOT reports $545.692 billion in adjusted IIJA authority, $392.472 billion obligated, and $237.220 billion outlayed through May 31, 2026. The formula-plus-discretionary universe is a hard series break from Phase 56X's mostly unobligated discretionary-award review.
- FHWA reports 82.0 percent obligated and 55.4 percent outlayed; FTA reports 57.3 and 34.9 percent; FRA reports 63.0 and 11.1 percent. These are lifecycle measures, not delivery or outcome rankings.
- NTIA reports 56 submitted BEAD final proposals, 53 NTIA approvals, 51 NIST approvals making funds available, and 44 signed award agreements as of April 13, 2026.
- BEAD operational performance requirements now expose speed, latency, outage, technology, speed-tier, and active-subscriber sample denominators, but they are requirements rather than observed results.
- Hanford reports 176 fire-water piping inspections completed two years early, zero Tank Farms preventive-maintenance delinquencies for two months, and average 222-S sample turnaround reduced from about 180 days to less than 90 days over five years.
- Savannah River reports 46 million fewer curies in tank waste since 2022, of which DOE attributes 36 million to processing, while gallons processed remain a separate measure.
- Idaho reports an authorized 84-vault, 1,344-canister storage capacity and nearly 400,000 gallons treated since 2023 against an approximately 900,000-gallon mission.
- LAP4's $5.865353 billion current planning basis reflects approved 30 Diamond changes but is not complete TPC or production capability. SRPPF's leased warehouse and anticipated design milestones remain inputs and forecasts rather than delivered operating capability.
