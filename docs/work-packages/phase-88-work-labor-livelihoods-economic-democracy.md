# Phase 88 Work Package: Work, Labor, Livelihoods And Economic Democracy

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Build the work, labor-power, livelihood-security and economic-democracy layer after Phase 87. Phase 88 asks whether a public notice corresponds to a real and accessible job; whether employment supplies adequate pay, predictable time, benefits, health, safety, dignity, stability and advancement; whether workers can organize, bargain, enforce rights and share material power; and whether workers, households and places remain secure through layoff, closure, automation, artificial intelligence, climate transition, industrial restructuring and other displacement.

The phase refuses six substitutions:

- a job posting is not an available job;
- employment is not decent work;
- a wage rate is not household livelihood security;
- training completion is not durable career mobility;
- worker consultation is not bargaining power; and
- reemployment is not a just transition.

## Activation rule

Phase 88 cannot open from a posting, vacancy count, labor-shortage claim, application, offer, payroll count, wage rate, training completion, consultation, union mention, ownership share, benefit announcement, retraining seat or reemployment event. At least one Phase 87 chain must contain real, reviewed and receipted:

- inclusive early-childhood and school access, participation and demonstrated learning;
- affordable postsecondary, vocational and apprenticeship access and completion;
- valid assessment, demonstrated capability and credible credentials;
- fair transitions into decent work and civic participation;
- usable public knowledge, research and translation;
- information literacy, provenance, correction and protected freedom;
- sustained libraries, archives, media, arts and cultural institutions;
- community-learning continuity, recovery and trust;
- long-horizon human-development evidence; and
- independent review, correction, remedy, propagation and dated receipts.

No Phase 87 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 88 record therefore remains inactive.
