- A sustained breach can initiate a capacity plan but cannot reduce quorum, witness, diversity, time, verifier, notification, evidence, or publication requirements.

- Recusal protects decision independence; precedent remains append-only and cannot assign blame, establish intent, or publish a claim automatically.
- Holdover and resynchronization cannot import sequence, counter, receipt, or observation-time rollback.
- An embargo, canary, hotfix, rollout, or rollback record does not prove deployment, safety, effectiveness, or reader acceptance.
- Tombstoned artifacts remain discoverable but inactive; restoration drills cannot silently reactivate reader state.
- Every synthetic event remains outside the evidence and publication ledgers.
