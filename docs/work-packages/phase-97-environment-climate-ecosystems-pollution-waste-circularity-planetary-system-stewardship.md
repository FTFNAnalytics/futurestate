# Phase 97 Work Package — Environment, Climate, Ecosystems, Pollution, Waste, Circularity And Planetary-System Stewardship

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only; no operating evidence gate was run early, no live decision changed, and publication remains owner-approved.

## Purpose

Phase 97 connects mobility, freight and digital-network access to the environmental systems in which those services operate. It introduces an explicit record chain for greenhouse-gas accounting and delivered decarbonization; pollution, exposure and environmental justice; ecosystems, biodiversity and restoration; and waste, circularity, climate adaptation, disaster risk and long-horizon planetary-system stewardship.

The phase prevents six common substitutions:

1. An emissions target is not delivered decarbonization.
2. A permit limit is not healthy exposure.
3. Protected-area designation is not ecosystem recovery.
4. Waste diversion is not circular material stewardship.
5. Adaptation spending is not reduced climate vulnerability.
6. Restored infrastructure is not restored community or ecosystem.

## Record admission contract

All 32 records inherit the exact eight Phase 96 cohort identities and source, signal, evidence-gap, canonical-file, reader-pathway and local-system references. Every record remains `Published` as a public contract but operationally `Inactive`, with `Not Open` as its decision state.

No record may advance until it has the relevant predecessor, lawful authority, exact system and population identities, measured baseline, environmental and distributional evidence, Indigenous-rights and environmental-justice review, alternatives, compound-risk stress testing, independent review, human decision and dated receipt. Empty substantive record arrays, null reviewer and receipt fields, `not_started` propagation, disabled automated scoring and ranking, and `none` Phase 64 change are mandatory.

## Family 1 — Climate mitigation and delivered decarbonization

The 20-gate dossier begins with a verified Phase 96 digital territorial-access ledger and covers inventory boundaries, scopes and gases; baseline and counterfactual; carbon budgets and pathways; direct and lifecycle emissions; removals and offsets; policy, finance and delivery instruments; sector transitions; workforce and community effects; Indigenous rights; distributional effects; monitoring, independent review, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat a target, pledge, model, expenditure, procurement or offset as evidence of delivered, additional and durable decarbonization.

## Family 2 — Pollution, exposure and environmental justice

The 22-gate ledger traces pollutant and source identity across air, water, soil, noise and chemical pathways; release and permit records; monitoring quality; cumulative exposure; health and ecological endpoints; hotspots; environmental-justice distribution; incidents and emergency response; enforcement; remediation; compensation; review, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat compliance with a permit limit, an area-wide average, facility monitoring or remediation activity as proof of healthy exposure or equitable protection.

## Family 3 — Ecosystems, biodiversity and restoration

The 22-gate register covers ecosystem and habitat identity; species and population condition; connectivity; freshwater, ocean, coast, forest, wetland, grassland, soil and cryosphere condition; disturbance and cumulative effects; protected areas; harvest and use; restoration baselines; monitoring; Indigenous stewardship; community value; independent review, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat designation, conservation spending, planted area or project completion as proof of ecological integrity, population recovery or durable restoration.

## Family 4 — Circularity, adaptation and planetary stewardship

The 24-gate ledger traces materials and products across extraction, design, durability, repair, reuse, recycling, producer responsibility, residual waste and hazardous streams; climate hazard, vulnerability and adaptation; preparedness and disaster risk; loss and damage; planetary-boundary interactions; participation, monitoring, review, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat diversion tonnage as circularity, adaptation spending as reduced vulnerability, reconstructed assets as recovered communities, or a single environmental indicator as planetary-system stewardship.

## Phase 98 handoff

Phase 98 should be **Law, Justice, Public Safety, Emergency Management, Security, Defense And Peace**. It should connect environmental and planetary-system obligations to rights, remedies, courts and administrative justice; prevention and accountable public safety; emergency preparedness, response and recovery; civil protection and critical-system security; defense and democratic oversight; conflict prevention, civilian protection, ceasefires and durable peace.

Its principal non-substitution boundaries should be:

1. A law on the books is not access to justice.
2. Police presence is not public safety.
3. An emergency declaration is not preparedness or recovery.
4. Security capability is not protected rights or reduced risk.
5. Defense spending is not security.
6. A ceasefire is not durable peace.
