# Phase 85 Work Package: Housing, Shelter, Land Use And Place Stability

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Create the housing and place-stability layer after Phase 84. Phase 85 asks whether housing need is measured; approved supply becomes completed, occupied, safe, accessible homes; tenure and complete household costs remain secure; public, social, cooperative, community, and Indigenous housing retain durable governance; homelessness responses lead to stable housing; displacement is prevented or remedied; retrofits create verified dwelling improvements; and relocation, reconstruction, and climate adaptation preserve the right to remain or return and the long-horizon life of a place.

The phase refuses six shortcuts: approved units are not delivered homes; a shelter bed is not stable housing; an affordability label is not household affordability; retrofit spending is not a safe and habitable dwelling; temporary relocation is not a right to return; and reconstruction is not community recovery.

## Activation rule

Phase 85 cannot open from a housing-need estimate, zoning change, permit, unit announcement, start, completion certificate, vacancy count, rent label, subsidy award, shelter-bed count, service enrollment, retrofit budget, insurance payment, evacuation placement, reconstruction contract, or rebuilt-unit count. At least one Phase 84 chain must contain real, reviewed, and receipted:

- adopted food production and stewardship with protected land, water, Indigenous sovereignty, workforces, biodiversity, and climate capacity;
- verified processing, storage, distribution, logistics, market, procurement, community-benefit, workforce, utility, traceability, and fallback capacity;
- measured household and institutional access, affordability, nutrition, dignity, school and care meals, benefit access, denials, burdens, and remedies;
- tested usable reserves, contamination controls, recalls and remedies, circular flows, emergency authority, fair release, restoration, and long-duration adaptation; and
- independent review, dissent, correction, propagation, and dated receipts.

No Phase 84 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 85 record therefore remains inactive.
