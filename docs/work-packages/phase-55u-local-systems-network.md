## Cross-Corridor Synthesis

`Local Authorization Is Not Operation` compares only the evidence-conversion structure:

```text
demand or resource or plan
  -> rule, permit, environmental decision, license, or finance
  -> construction and inspection
  -> acceptance, connection, or commissioning
  -> compliant operation
  -> sustained output, utilization, and local outcomes
```

The map does not claim that compute, mining, and launch systems are technologically, economically, or institutionally equivalent.

`Local Watch 002` remains `In Review`. It is useful as a synthesis draft, but the three dated downstream questions should mature before a separate briefing-publication gate.
