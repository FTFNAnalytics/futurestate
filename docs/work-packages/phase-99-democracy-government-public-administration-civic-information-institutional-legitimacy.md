# Phase 99 Work Package — Democracy, Government, Public Administration, Civic Information And Institutional Legitimacy

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only; no operating evidence gate was run early, no live decision changed, and publication remains owner-approved.

## Purpose

Phase 99 connects the Phase 98 justice, security and peace chain to the institutions and information systems through which people authorize, contest, observe and renew public power. It introduces separate governed records for inclusive representative democracy; constitutional government and public-administration capability; fiscal, audit, procurement and open-government accountability; and civic information, media pluralism, public trust, institutional legitimacy and democratic resilience.

The phase prevents six common substitutions:

1. An election held is not inclusive representative democracy.
2. A public institution is not administrative capacity.
3. A published plan is not accountable government delivery.
4. Open data is not usable civic information.
5. Approval or compliance is not institutional legitimacy.
6. Government continuity is not democratic resilience.

## Record admission contract

All 32 records inherit the exact eight Phase 98 cohort identities and source, signal, evidence-gap, canonical-file, reader-pathway and local-system references. Every record remains `Published` as a public contract but operationally `Inactive`, with `Not Open` as its decision state.

No record may advance until it has the relevant predecessor, lawful authority, exact people and institutions, measured baseline, rights and inclusion safeguards, distributional evidence, alternatives, independent review, a human decision and a dated receipt. Empty substantive record arrays, null reviewer and receipt fields, `not_started` propagation, disabled automated scoring and ranking, and `none` Phase 64 change are mandatory.

## Family 1 — Inclusive representative democracy

The 20-gate dossier traces franchise, registration, candidacy, electoral boundaries, campaign finance, political information, administration, voting, counting, certification, representation, participation, inclusion, peaceful transfer, independent review and remedy.

It cannot treat an election event, a formal franchise, a turnout rate, a party system or certified results as proof of equal practical participation, fair representation or democratic integrity.

## Family 2 — Constitutional government and administrative capability

The 22-gate ledger covers constitutional checks, legislatures, executive authority, public-service capability, administrative process, operating resources, intergovernmental coordination, integrity, accessible service delivery, implementation, continuity, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat an institution, mandate, plan, budget, program, dashboard or compliance activity as proof of administrative capacity or accountable delivered outcomes.

## Family 3 — Public accountability and open government

The 22-gate register traces revenue, budgets, expenditure, assets and liabilities; controls and audit; procurement and beneficial ownership; grants and public support; meetings, records and access; usable open data; complaints, enforcement, correction and recovered public value.

It cannot treat publication, an audit finding count, an open-data portal or procurement compliance as proof of transparency, integrity, usable civic information, correction or public value.

## Family 4 — Civic information, legitimacy and democratic resilience

The 24-gate ledger traces civic-information needs, media freedom and pluralism, local news and journalist safety, information provenance, platform governance, participation and institutional response, competence and fair treatment, trust, legitimacy, backsliding, civic space, democratic renewal and resilience.

It cannot treat communication volume, consultation counts, approval, confidence, satisfaction, compliance, continuity of office or continuity of service as proof of informed participation, legitimacy or democratic resilience.
