Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-26; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 92 extends democratic financial governance into the fiscal and cross-border institutions that raise and forgo public revenue, authorize and deliver expenditure, stabilize households and economies, issue and service sovereign obligations, govern public balance sheets, regulate trade and capital flows, preserve essential supply, coordinate macroeconomic policy, and allocate burdens and benefits across places and generations. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not assess a tax, determine liability, enforce collection, allocate a budget, appropriate funds, award procurement, decide benefit eligibility, establish delivery or public value, issue or restructure debt, declare fiscal sustainability, impose a tariff or sanction, decide a trade remedy or treaty claim, screen an investment, declare supply resilience, coordinate macroeconomic policy, or score or rank a taxpayer, beneficiary, creditor, worker, consumer, firm, community, jurisdiction, or country.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Revenue capacity is not distributive justice.
- Expenditure authorization is not delivered public value.
- Deficit size is not fiscal sustainability.
- Sovereign borrowing is not productive public investment.
- Export growth is not resilient development.
- Aggregate stabilization is not democratic macroeconomic legitimacy.
