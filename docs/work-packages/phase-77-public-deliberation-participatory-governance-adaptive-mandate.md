# Phase 77 Work Package: Public Deliberation, Participatory Governance And Adaptive Mandate

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: Complete and locally release-validated
Publication state: Local build only; owner-only deployment remains a separate approval

## Goal

Create the public-governance layer after Phase 76 cross-case learning, portfolio governance, and policy retirement. Phase 77 defines how a real cross-case or non-transfer finding can become a public decision question; how affected publics and rights holders receive standing; how notice becomes usable access; how consultation, consent, comments, hearings, evidence, alternatives, and dissent enter a common record; how material issues receive public reasons; how legitimacy, appeal, and mandate remain separate; and how authorization is reopened when time, evidence, harm, context, rights, or performance changes.

The phase is deliberately participation-empty, consent-empty, response-empty, appeal-empty, mandate-empty, and adaptive-review-empty. It creates a public destination without treating publication as notice, attendance as access, engagement as consultation, consultation as consent, comment count as evidence weight, participation as consensus, completed procedure as legitimacy, authorization as permanent mandate, or elapsed time as renewal.

## Activation rule

Phase 77 cannot open from a Phase 75 recommendation or decision alone. At least one Phase 76 record must contain an admitted transfer or non-transfer finding connected to a real portfolio or policy question. The question must then pass separate controls for:

1. convening authority, jurisdiction, duties, decision scope, alternatives, and status quo;
2. affected-public, rights-holder, worker, user, payer, expert, dissenting, and future-user standing;
3. notice, accessibility, language, participation support, privacy, safety, challenge, and dual review;
4. authorized consultation, public-comment, hearing, evidence, alternative, issue, dissent, and reasoned-response processes;
5. legitimacy audit, appeal, reconsideration, stay, remedy, mandate scope, duration, conditions, sunset, and receipt; and
6. registered baselines, indicators, monitoring, time and event triggers, renewed participation, adaptive options, and a separate mandate-change decision.

No Phase 76 record meets the first prerequisite. Every Phase 77 record therefore remains inactive.

Sixteen gates cover a real public question, authority, decision scope, affected people, rights and treaties, cumulative burden, future users, expertise, dissent, notice, accessibility, material support, privacy and safety, challenge, dual review, receipt, archive, and propagation.

Twelve constituency classes cover directly affected residents; Indigenous nations and rights holders; workers and labor; service users and accessibility; environmental-justice communities; public authorities; suppliers and contractors; technical and local-knowledge experts; safety and emergency actors; taxpayers and ratepayers; future users and youth; and dissenting, excluded, or hard-to-reach groups.

Every register remains `Inactive - No Admitted Cross-Case Finding` and `Not Open` with no finding, question, authority, inventory, rights record, standing decision, notice plan, accessibility plan, participation support, privacy plan, challenge, reviewer, or receipt.

Automatic standing, automatic exclusion, volume-as-legitimacy inference, scoring, and ranking are disabled.

Every issue remains `Unopened`. Every quality dimension remains `Not Measured`. Every docket remains `Inactive - No Authorized Participation Process` and `Not Open` with no authorization, calendar, common record, consultation, consent finding, comment, hearing, intergenerational review, material issue, response, dissent, quality finding, reviewer, or receipt.

Automatic consensus, automatic consent, comment-count weighting, scoring, and ranking are disabled.

## Content expansion

Phase 77 adds ten Published long-form guides:

- **Community Standing 001** treats the public as multiple affected constituencies rather than an undifferentiated audience.
- **Indigenous And Treaty-Aware Consultation 001** distinguishes nations, rights, treaties, consultation, accommodation, agreement, and consent.
- **Worker And Supply-Chain Voice 001** exposes occupational, safety, skills, contractor, supplier, and local-capacity evidence.
- **Accessibility And Service-User Participation 001** makes discoverability, usability, support, privacy, and non-user evidence part of the decision contract.
- **Environmental Justice 001** carries cumulative environmental, infrastructure, service, fiscal, and distributional burdens across project boundaries.
- **Technical Expertise And Public Knowledge 001** separates evidentiary expertise, operating knowledge, lived experience, authority, conflict, and uncertainty.
- **Public Reason 001** converts submissions into a versioned material-issue and response matrix without treating comments as votes.
- **Dissent, Appeal And Reconsideration 001** keeps closure contestable through explicit grounds, independent review, stays, correction, remedy, and reopening.
- **Intergenerational Review 001** gives future users a visible standing method and exposes irreversibility, lock-in, option value, residual duties, and sunset.
- **Adaptive Democratic Mandate 001** connects standing, participation, public reason, legitimacy, conditions, monitoring, reopening, amendment, reversal, sunset, and retirement.

Four Published dependency maps make the major boundaries inspectable:

- `Notice Is Not Accessible Participation`;
- `Participation Volume Is Not Consent Or Legitimacy`;
- `A Comment Count Is Not A Reasoned Response`; and
- `A Mandate Is Not Permanent Authorization`.

The guides and maps are integrated through ten reader pathways, eight canonical named files, five local systems, Outcomes Watch, seven other operating guides, the data index, sitemap, Phase 76 detail routes, and the Phase 77 public registry.
