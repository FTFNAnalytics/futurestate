# Phase 124 — Integrated Topic Research Workbenches

**Status:** Complete<br>
**Effective date:** 2026-08-30
**Program:** FTFN v0.5 — The Evidence Fieldbook

## Purpose

Give each canonical topic one operational research surface. The workbench composes existing architecture and evidence into visible next work; it does not replace the coverage file or encyclopedia chapter.

## Delivered

- 17 topic workbenches with exactly four missions each.
- All 68 Phase 121 missions preserve their exact acquisition state and ordered packet IDs: 56 are Covered and 12 remain Gap.
- All 80 Phase 120 packets are reachable as topic-wide discovery inventory only; that inventory is not an assignment to every mission.
- 1540 exact mission-to-packet relationships, 30 Phase 122 playbooks and 12 Phase 123 dossiers are linked by stable ID.
- 24 project and 15 place joins.
- Independent quality states, exact next-artifact requirements, owners, freshness and correction paths.
- Eighteen routes and one public JSON export.

## Boundary

A workbench is an index, not an adjudication. All mission answers and quality dimensions remain unadjudicated. Topic-wide Candidate packet lists are discovery inventory only; exact mission coverage comes exclusively from each copied Phase 121 acquisition_packet_ids array. Comparison dossiers remain context-only.

## Completion standard

Phase 124 is complete when all seventeen topics resolve one-to-one, every workbench has four missions, every mission preserves its exact Phase 121 acquisition state and packet IDs, the 56-covered/12-gap split is unchanged, every Phase 120–123 object is reachable from at least one workbench where applicable, canonical project/place identity is preserved, and no evidence or future-gate state changes.
