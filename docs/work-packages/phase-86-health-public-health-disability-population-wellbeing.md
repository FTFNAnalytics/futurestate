# Phase 86 Work Package: Health, Public Health, Disability And Population Wellbeing

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Create the health and population-wellbeing layer after Phase 85. Phase 86 asks whether people can obtain timely, affordable, accessible, culturally safe and continuous primary and preventive care; whether acute, emergency, specialty, behavioral, reproductive, maternal, child, elder, rehabilitation, home and palliative care are safe and effective; whether medicines, diagnostics and supply systems are usable; whether surveillance and outbreak response preserve scientific and civil-rights boundaries; whether environmental and occupational exposures are reduced and remedied; whether disability rights and supports remain enforceable; and whether preparedness, recovery and long-horizon population wellbeing are real rather than administrative labels.

The phase refuses six shortcuts: nominal capacity is not timely access; coverage is not affordable care; an encounter is not a health outcome; a surveillance signal is not a diagnosis; an emergency declaration is not readiness; and service reopening is not population recovery.

## Activation rule

Phase 86 cannot open from a clinic listing, nominal provider count, coverage card, enrollment, appointment, encounter, procedure, bed count, discharge, laboratory signal, case alert, emergency declaration, stockpile total, exercise schedule, reopened service, or aggregate wellbeing index. At least one Phase 85 chain must contain real, reviewed, and receipted:

- adopted housing need, delivery, occupancy, condition, habitability, accessibility and neighborhood-infrastructure findings;
- durable tenure, complete household affordability, public and community-housing stewardship, allocation rights, maintenance and continuity;
- measured homelessness need, accessible emergency protection, permanent and stable exits, rights, care continuity, displacement protection and remedy;
- completed retrofit, repair, climate and disaster-housing evidence with tenant and resident protections;
- voluntary relocation, compensation, enforceable return, reconstruction, household and neighborhood recovery, and long-horizon place stability; and
- independent review, dissent, correction, propagation and dated receipts.

No Phase 85 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 86 record therefore remains inactive.
