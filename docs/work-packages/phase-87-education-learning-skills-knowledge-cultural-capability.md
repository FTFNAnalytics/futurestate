# Phase 87 Work Package: Education, Learning, Skills, Knowledge And Cultural Capability

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Build the human-development and public-knowledge layer after Phase 86. Phase 87 asks whether children, learners, workers and communities can reach inclusive education; participate, persist and learn; afford postsecondary, vocational and apprenticeship pathways; demonstrate durable capabilities rather than merely receive credentials; move into decent work and civic life; and sustain libraries, research, journalism, information literacy, arts, culture, memory and community learning through disruption and recovery.

The phase refuses six shortcuts:

- enrollment is not learning;
- a credential is not demonstrated capability;
- a training seat is not access to decent work;
- published research is not usable public knowledge;
- digital access is not information literacy; and
- institution reopening is not community learning recovery.

## Activation rule

Phase 87 cannot open from an enrollment count, attendance total, training seat, course completion, graduation, badge, credential, library opening, publication, broadband connection, cultural-event calendar or reopened institution. At least one Phase 86 chain must contain real, reviewed and receipted:

- timely, affordable, accessible and culturally safe primary, preventive and community care;
- safe acute, emergency, specialty, behavioral, reproductive, maternal, child, elder and disability care;
- usable medicine, diagnostic and health-supply systems;
- governed surveillance, outbreak and environmental-exposure controls;
- disability rights, accessibility, community supports and legal capacity;
- tested all-hazards preparedness, service continuity and equitable recovery;
- measured population health, function, autonomy, participation and wellbeing; and
- independent review, correction, propagation and dated receipts.

No Phase 86 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 87 record therefore remains inactive.
