# Phase 101 Work Package — Whole-System Futures, Scenario Governance, Polycrisis Readiness, Civilizational Resilience And Future Generations

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-28
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only. No scenario, forecast, risk, readiness, recovery, resilience or future-generations decision is inferred.

## Purpose

Phase 101 synthesizes the domain-complete Phase 100 stack into governed whole-system futures. It separates scenario construction from prediction, cross-domain stress testing from readiness, preparedness options from usable capability, continuity from recovery and renewal, and long-term aspiration from accountable protection of future generations.

Its six non-substitution rules are:

1. A scenario is not a forecast.
2. A risk register is not readiness.
3. A foresight exercise is not a decision.
4. Redundancy is not resilience.
5. Continuity is not renewal.
6. A long-term goal is not protection of future generations.

## Admission contract

All records inherit the exact Phase 100 cohort and source identities. They remain `Published` as public contracts but `Inactive` and `Not Open` operationally. Substantive arrays are empty, reviewer and receipt fields are null, propagation is `not_started`, automated decisions, scoring and ranking are disabled, and Phase 64 change is `none`.

A human-reviewed scenario can open only after its purpose, scope, identity, baseline, assumptions, boundaries, uncertainty, models, distributional effects, rights safeguards, challenges and prohibited uses are complete. A stress-test ledger additionally requires real dependencies, hazards, cascade paths, exercise protocols, observations and verified corrective action. A readiness portfolio requires lawful authority, usable resources, options, sequencing, protected floors, activation criteria and recovery evidence. A civilizational-resilience ledger requires ecological viability, dignified essential floors, knowledge and institutional continuity, just recovery, renewal and future-generation standing.
