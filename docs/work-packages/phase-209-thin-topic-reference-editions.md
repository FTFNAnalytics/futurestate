## Work

1. Produce one substantive edition for each of the four thin topics around the reader questions in the roadmap.
2. Bind at least three distinct exact primary artifacts per edition, including substantive method or measured evidence. Use official data and original research appropriate to the topic.
3. Write a clear system overview, what is established, two bounded cases, dependencies, constraints, uncertainty, and the next decisive questions.
4. Use claim-level citations with artifact dates, units/denominators where relevant, and limits on transfer. New signals must describe distinct external developments.
5. Review high-consequence factual and interpretive claims, then render approved copy in the canonical template; provide a sources-and-limitations appendix.

## Acceptance
