## Evidence Boundaries

- Technology assessments identify options and barriers; they do not establish built supply.
- Selection announcements are not necessarily executed awards or obligations.
- Proposed generators, observed plant output, transmission capacity, and customer service are different evidence stages.
- County labor and housing-authorization data are baselines, not project workforce or housing-delivery proof.
- Preliminary capital programming is not procurement, award, construction, acceptance, operation, or service.
- An application and procedural record are not a regulatory decision.
- A procurement pipeline is not a solicitation or delivery commitment.
- Environmental review is not every permit and does not prove construction, qualification, production, or local outcomes.
