# Phase 98 Work Package — Law, Justice, Public Safety, Emergency Management, Security, Defense And Peace

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only; no operating evidence gate was run early, no live decision changed, and publication remains owner-approved.

## Purpose

Phase 98 connects environmental and planetary-system stewardship to the institutions that protect rights, prevent harm, respond to emergencies, govern security power, protect civilians and build durable peace. It introduces an explicit record chain for rights and practical justice access; violence prevention and accountable public safety; emergency management, civil protection and critical-system resilience; and democratic security, defense, conflict prevention, civilian protection and peace stewardship.

The phase prevents six common substitutions:

1. A law on the books is not access to justice.
2. Police presence is not public safety.
3. An emergency declaration is not preparedness or recovery.
4. Security capability is not protected rights or reduced risk.
5. Defense spending is not security.
6. A ceasefire is not durable peace.

## Family 1 — Rights, rule of law and practical justice

The 20-gate dossier begins with a verified Phase 97 planetary-stewardship record and covers authority, applicable law, standing, notice, accessibility, counsel and legal aid, filing, evidence, hearing, independence, timeliness, due process, interim protection, judgment, remedy, enforcement, appeal, distribution, Indigenous jurisdiction and independent review.

It cannot treat formal rights, the existence of a court, a digital portal or a legal-aid program as proof that a person or community can obtain timely, affordable, accessible and enforceable justice.

## Family 2 — Public safety, prevention and accountability

The 22-gate ledger traces harms, prevention and social determinants; police, fire, emergency-medical, regulatory, correctional and community roles; calls, response, force, detention and diversion; victim and survivor support; de-escalation; legality and proportionality; complaints, independent investigation, discipline, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat institutional presence, patrols, cameras, arrests, incarceration or falling reported incidents as proof of reduced harm, equitable protection, trust or accountability.

## Family 3 — Emergency resilience and critical-system security

The 22-gate register covers hazards, vulnerabilities, compound risks, warnings, evacuation, shelter, medical and essential-service capacity, logistics, mutual aid, critical dependencies, cybersecurity and privacy, drills, emergency powers, response, displacement, damage, recovery, Indigenous jurisdiction and independent after-action review.

It cannot treat a plan, declaration, exercise, stockpile, restored asset or restored service as proof of preparedness, delivered capacity, protected rights or recovered households, communities and ecosystems.

## Family 4 — Democratic security, civilian protection and durable peace

The 24-gate ledger traces lawful authority, civilian control, threat assessment, readiness and sustainment, rights-protective intelligence, cyber and strategic risk, deterrence and arms control, civilian protection, diplomacy, mediation, ceasefire verification, agreement implementation, transitional justice, return, restitution, reconciliation, inclusion, harm accounting and independent oversight.

It cannot treat capability, intelligence volume, surveillance coverage, spending, force size, weapons delivery, readiness claims, agreement signatures or ceasefires as proof of reduced risk, protected civilians, security or durable peace.
