## Required boundaries

- The seven portfolios use different inventories and subject mixes; their counts are not comparable agency rates.
- Implemented, added, de-prioritized, current, open, and closed states remain distinct.
- The government-wide range is a model of potential future measurable financial benefits, not a forecast, appropriation, budget score, agency allocation, or realized savings.
- Portfolio movement does not establish agency performance, service outcomes, readiness, safety, quality, productivity, value, or causation.
- The inherited HHS hospital-control tracker record remains In Review while its official tracker remains last updated July 22.
- The Phase 56F evidence ledger remains one Closed, twenty-one Partially Closed, and two Open.
- Public access, DNS, package freeze, and public GitHub remain out of scope.
