# Phase 78 Work Package: Interjurisdictional Compacts, Shared Public Value And Emergency Resilience

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: Complete and locally release-validated
Publication state: Local build only; owner-only deployment remains a separate approval

## Goal

Create the multi-authority and crisis-governance layer after Phase 77 public deliberation and adaptive mandate. Phase 78 defines how a real, time-bounded public mandate can become a joint-authority question; how constitutional, statutory, municipal, Indigenous, treaty, regulatory, public-utility, fiscal, service, and emergency jurisdictions remain distinct; how cross-border benefits, burdens, risks, and residual duties enter a common record; how public value and contributions become explicit compact terms; how mutual aid, service continuity, disputes, emergency powers, civil safeguards, hard expiry, restoration, and democratic reauthorization remain separate decisions.

The phase is deliberately mandate-input-empty, jurisdiction-finding-empty, compact-empty, contribution-empty, continuity-empty, dispute-empty, emergency-empty, normalization-empty, and reauthorization-empty. It creates a public destination without treating one public mandate as authority for every jurisdiction, consultation as treaty agreement, coordination as delegation, local benefit as shared public value, payment as governing control, mutual aid as permanent command, emergency activation as rights suspension, expiry as normalization, or continuing usefulness as democratic reauthorization.

## Activation rule

Phase 78 cannot open from a proposed, incomplete, perpetual, or unreceipted Phase 77 mandate. At least one Phase 77 record must contain a real authorized public mandate with completed standing, notice, accessibility, deliberation, material-issue, reasoned-response, legitimacy, appeal, condition, duration, sunset, and receipt records. The resulting joint question must then pass separate controls for:

1. jurisdiction, competence, duty, delegation, retained authority, treaty, rights, standing, and forum;
2. shared assets, cross-border flows, externalities, uncertainty, alternatives, conflicts, time, and independent review;
3. parties, non-parties, public purpose, value, benefit, burden, contribution, governance, transparency, safeguards, dispute, change, and separate authorization;
4. critical services, resources, requests, command, interoperability, access, cost, priority, continuity, evidence, remedies, handback, and closure;
5. emergency threshold, lawful authority, necessity, proportionality, scope, expiry, rights, oversight, data, procurement, challenge, restoration, and public accounting; and
6. a fresh ordinary-law and Phase 77 mandate if any extraordinary measure is to continue.

No Phase 77 record meets the first prerequisite. Every Phase 78 record therefore remains inactive.

## Content expansion

Phase 78 adds ten Published long-form guides:

- **Cooperative Federalism And Municipal Authority 001** maps competence, duties, delegation, and retained local authority before coordination.
- **Indigenous, Treaty And Intergovernmental Compacts 001** keeps nation-to-nation jurisdiction, rights, consultation, accommodation, agreement, and consent distinct.
- **Cross-Border Infrastructure And Externalities 001** follows service, resource, cost, benefit, risk, and residual-duty flows across system boundaries.
- **Shared Public Value Allocation 001** separates plural public values, distribution, burdens, safeguards, and remedies from aggregate benefit.
- **Fiscal And Capacity Sharing 001** records cash, assets, guarantees, workforce, infrastructure, data, risk, and continuing obligations without turning contribution into control.
- **Mutual Aid And Service Continuity 001** defines requests, acceptance, command boundaries, interoperability, service floors, restoration, and handback.
- **Intergovernmental Dispute Resolution 001** keeps interim continuity separate from the merits, appeal, remedy, and rights or treaty claims.
- **Emergency Powers And Civil Safeguards 001** bounds crisis action through evidence, legality, necessity, proportionality, rights, oversight, expiry, and remedy.
- **Crisis Evidence And Public Communication 001** distinguishes observations, estimates, assumptions, corrections, uncertainty, public reasons, and accessible notice.
- **Return From Emergency Authority 001** makes restoration, deletion, fiscal reconciliation, accountability, residual duties, and fresh reauthorization explicit.

Five Published dependency maps make the major boundaries inspectable:

- `One Public Mandate Is Not A Multi-Authority Compact`;
- `Local Benefit Is Not Shared Public Value`;
- `Fiscal Contribution Is Not Governing Control`;
- `Emergency Activation Is Not Permanent Authority`; and
- `Service Continuity Is Not Rights Suspension`.

The guides and maps are integrated through ten reader pathways, eight canonical named files, five local systems, Outcomes Watch, seven other operating guides, the data index, sitemap, Phase 77 detail routes, and the Phase 78 public registry.
