# Phase 117 — Global Authority Graph

**Status:** Complete
**Effective date:** 2026-08-30
**Program:** FTFN v0.4 — The Public Conversion Observatory

## Purpose

Expand FTFN beyond its United States concentration by mapping primary official rails across national, supranational and multilateral systems. The graph is an acquisition and monitoring layer, not a claim or completeness layer.

## Delivered

- 20 jurisdiction layers across 8 world regions.
- 80 Candidate authority rails and matching source records.
- Four authority classes per jurisdiction: statistics, energy/system operation, infrastructure/territorial delivery, and science/innovation.
- Coverage of all 17 FTFN topics and 8 conversion stages.
- Jurisdiction, institution and rail routes plus a public JSON export.

## Admission boundary

A mapped portal is not an admitted artifact. Every downstream use requires the exact document, dataset, docket, filing, permit, inspection, acceptance, operating or measurement record to be checked on its real date. No source mapping creates a signal, receipt, gate result, observation, outcome, comparison, score or ranking.

## Completion standard

Phase 117 is complete when the registry contains twenty distinct jurisdiction layers, eighty unique official HTTPS rails, all seventeen topics, all eight conversion stages, four authority classes per jurisdiction, Candidate monitoring status on every new source, and no future Phase 60 mutation.
