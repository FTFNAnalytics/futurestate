## Evidence Boundaries

- Source registries and portals establish availability and scope, not implementation or outcomes.
- Research roadmaps identify opportunities and barriers, not delivered factory capability.
- Concept notes and preliminary drafts are not final or binding requirements.
- Agricultural forecasts and weekly surveys are revisable and do not establish farm-level outcomes.
- Aviation registrations, data sheets, recalls, and investigations represent different administrative and safety stages.
- Space and discovery portals require a selected dataset, mission, product, or report before signal conversion.
- Area-wide groundwater requirements do not establish a named facility's water right, provider commitment, use, compliance, or operating outcome.
