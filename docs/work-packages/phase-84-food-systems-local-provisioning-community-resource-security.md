# Phase 84 Work Package: Food Systems, Local Provisioning And Community Resource Security

Date: 2026-08-24

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Create the food and material-provisioning layer after Phase 83. Phase 84 asks whether communities possess ecologically viable and rights-respecting production; secure land, soil, water, seed, biodiversity, energy, and workforce foundations; usable processing, storage, cold-chain, logistics, market, cooperative, and procurement capacity; affordable, nutritious, dignified, and culturally appropriate household and institutional access; and usable reserves, contamination controls, circular flows, climate adaptation, and long-horizon community resource security.

The phase refuses six shortcuts: food availability is not nutritional access; inventory is not usable reserve; local production is not resilient provisioning; procurement spend is not community benefit; waste diversion is not circular capacity; and short-term distribution is not durable food security.

## Activation rule

Phase 84 cannot open from acreage, farm count, nominal yield, facility count, storage capacity, inventory, local-purchasing percentage, procurement spend, meal count, benefit enrollment, food-bank distribution, reserve announcement, recall notice, waste-diversion rate, community-kitchen activity, or emergency delivery. At least one Phase 83 chain must contain real, reviewed, and receipted:

- an adopted community-institution baseline with verified access, trust, purpose, ownership, workforce, assets, continuity, and closure safeguards;
- admitted civic associations, cooperative and community-ownership capacity, mutual-aid capacity, protected volunteers and paid workers, resources, succession, and accountability;
- an admitted local-information ecosystem with publisher identity, public-record access, provenance, correction, accessibility, trusted intermediaries, public reasoning, and integrity review;
- tested preparedness, accessible warning and evacuation, community hubs, essential-system continuity, trauma and grief support, institutional restoration, democratic normalization, long-duration recovery, and recurrence prevention; and
- independent review, dissent, correction, propagation, and dated receipts.

No Phase 83 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 84 record therefore remains inactive.
