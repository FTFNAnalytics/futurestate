# Phase 102 Work Package — Public Knowledge Synthesis, Civic Decision Literacy, Reader Navigation, Content Closure And Evergreen Stewardship

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-28
**Roadmap state:** Planned content expansion is complete. Dated evidence operations, corrections and evergreen stewardship continue.

## Purpose

Phase 102 is the terminal planned content layer. It connects the full evidence and governance stack to canonical public synthesis, civic decision literacy, usable reader navigation, accessibility and translation, then closes the expansion roadmap with explicit maintenance, correction, archive and refresh contracts.

Its six non-substitution rules are:

1. A synthesis is not new evidence.
2. An explanation is not a recommendation or authorization.
3. Navigation is not comprehension or access.
4. Publication volume is not content completeness.
5. An archive is not erasure.
6. A stable page is not evergreen truth.

## Content-complete contract

`Content complete` means the planned subject-matter, governance, synthesis, reader-access and maintenance layers now have bounded public contracts. It does not mean every evidence gap is resolved, every page is permanently current, every reader has comprehension or access, or the evidence operation is finished.

All 32 records inherit the exact Phase 101 cohorts. They remain `Published`, `Inactive` and `Not Open` with empty substantive arrays, null reviewers and receipts, `not_started` propagation, disabled automation, and no Phase 64 change.

Canonical synthesis must preserve claim classes, evidence lineage, counterevidence, uncertainty, citations, corrections and retained history. Decision-literacy work must expose authority, baselines, options, tradeoffs, distribution, rights, causation, implementation limits and accountability without covert recommendation. Reader navigation must support orientation, progressive disclosure, accessibility, translation and low-bandwidth access without classifying or scoring readers. Evergreen stewardship must maintain coverage, source health, corrections, archives, owners, service levels and refresh triggers.
