## Vertical Contract

1. a named panel with declared indicator, unit, denominator, method, attribution, observations, and reporting breaks;
2. a dossier separating candidate drivers, constraints, alternative explanations, and temporal order;
3. a later-record test declaring compatibility, attribution, validation status, result, and unresolved next records.

The entity layers can publish independently. The portfolio interpretations remain held because the agency, production-line, battery, and carrier records do not share a common unit, denominator, method, identity, attribution level, or observation window.

## Publication Boundary

The build does not establish causal effects, comparative performance, readiness, or a common cross-entity score. Recommendation counts, audit findings, cumulative deliveries, fielding, contractor totals, incident response, installed capacity, annual cancellations, monthly on-time arrivals, complaints, and stated commitments remain separate measures. Company, operator, regulator, auditor, and independent-oversight attribution remain visible.

Public GitHub synchronization, package freeze, public access, Hostinger DNS changes, custom-domain attachment, and public launch remain separate explicit decisions.
