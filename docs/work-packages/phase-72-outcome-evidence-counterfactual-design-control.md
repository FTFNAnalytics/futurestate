# Phase 72 Work Package: Outcome Evidence Packet And Counterfactual Design Control

Date: 2026-08-23
Status: Complete and locally release-validated
Publication state: Local build only; owner-only deployment remains a separate approval

## Goal

Create the governed research layer between a Phase 71 panel and any stronger outcome language. Phase 72 gives every panel an exact evidence-packet destination, gives every named file an explicit alternative-explanation register, and provides an inactive counterfactual-design docket that cannot inspect results or publish causal language before a design is registered.

The phase is deliberately pre-publication and evidence-empty. It defines how descriptive status, level, change, target attainment, association, contribution, attribution, and causal-effect language must be separated without manufacturing an eligible panel, claim, alternative assessment, comparison, result, receipt, score, rank, or stage change.

## Delivered records

Each packet exposes seven claim classes and twelve evidence gates covering the eligible panel and lineage, question, claim class and verb, calculation, timing, continuity, adverse evidence, alternatives, attribution boundary, uncertainty, reproducibility, and dual review with receipt and propagation.

Automatic classification and publication are disabled.

### Eight alternative-explanation registers

One register maps exactly to each named file and its four outcome-evidence packets. Ten categories are published: pre-existing trend, concurrent intervention, policy, market, capacity, workforce, infrastructure, measurement, selection, and external events.

All eighty category assessments remain `Not Assessed`. Applicability, evidence for and against, uncertainty, disposition, selected explanations, adverse observations, reviewer notes, decisions, receipts, and propagation remain empty. Silence never means no alternative explanation, and automatic `none` decisions are disabled.

### Eight counterfactual-design dockets

One docket maps exactly to each Phase 71 outcome and comparison record, its Phase 72 alternative register, and four outcome-evidence packets. Each offers six governed design families: randomized allocation, interrupted time series, difference in differences, synthetic control, matched comparison, and regression discontinuity.

Twelve design gates cover the causal question, unit and population, outcome and estimand, baseline, comparison construction, identification, interference, confounding, missing data, falsification, power and uncertainty, and pre-registration with dual review and propagation.

All dockets remain `Inactive - No Causal Claim Proposed` and `Not Registered`; all ninety-six design checks remain `Inactive`. No design, estimand, control, donor pool, confounder, spillover, plan, pre-registration, reviewer, result inspection, decision, receipt, or propagation is present. Automatic design selection, result inspection, and causal publication are disabled.
