# Phase 91 Work Package: Finance, Banking, Credit, Capital Allocation, Monetary Systems And Financial Stability

Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-26; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 91 extends democratic economic governance into the monetary and financial institutions that create, transfer, price, allocate, insure, stabilize, guarantee, restructure, and resolve financial claims. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not classify money, admit a customer or account, authorize a payment, extend credit, underwrite or price a loan, service or collect a debt, allocate capital, value or rate an asset, make an investment, decide an insurance claim, change monetary policy, designate systemic importance, issue a public guarantee, resolve an institution, allocate losses, or declare public value.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Account access is not financial inclusion.
- Credit availability is not productive allocation.
- Asset-price growth is not durable wealth.
- Capitalization is not resilience.
- Central-bank intervention is not distributional legitimacy.
- Financial innovation is not financial stability.
