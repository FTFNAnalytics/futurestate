## Phase 55T: Thin-Topic Corpus Build

Goal: ensure every one of the 17 topics has a useful multi-record evidence shelf.

Priority order:

1. Cybersecurity and Aviation, which currently have no Published primary-topic signal.
2. Climate, Discovery Technologies, Mobility, and Space, which currently have one signal each.
3. Advanced Manufacturing, Agriculture and Bioeconomy, AI for Science, Quantum, Water, and Human Futures.
4. Energy, Chips and Compute, Policy and Standards, Critical Minerals, and Finance and Risk as cross-cutting support rather than the first destination for volume.

Minimum topic outcome:

- at least four bounded signals per topic,
- at least two publication-ready or Published records per topic where the evidence supports them,
- one clear topic-level current-state summary,
- named evidence gaps and next records,
- links to research collections, organizations, technologies, local systems, and pathways where relevant.

## Phase 55U: Local-Systems Network

Goal: extend FTFN's distinctive record-level local analysis beyond Ontario and the U.S. Southwest.

Select three or four additional systems using:

- dense official record availability,
- clear infrastructure and institutional dependencies,
- repeated relevance to multiple FTFN topics,
- named utility, planning, permitting, environmental, workforce, finance, and project records,
- enough evidence to separate authorization, construction, acceptance, operation, and scale.

Target system archetypes:

- a grid-and-compute growth corridor,
- a critical-minerals processing and manufacturing corridor,
- an advanced-manufacturing or defense-industrial corridor,
- a space or aviation infrastructure corridor.

Each new dossier requires:

- 15-25 active sources,
- 5-8 bounded signals,
- a constraint matrix,
- actors with authority,
- named projects and records,
- evidence limits,
- a reader pathway,
- dated monitors for unresolved downstream stages.

## Continuous Dated Inserts

The expansion program does not wait for:

- Phase 55H Toronto outcome recheck on August 1,
- Phase 55R DARPA Lift outcome recheck on August 10,
- Arizona wastewater acceptance and operation recheck on September 22,
- Project Baccara downstream service, construction, testing, occupancy, or operation records.

When a scheduled task runs, it enters the current phase as a bounded insert. If the official stage did not advance, record the dated result and return immediately to the expansion queue.

## Quality And Release Gates

Every expansion wave must:

- name what each source can and cannot prove,
- keep company claims labeled as interested-party evidence,
- separate source intake from signal drafting and publication,
- prevent private candidate IDs and notes from entering public output,
- update the public correction and update log,
- pass candidate validation, content validation, source health, Astro diagnostics, production build, and release assertions,
- commit the exact source state,
- preserve owner-only Sites access,
- stop before public GitHub, package freeze, DNS, custom-domain, or public-launch changes.
