# Phase 82 Work Package: Household Capability, Care Infrastructure And Everyday Security

Date: 2026-08-24

Status: Complete and locally release-validated; all governed records remain inactive

## Goal

Create the household-capability layer after Phase 81. Phase 82 asks whether essential systems can become an adequate, usable, and durable household service bundle; whether care need is met through real facilities, home and community capacity, a protected workforce, respite, coordination, rights, and continuity; whether money, time, volatility, debt, arrears, and administrative work make nominally available services unusable; and whether neighborhood access, displacement safeguards, crisis stabilization, and long-horizon recovery preserve everyday security.

The phase deliberately refuses six shortcuts: service availability is not household capability; unpaid care is not free capacity; program eligibility is not benefit access; a low monthly bill is not household affordability; neighborhood proximity is not accessibility; and temporary relief is not household recovery.

## Activation rule

Phase 82 cannot open from a program announcement, eligibility rule, facility licence, nominal vacancy, average household income, listed monthly price, neighborhood map, temporary payment, shelter placement, relocation offer, or recovery claim. At least one Phase 81 chain must contain real, reviewed, and receipted:

- an adopted essential-service classification and enforceable floor;
- lawful eligibility, access, non-discrimination, and accommodation duties;
- verified affordability, coverage, capacity, take-up, and quality findings;
- funded subsidy and cross-subsidy rules;
- an executable provider, public-option, interoperability, and portability model;
- tested continuity, mutual aid, provider-failure, and step-in readiness;
- adopted rights, complaints, appeals, remedies, rationing, restoration, and normalization rules; and
- independent review and propagation receipts.

No Phase 81 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 82 record therefore remains inactive.
