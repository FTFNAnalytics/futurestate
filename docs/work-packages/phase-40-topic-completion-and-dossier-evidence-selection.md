# Phase 40: Topic Completion And Dossier Evidence Selection

Date: 2026-07-10

## Goal

Execute the next content-library step toward FTFN becoming a comprehensive analytical resource:

- complete the public topic taxonomy by adding `Cybersecurity` and `Discovery Technologies`,
- add authoritative source anchors for discovery, sensing, Earth observation, ocean exploration, and local conversion evidence,
- deepen the Arizona and Ontario local dossiers with specific process and evidence sources,
- preserve the boundary between source coverage and publishable conclusions.

## Implemented

- Added topic records for:
  - `Cybersecurity`,
  - `Discovery Technologies`.
- Added 11 Tier 1 source records:
  - USGS 3D Elevation Program,
  - NASA Earthdata Common Metadata Repository API,
  - USGS Landsat Data Access,
  - NOAA Ocean Exploration Data Access,
  - Arizona Corporation Commission Integrated Resource Planning,
  - Arizona Corporation Commission Biennial Transmission Assessment,
  - City of Phoenix Planning and Development Department,
  - SHAPE PHX Planning and Permitting Portal,
  - City of Phoenix Water and Sewer,
  - City of Toronto Development Guide,
  - City of Toronto Building Permits.
- Increased the source library from 55 to 66 records.
- Increased the public topic library from 15 to 17 records.
- Linked new Arizona power, transmission, permitting, and water-service sources to the U.S. Southwest Chip Corridor profile.
- Linked new Toronto planning and building-permit process sources to the Ontario Real Estate profile.
- Added Phase 40 dossier notes to both local system profiles.

## Source Posture

The new sources improve coverage but do not authorize stronger local conclusions by themselves.

They support:

- discovery and sensing source coverage,
- climate, water, land, and geospatial data discovery,
- Arizona utility planning and transmission context,
- Phoenix permitting and municipal water-service process context,
- Toronto development review and permit-process context.

They do not prove:

- facility-level service readiness,
- specific interconnection timelines,
- project-level water capacity,
- permit approval outcomes,
- municipal servicing sufficiency,
- housing completions,
- project financing viability.

## Boundary

This phase does not:

- fetch source content,
- poll endpoints,
- create automated ingestion,
- generate or publish source-change claims,
- add scoring,
- add a database,
- promote any signals to `Published`,
- deploy the site.
