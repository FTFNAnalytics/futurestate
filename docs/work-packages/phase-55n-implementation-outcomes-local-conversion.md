## Purpose

Phase 55N tests the Phase 55L implementation trails against later records and extends the same method into the two local dossiers. It asks what happened after an award, agreement, policy direction, or early milestone and preserves the strongest stage supported by each official source.

The pass deliberately includes discontinuities and evidence limits. Implementation is not treated as a one-way ladder.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Commerce's stated legal and funding position does not establish that every NAPMP recipient action was canceled.
- GAO's department-wide finding does not establish that the OpenAI prototype failed.
- A versioned research tool is not commercial interoperability, certification, or a 6G deployment.
- Topping out is not building completion, occupancy, equipment qualification, or production.
- A purchasing catalog is not agency adoption or a mission outcome.
- A preliminary working draft is not a formal or deployed standard.
- A scheduled Council item is not adoption, enacted by-laws, permits, construction, or occupancy.
