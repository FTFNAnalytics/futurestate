# Phase 75 Work Package: Decision Accountability, Implementation Commitment And Realized-Impact Audit

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: Complete and locally release-validated
Publication state: Local build only; owner-only deployment remains a separate approval

## Goal

Create the governed accountability layer after Phase 74 decision translation. Phase 75 gives every named file an exact destination for institutional decisions and gives every measure lineage a commitment, resource, capacity, safeguard, output, benefit, harm, distribution, counterfactual-audit, sunset, reversal, and remediation contract.

The phase is deliberately decision-empty, implementation-empty, and impact-empty. It explains how a future recommendation could become an accountable institutional choice and how that choice could later be audited without manufacturing a decision owner, authorization, commitment, baseline, output, benefit, harm, remedy, score, rank, or operating outcome.

## Interpretation boundary

- a recommendation is not an institutional decision;
- an institutional decision is not legal, fiscal, or operating authority unless the exact authority exists;
- authorization is not an implementation commitment;
- funding, procurement, construction, installation, testing, acceptance, availability, and repeat operation remain separate;
- delivered output is not beneficiary exposure or realized benefit;
- favorable outcomes do not erase harms, burdens, disparities, dissent, or counterfactual uncertainty;
- contribution is not attribution, and attribution is not automatically causal;
- a sunset date does not enforce itself;
- remediation activity is not proof that harm was repaired;
- no silent renewal, stage advance, score, rank, or net-benefit claim is allowed.
