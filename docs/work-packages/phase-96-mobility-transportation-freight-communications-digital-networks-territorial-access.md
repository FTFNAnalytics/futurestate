# Phase 96 Work Package — Mobility, Transportation, Freight, Communications, Digital Networks And Territorial Access

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only; no operating evidence gate was run early, no live decision changed, and publication remains owner-approved.

## Purpose

Phase 96 connects commissioned territorial systems to the movement of people, goods and information. It introduces an explicit record chain for passenger mobility and complete trips; multimodal service planning, operations, safety and reliability; freight custody and completed delivery; and affordable, reliable, interoperable communications and digital access.

The phase prevents six common substitutions:

1. Infrastructure availability is not usable access.
2. A scheduled service is not a completed trip.
3. Vehicle throughput is not safe mobility.
4. Freight movement is not reliable delivery.
5. Network coverage is not affordable reliable connectivity.
6. A restored route is not community recovery.

## Record admission contract

All 32 records inherit the exact eight Phase 95 cohort identities and source, signal, evidence-gap, canonical-file, reader-pathway and local-system references. Every record remains `Published` as a public contract but operationally `Inactive`, with `Not Open` as its decision state.

No record may advance until it has the relevant predecessor, lawful authority, exact identities, measured baseline, accessibility and affordability evidence, distributional analysis, alternatives, compound-risk stress testing, independent review, human decision and dated receipt. Empty arrays, null reviewer and receipt fields, `not_started` propagation, disabled automated scoring and ranking, and `none` Phase 64 change are mandatory.

## Family 1 — Passenger mobility and complete access

The 20-gate dossier begins with a verified Phase 95 commissioned territorial-system record and covers trip purpose, destinations, suppressed demand, trip chains, proximity, physical and digital access, full household burden, multimodal options, universal design, rural and remote service, Indigenous jurisdiction, protected-status distribution, safety, complete-trip evidence, alternatives and independent public-value review.

It cannot treat an available asset, nearby destination, route or coverage statistic as proof that a person can complete a safe, affordable and dignified trip.

## Family 2 — Multimodal service, operations, safety and reliability

The 22-gate ledger covers walking, cycling, road, transit, rail, aviation, maritime and intercity networks; schedules and fares; fleets and facilities; workforce; operations control; safe-system performance; reliability and cancellations; accessibility and passenger rights; emergency continuity; externalities; integrated governance; and independent review.

It cannot treat a timetable as a completed trip, vehicle throughput as safety, or a ridership, speed or on-time statistic as equitable reliable service.

## Family 3 — Freight and completed delivery

The 22-gate register traces the service promise, shipment and custody identity, modal routing, terminals, border processes, equipment, workforce, inventory, quality, dwell, completed delivery, contractual cost, critical-goods priority, labor rights, community and environmental burdens, concentration, shock resilience, restoration and independent review.

It cannot treat tonnage, vehicle movement, throughput or terminal activity as completed reliable delivery or community benefit.

## Family 4 — Communications, digital infrastructure and territorial access

The 24-gate ledger traces network topology, backhaul and power, advertised and experienced performance, total affordability, accessible use, digital public infrastructure, interoperability, privacy and cybersecurity, universal-service and public-option models, rural and Indigenous access, concentration and lock-in, workforce and lifecycle capacity, outages, emergency restoration, environmental externalities, public-interest governance and independent review.

It cannot treat mapped coverage as affordable reliable connectivity, service availability as meaningful use, or a restored route or signal as community recovery.

## Phase 97 handoff

Phase 97 should be **Environment, Climate, Ecosystems, Pollution, Waste, Circularity And Planetary-System Stewardship**. It should connect mobility, freight and digital-network use to greenhouse-gas emissions; air, noise, water and soil pollution; ecosystems and biodiversity; material cycles and waste; mitigation and adaptation; disaster risk; environmental justice; Indigenous stewardship; restoration; monitoring; and long-horizon planetary boundaries.

Its principal non-substitution boundaries should be:

1. An emissions target is not delivered decarbonization.
2. A permit limit is not healthy exposure.
3. Protected-area designation is not ecosystem recovery.
4. Waste diversion is not circular material stewardship.
5. Adaptation spending is not reduced climate vulnerability.
6. Restored infrastructure is not restored community or ecosystem.
