# Phase 89 Work Package: Income, Wealth, Poverty, Social Protection And Economic Security

Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-25; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 89 extends the verified work-and-livelihood chain into household resources, balance sheets, material deprivation, social-protection access, distribution, shock resilience, and mobility. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not decide a tax liability, transfer, eligibility, denial, sanction, wealth value, poverty status, benefit, distributional result, or mobility outcome.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- An income transfer is not freedom from poverty.
- Earnings are not household wealth.
- Benefit eligibility is not benefit access.
- Aggregate growth is not shared prosperity.
- Asset ownership is not economic security.
- Poverty exit is not durable mobility.
