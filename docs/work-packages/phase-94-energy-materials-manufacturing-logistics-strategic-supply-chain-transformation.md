# Phase 94 Work Package: Energy, Materials, Manufacturing, Logistics And Strategic Supply-Chain Transformation

Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-26; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 94 extends productive-development governance into the physical systems that deliver industrial energy and water, qualify materials, sustain manufacturing, move goods, hold inventories and strategic reserves, and support emergency conversion and recovery. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not allocate a utility service or scarce input, qualify a material, accept production, release a reserve, activate emergency conversion or mutual aid, declare resilient access, technological sovereignty or just industrial transition, or score or rank an asset, firm, supplier, worker, community, region, technology, jurisdiction or country.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Installed capacity is not delivered reliable energy.
- Resource endowment is not qualified material supply.
- A factory announcement is not accepted production.
- Logistics throughput is not resilient access.
- Domestic-content percentage is not technological sovereignty.
- A decarbonization target is not a just industrial transition.
