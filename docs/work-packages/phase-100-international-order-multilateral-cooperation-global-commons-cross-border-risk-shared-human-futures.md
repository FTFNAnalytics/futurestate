# Phase 100 Work Package — International Order, Multilateral Cooperation, Global Commons, Cross-Border Risk And Shared Human Futures

**Status:** Complete and locally release-validated on 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Governed content expansion only; no operating evidence gate was run early, no live decision changed, and publication remains owner-approved.

## Purpose

Phase 100 connects the Phase 99 democratic-legitimacy chain to cooperation beyond a single jurisdiction and to risks, resources and futures that no one institution can govern alone. It introduces separate records for international order and treaty implementation; representative, financed multilateral delivery; migration, displacement and humanitarian shared responsibility; and global commons, catastrophic-risk governance, intergenerational equity and shared human futures.

The phase prevents six substitutions:

1. A treaty signed is not effective cooperation.
2. Institutional membership is not equal representation or influence.
3. A global commitment is not financed delivery.
4. Data sharing is not coordinated cross-border risk reduction.
5. A shared-resource designation is not governed stewardship.
6. A global goal is not a secured shared human future.

## Record admission contract

All 32 records inherit the exact eight Phase 99 cohort identities and their source, signal, evidence-gap, canonical-file, reader-pathway and local-system references. Every record is `Published` as an inspectable contract while operationally `Inactive`, with `Not Open` as its decision state.

No record may advance without its required predecessor; lawful and human-rights authority; exact party, institution, person, status, route, territory, commons, risk and period identity; implementation, finance and capability evidence; rights and distribution safeguards; independent review; a human decision; and a dated receipt. Substantive evidence arrays remain empty, reviewer and receipt fields remain null, propagation remains `not_started`, automated scoring and ranking stay disabled, and Phase 64 change remains `none`.

## Family 1 — International order and peaceful cooperation

The 20-gate dossier traces diplomatic and negotiating authority, treaty formation, ratification, entry into force, domestic implementation, compliance, verification, peaceful dispute resolution, human rights, power asymmetries, enforcement, correction and remedy.

It cannot treat a signature, ratification, meeting, declaration or communique as proof of implemented obligations or effective cooperation.

## Family 2 — Multilateral representation and collective delivery

The 22-gate ledger traces charters, members, voice, votes, vetoes, agenda access, informal influence, contributions, pledges, additionality, allocation, disbursement, local ownership, institutional capability, delivery, benefit, integrity and correction.

It cannot treat membership as equal influence, a pledge as finance, disbursement as locally owned benefit, or coordination language as delivered collective action.

## Family 3 — Human mobility and shared responsibility

The 22-gate register traces status and routes; asylum and non-refoulement; reception, services and due process; family, child, disability and privacy protection; work and inclusion; responsibility sharing; resettlement, return and reintegration; host-community capacity; durable solutions and remedy.

It cannot treat a border crossing, admission, status grant, return count or exchanged data as proof of protection, a durable solution or coordinated responsibility.

## Family 4 — Global commons and shared human futures

The 24-gate ledger traces planetary and human-system boundaries, transboundary and compound risk, observation and warning, coordinated authority, financed capability, benefit and burden sharing, Indigenous sovereignty, catastrophic-risk safeguards, long-horizon scenarios, future generations, resilience, renewal and remedy.

It cannot treat a commons designation, dataset, warning system, exercise, global goal, summit declaration, technology capability or aggregate progress measure as proof of stewardship, coordinated risk reduction, equitable benefit or a secured shared future.
