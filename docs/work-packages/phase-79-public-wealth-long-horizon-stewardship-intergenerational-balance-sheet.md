## Goal

Create the public-wealth and long-horizon stewardship layer after Phase 78. Phase 79 defines how a real executed compact can identify public assets and obligations; how capital, operating, maintenance, renewal, procurement, debt, guarantee, insurance, reserve, closure, restoration, distribution, and future-user records remain distinct; and how fiscal stress can trigger bounded review without converting emergency power, creditor preference, market value, or a composite score into public authority.

## Activation rule

Phase 79 cannot open from a proposed compact, emergency activation, contribution alone, capital announcement, budget line, procurement notice, financial statement, insurance certificate, or reserve label. At least one Phase 78 record must contain a real executed compact with separately authorized:

- public value, beneficiaries, burdens, and remedies;
- fiscal and capacity contributions;
- ordinary legal authority for every commitment;
- continuity, dispute, amendment, exit, and residual-duty terms;
- decommissioning and restoration duties;
- independent review, public notice, dissent, archive, and receipts; and
- no unresolved emergency authority being treated as ordinary fiscal authorization.

No Phase 78 record meets that prerequisite. Every Phase 79 record therefore remains inactive.
