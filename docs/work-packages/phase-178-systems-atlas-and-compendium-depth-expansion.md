# Phase 178 work package — Systems Atlas and Compendium Depth Expansion

**State:** Complete locally  
**Target window:** 2026-10-12 through 2026-10-25

## Objective

Expand FTFN from broad coverage toward best-in-class place-level and cross-system depth.

## Content contract

- Ten new place-system dossiers selected through a published coverage matrix.
- Six system layers, four bounded timeline entries, and five evidence dependencies per new place.
- Eight comparison overlays using compatible measures or an explicit non-comparability decision.
- Four cross-system essays connecting infrastructure, institutions, finance, workforce, environment, and operations without creating causal claims.
- Backlinks to canonical sources, signals, named projects, evidence gaps, pathways, and dependency maps.

## Place-selection contract

Assess candidates for editorial prioritization using official-source depth, named-project identity, local infrastructure relevance, policy/permit visibility, operating/outcome evidence, geographic diversity, and coverage-gap value. Publish the selected coverage ledger and its boundary, but do not publish a synthetic prioritization, readiness, or performance score.

## Acceptance

- Place and project identities remain separate.
- Every layer and timeline event is source-bound and dated.
- Dependency edges are qualitative and noncausal.
- Comparisons preserve denominator, period, method, and jurisdiction.
- New essays synthesize evidence without upgrading any underlying record state.
