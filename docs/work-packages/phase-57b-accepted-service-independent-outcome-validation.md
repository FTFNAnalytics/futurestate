## Strongest findings

- Amtrak reports eleven station projects turned over for passenger use and eight final completions during the November 2025 through April 2026 window; Camden, Detroit Lakes, and Fort Morgan occur in both named lists.
- Amtrak reports thirteen modified boarding-ramp installations, bringing the cumulative completed-car total to 141, while fleet availability and actual use remain unknown.
- Louisiana reports one Nextlink tower active with service available to 104 Bossier Parish BEAD locations; subscribers, installations, tests, uptime, affordability, adoption, and closeout remain undisclosed.
- Hanford's Central Plateau water facility passed operational acceptance testing and entered potable-water service; stated daily capacity is not treated as observed throughput.
- Hanford reports a 7.25-million-gallon ETF campaign, Washington Ecology reports about 50,000 gallons treated into 34 containers, project minutes report 66 containers shipped, and DOE reports 2.3 billion groundwater gallons treated in FY 2024.
