Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-26; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 93 extends fiscal and macroeconomic governance into the institutions that define development missions, coordinate production ecosystems, shape markets, fund research and demonstration, diffuse technology, govern standards and intellectual property, develop suppliers and workforces, anchor regional clusters and corridors, narrow regional disparities, diversify and decarbonize production, and distribute the gains and burdens of transformation. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not select a mission, sector, firm, technology, cluster, corridor, supplier, worker, or place; award a subsidy or procurement; declare additionality, readiness, diffusion, commercialization, convergence, resilience, shared prosperity, or just transformation; or score or rank any firm, worker, community, region, institution, sector, technology, jurisdiction, or country.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Subsidy volume is not additional productive capacity.
- Firm attraction is not rooted regional development.
- Patent count is not innovation diffusion.
- Export complexity is not shared prosperity.
- Infrastructure spending is not an executable production ecosystem.
- Output growth is not a just productive transformation.
