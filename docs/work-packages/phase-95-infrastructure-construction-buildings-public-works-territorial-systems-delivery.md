# Phase 95 Work Package: Infrastructure, Construction, Buildings, Public Works And Territorial Systems Delivery

Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-27; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 95 extends the physical-economy chain into the territorial systems that turn plans, land, rights of way, designs, permits, procurements and construction packages into commissioned, accessible and maintainable assets, public works and places. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not approve land or a project, issue a permit, award procurement, authorize construction, accept work, commission or occupy an asset, declare safe accessible service, infer place value or community recovery, or score or rank an asset, project, contractor, supplier, worker, community, place, institution, jurisdiction or country.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- A capital plan is not an executable project.
- A permit is not a construction start.
- Construction spending is not a commissioned asset.
- Substantial completion is not safe accessible service.
- Floor area is not place value.
- Reconstruction output is not community recovery.
