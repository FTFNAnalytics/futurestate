# Phase 76 Work Package: Cross-Case Learning, Portfolio Governance And Policy Retirement

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: Complete and locally release-validated
Publication state: Local build only; owner-only deployment remains a separate approval

## Goal

Create the institutional-learning layer after Phase 75 decision accountability and realized-impact audit. Phase 76 defines how an independently audited decision can enter institutional memory, how two admitted decisions can be compared, how shared dependencies and cumulative burdens can be governed across a portfolio, and how obsolete policy can be superseded, retired, and decommissioned without erasing evidence, dissent, failure, or unresolved harm.

The phase is deliberately audit-empty, lesson-empty, comparison-empty, portfolio-decision-empty, and retirement-empty. It creates a public destination for future cross-case work without treating thematic similarity as comparability, repetition as a best practice, aggregate benefit as equitable distribution, a successor policy as complete retirement, or archive deletion as closure.

## Activation rule

Phase 76 cannot open from an upstream recommendation, authorization, implementation activity, or reported benefit. At least two Phase 75 decisions must each:

1. contain an accountable institutional decision and authorization receipt;
2. contain an implementation commitment, registered baselines, delivery evidence, accepted outputs, and benefit, harm, burden, and distribution records;
3. complete an independent post-decision and counterfactual audit;
4. pass the separate fourteen-gate Phase 76 learning-admission review; and
5. pass a sixteen-gate pairwise comparability review before a transfer or non-transfer finding can be issued.

No Phase 75 record meets the first prerequisite. All Phase 76 records therefore remain inactive.

Fourteen gates cover independent audit, decision identity, implementation fidelity, baselines, outputs and outcomes, harms, distribution, counterfactual boundaries, negative and failed results, context, reviewer independence, challenge lineage, memo scope, dual review, archive, receipt, and propagation.

Twelve retention classes preserve positive, null, negative, failed, harmful, distributional, incident, dissent, exception, correction, remediation, and retirement records. Every class remains Empty. Every dossier remains `Inactive - No Independently Audited Decision` and `Not Open` with no audit receipt, admitted decision version, learning question, learning memo, bounded lesson, non-transfer finding, failure record, challenge, correction, reviewer, or receipt.

Automatic learning, transfer, policy reuse, scoring, and ranking are disabled.

### Eight policy-supersession and retirement ledgers

One ledger maps exactly to each named learning dossier and Phase 75 decision identity.

Every ledger remains `Inactive - No Authorized Policy Or Retirement Decision` and `Not Open` with no policy, owner, authority, successor, supersession, transition, decommissioning plan, residual duty, unresolved-harm disposition, archive, reviewer, or receipt.

Silent renewal, automatic supersession, automatic retirement, automatic archive deletion, scoring, and ranking are disabled.

## Content expansion

Phase 76 adds eight Published long-form guides:

- **Cross-Case Infrastructure Delivery 001** explains when lessons can travel across corridors and why delivery labels do not establish comparability.
- **Cross-Case Public Authority 001** distinguishes permissions, obligations, mandates, duties, discretion, appeal, and remedy.
- **Cross-Case Regulated Autonomy 001** binds safety evidence to operating domains, exposure denominators, fallback, versions, and affected users.
- **Cross-Case Industrial Capacity 001** separates announced, installed, qualified, accepted, and recurring capacity while exposing common-mode dependencies.
- **Cross-Case Digital Assurance 001** binds control portability to threat models, system boundaries, evidence methods, deployment, rollback, and retirement.
- **Cross-Case Local-System Burden 001** traces cumulative infrastructure, service, environmental, housing, fiscal, workforce, and access burdens.
- **Institutional Memory 001** preserves positive, null, negative, failed, harmful, corrected, superseded, and retired evidence.
- **Policy Retirement 001** separates review, sunset, supersession, transition, retirement, decommissioning, residual duties, remedy, and archive.

Three Published dependency maps make the boundaries inspectable:

- `An Audited Decision Is Not A Transferable Policy`;
- `A Portfolio Benefit Is Not A Shared Benefit`; and
- `Supersession Is Not Retirement Or Erasure`.

The guides and maps are integrated through ten reader pathways, eight canonical named files, five local systems, Outcomes Watch, Phase 75 operating guides, the data index, sitemap, and the Phase 75 detail destination.
