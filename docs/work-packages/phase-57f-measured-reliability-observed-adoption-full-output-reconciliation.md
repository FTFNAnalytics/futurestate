## Strongest findings

- Amtrak's FY24-29 station plan identifies 120 stations with known or potential PIDS deficiencies and 96 completed deployments as of April 30, 2023. Those values anchor but do not reconcile the June 2026 93-deployment and 117-installation aggregates.
- The historical Amtrak ADA program boundary contains 515 required stations, with Amtrak holding full or partial responsibility at 385. The named train-access cohort contains 30 stations: 18 completed, five third-party, and seven in design as of April 2023.
- ConnectMT requires LEO quarterly reports from grant execution through ten years after universal project availability certification. The schema requires active-subscriber counts, CPE shipments, served-BSL files, service availability, and may require performance tests.
- The Montana reporting contract is a useful privacy-safe model but is not evidence about Louisiana's 104-location Nextlink cohort, Louisiana's 10,635-location Starlink cohort, or any Montana awardee outcome.
- Hanford's February project-manager record reports nineteen vitrified-waste containers sent to IDF. Together with later 34-filled, 66-shipped, and approximately-30-staged observations, it forms a dated stage ledger rather than a one-to-one container reconciliation.
- DOE reports more than 100,000 gallons immobilized since October 2025 hot commissioning and describes a filled container as approximately seven metric tons. The lower bound and nominal specification cannot be multiplied to manufacture an exact glass-mass result.
- NNSA's current public program page characterizes U.S. capability as limited to R&D pits unsuitable for stockpile use, while the agency reported one fully qualified and diamond-stamped W87-1 first production unit in October 2024. The two official assertions require a dated terminology and output crosswalk.
- GAO-23-104661 Recommendation 1 remains Open. As of April 2026, GAO said no aligned lifecycle cost estimate existed and recorded NNSA's forecast to establish one in December 2026; a forecast is not a completed or GAO-sufficient baseline.
