# Phase 90 Work Package: Markets, Firms, Competition, Corporate Power And Democratic Economic Governance

Status: locally complete and release-validated on 2026-08-25; content commit and owner-only deployment require separate approval.

## Purpose

Phase 90 extends household economic security into the firms, ownership structures, markets, platforms, supply chains, public supports, regulators, rights, and remedies that shape prices, quality, wages, investment, innovation, resilience, public value, and democratic power. It publishes a governed empty state; it does not classify a firm, define a market, find power or abuse, approve a merger, allocate a subsidy, impose a remedy, or declare public benefit.

## Interpretation Boundaries

- A firm count is not a competitive market.
- A lower price is not complete consumer welfare.
- Market concentration is not proven abuse.
- Profit is not productive investment.
- Merger approval is not public benefit.
- Corporate disclosure is not democratic accountability.
