# FTFN v0.4 roadmap — Public Conversion Observatory

**Version:** 0.4<br>
**Executed:** 2026-08-30<br>
**Phases:** 116–119
**Status:** Complete locally; publication remains a separate owner-controlled release step.

## Core objective

FTFN exists to make the path from frontier claim to real-world consequence legible. The product should let a reader establish what was proposed, which authority acted, what was financed or procured, what was built, what was tested, what a receiving party accepted, what operated repeatedly, and whether a comparable outcome was actually measured. Those stages must remain distinct even when a compelling narrative would make them easy to collapse.

The long-range goal is the best available public resource on frontier technology, infrastructure, institutional delivery, and local conversion: broad enough to connect systems and places, deep enough to follow named files, and disciplined enough to show uncertainty, missing records, and incompatible evidence instead of manufacturing closure.

## Content architecture

The v0.4 public model is deliberately layered:

1. **Coverage** says what the publication intends to know and how quality is assessed.
2. **Authority** says where exact official artifacts can be found; it does not treat an institutional portal as evidence.
3. **Encyclopedia** explains the current record and its limits in authored, maintained chapters.
4. **Atlas** follows named projects and receiving places through evidence, chronology, conversion stage, unresolved gate, and next artifact.
5. **Existing evidence operations** continue to govern all observations, decisions, outcomes, corrections, and dated evidence gates.

## Completion standard

v0.4 is complete when:

- every one of the 17 canonical topics has a public coverage page and an authored encyclopedia chapter;
- all eight conversion stages are represented in the coverage and authority models;
- every Phase 117 source remains `Candidate` until an exact artifact is reviewed;
- all 27 encyclopedia chapters expose evidence IDs, competing interpretations, decisive evidence, and claim boundaries;
- all 39 atlas records expose their coverage tier, source and signal lineage, stop rule, and exact next artifact;
- every public route is canonical, indexable, present in the sitemap, and represented in the release manifest;
- the public JSON catalogue exposes each phase and the aggregate v0.4 registry;
- the eleven Phase 60 gates scheduled after 2026-08-30 remain scheduled, undecided, and without receipts; and
- candidate validation, content-reference validation, source health, phase assertions, Astro checking, the production build, post-build route verification, release verification, and diff checks all pass.

## Publication boundary

v0.4 is a content and navigation expansion, not a mass evidence promotion. It creates no new observation, project decision, outcome, score, ranking, or causal finding. International portals are discovery rails. Encyclopedia prose is synthesis of existing Published records. Atlas pages preserve the state of the governed records they reference. Future evidence checks remain future checks.

## What v0.4 unlocks

The observatory can now be expanded by completing exact-artifact acquisition against the authority graph, promoting curated project and place files into governed records, deepening thin topic chapters with compatible international evidence, and adding longitudinal outcome series only when stable identities, methods, periods, and denominators are available. Those are evidence operations, not automatic consequences of publishing the v0.4 architecture.
