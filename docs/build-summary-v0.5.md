# FTFN v0.5 Build Summary

**Edition:** Evidence Fieldbook<br>
**Completed:** 2026-08-30<br>
**Phases:** 120–124
**Status:** Complete and release-validated locally. GitHub publication and hosted deployment remain separate owner-controlled actions.

## Outcome

v0.5 turns the v0.4 Public Conversion Observatory into an inspectable research workflow. Readers can move from an official discovery rail to a prepared exact-artifact acquisition packet, a bounded research mission, the applicable verification method, a context-only comparative dossier, and a topic workbench that exposes the remaining work.

The release adds 121 canonical routes to the previous 6,207-page build and substantively upgrades 92 existing authority and cross-system routes, producing 6,328 generated route pages and 213 new or enhanced public surfaces. It does not admit an artifact, answer a mission, operate a future evidence gate, promote a Candidate source, or create an observation, outcome finding, score, ranking, recommendation, or causal claim.
