# Phase 55U Gap-013: Shuttle Landing Facility License Recheck

Check date: 2026-08-15
Decision: dated hold — no formal disposition located
Scope: Space Florida Shuttle Landing Facility site license `LRSO 18-018`

## Finding

No official FAA record reviewed on the check date establishes that `LRSO 18-018` was renewed, replaced, allowed to lapse, surrendered, or otherwise formally disposed of after its displayed January 15, 2026 expiration.

The FAA Shuttle Landing Facility page still states that the January 15, 2021 license would expire on January 15, 2026 and that Space Florida may apply to renew. The FAA Spaceports by State inventory, last updated June 15, 2026, still lists the facility as FAA Licensed under `LRSO 18-018` while displaying the same expiration date. The linked FAA license PDF is Revision 1, issued and effective January 15, 2021; it provides no later order or disposition. These records support a hold, not an inference of renewal or lapse.

## Layer Boundary

NASA's Shuttle Landing Facility page, updated August 12, 2026, states that Space Florida operates and manages the physical site under a 30-year property agreement with NASA. That is infrastructure and management evidence only. It is not an FAA site-license disposition, a vehicle or operator license, mission approval, operational authorization, or utilization measure.

The FAA's page describes Sierra Space as applying for a Vehicle Operator License. A proposed vehicle or operator authorization is also separate from Space Florida's site license and establishes neither a current site-license disposition nor a completed mission.
