## Roseville, California — a silicon-carbide facility locality

Identity: `178-DOSSIER-001`. Scope: Roseville facility locality, California. Historical route: `/review/v1oct/atlas-depth/roseville-california/`.

Answer: Roseville provides the local setting for Bosch's reported silicon-carbide facility transformation. The retained evidence supports an agreement and reported sample production, not a finding that the city has gained a measured amount of commercially accepted semiconductor output.

### A locality, not a city-wide production measure

The July 13, 2026 Commerce release identifies the Bosch facility with Roseville. That is a useful place anchor, but the retained geographic check did not establish a surveyed facility boundary or the relevant parcels. The dossier therefore describes a facility-associated locality. It does not attribute every manufacturing activity, utility demand or employment change in Roseville to this project. A later site-to-jurisdiction record should connect the named intervention to its actual local footprint without replacing the wider place identity with the factory itself.

### The agreement and the production milestone

The retained source describes a signed direct-funding agreement for up to US$225 million supporting a stated US$2 billion transformation. The ceiling and the stated investment are different financial quantities; neither is a record of money already disbursed or of accepted products delivered. Commerce also reported that sample production had begun and expected commercial production in 2026. That expectation remains a dated statement, not a milestone completed merely because the calendar has advanced.

### What would connect the factory to a place outcome

A commercial-start record should identify the exact Roseville line, qualified product and acceptance basis. Repeated output then needs a period and a product-specific denominator before it can describe dependable production. Place-level workforce, infrastructure or economic conclusions require their own local evidence; they cannot be inferred from the funding agreement. The present manuscript accepts the dated facility context and explicitly leaves commercial acceptance and wider local effects unestablished.

Next artifacts: a site/parcel-to-jurisdiction crosswalk; separately, a dated Bosch or Commerce record identifying the commercial line, qualification, customer acceptance and output period. Do not convert samples into qualified commercial output.

## Malta, New York — one locality, several semiconductor workstreams

Identity: `178-DOSSIER-003`. Scope: Malta semiconductor-campus locality, New York. Historical route: `/review/v1oct/atlas-depth/malta-new-york/`.

Answer: Malta is one place account containing several GlobalFoundries workstreams. New fabrication, modernization and packaging must remain distinguishable components, not three additional places or interchangeable evidence of production.

### A contact address locates an anchor, not the whole campus

The retained NIST account distinguishes new-fab, existing-facility and packaging work at Malta. GlobalFoundries' retained contact entry gives 400 Stonebreak Road Extension as its Malta address. These records anchor the locality, but an address does not define all campus parcels, municipal extent or the footprint of each component. ### Keep component and aggregate financial scopes visible

The original Phase 178 record lists US$1.45 billion across fabrication, modernization and campus expansion. The later geographic check explicitly did not reconcile differing aggregate funding figures. Accordingly, this manuscript does not present that amount as a newly reconciled, current Malta-only funding total or allocate it to packaging. An amount attached to a broader agreement cannot be multiplied across the workstreams. A strategic supply agreement also records a commitment, not commissioned tools or customer-accepted volume.

### Test each workstream on its own evidence

A fabrication milestone cannot establish that a packaging capability is in service, and modernization of an existing facility does not prove completion of a new one. Later records should name the workstream, tools or process, qualification, customer acceptance and output period. Only then can compatible observations be brought together without obscuring their different units. The place account explains their shared locality while preserving those separate technical and financial questions; it does not claim town-wide output or dependable supply from the agreement alone.

Next artifacts: an authoritative campus/parcel/local-jurisdiction crosswalk; an exact financial-scope reconciliation; and separate component-specific commissioning, qualification and acceptance records. Do not count project components as extra localities.

## Yakima Basin, Washington — water storage, entitlement and delivery

Identity: `178-DOSSIER-010`. Scope: Yakima Basin, Washington, as a hydrologic/managed basin; not the City of Yakima. Historical route: `/review/v1oct/atlas-depth/yakima-basin-washington/`.

Answer: The retained August 2026 forecast describes Yakima basin water conditions and projected Yakima Project entitlements. It does not establish final delivered water, a City of Yakima outcome or a measured effect on farms, households or ecosystems.

### Distinguish the basin from the managed delivery system

The August 6 Reclamation release concerns the basin and Yakima Project management, including its five-reservoir context. The geographic eligibility decision counts the named basin once. A basin, a reservoir system and a project service area are related scopes, not interchangeable polygons. A service-area crosswalk is needed before a managed-project delivery total is described as covering every water user in the basin. Nothing in this place account substitutes the City of Yakima for that wider hydrologic scope.

### Keep dates and denominators attached to the numbers

The retained source reports August 1 storage of 605,000 acre-feet, described as 57% full and 80% of average. Those percentages refer to different comparisons and must not be averaged or read as entitlement percentages. Separately, the release projects July-to-September entitlements of 100% for senior and 58% for junior right holders. Those are class-specific forecast values, not final delivered volumes. The August 6 publication date is also distinct from the August 1 observation date.

### Final accounting must preserve the right classes

A later assessment should obtain the final 2026 delivered-water ledger with delivery periods, right classes, methods and entitlement denominators, and identify revisions to the August forecast. A forecast-to-final comparison needs compatible scope and units before a difference can be interpreted. Even verified water delivery would not alone measure crop effects, household welfare or ecosystem response. The present account accepts the dated forecast and storage context while admitting no final delivery series or wider outcome.

Next artifacts: a basin/project service-area crosswalk; the final 2026 delivery accounting by right class and period, with the applicable denominators and forecast revisions. Missing final observations must not be displayed as zero delivery.
