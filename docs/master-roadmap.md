# FTFN Master Roadmap

Civilization is a choice.

FTFN is a future-state intelligence platform planned for `ftfn.io`: a news source, data source, roadmap, and narrative publication about the systems shaping what civilization could become.

## 1. North Star

Build a public-facing platform that helps readers understand:

- what frontier technologies are emerging,
- what dependencies make them possible,
- what constraints could block them,
- what choices society faces,
- and which futures are plausible, urgent, speculative, or already arriving.

The project should never be a press-release mirror. It should summarize, classify, contextualize, and cite.

## 2. Core Thesis

```text
42 is possibility.
59 is urgency.

The future is not a list of inventions.
It is a stack of dependencies.
```

The editorial posture is:

- curious, not breathless,
- skeptical, not cynical,
- urgent, not apocalyptic,
- imaginative, not naive.

## 6. Editorial Method

Every published signal should answer:

- What happened?
- Who is claiming it?
- What evidence exists?
- Why does it matter?
- Where does it sit on the maturity ladder?
- What does it depend on?
- What could block it?
- Who benefits?
- Who pays?
- What is the externality?
- What is the original source?

House rule:

```text
Summarize the claim.
Identify who is making it.
Explain the technical context.
Locate it on the maturity ladder.
Name the dependencies.
Name the blockers.
Link to the original source.
```

## 7. Signature Formats

### Signal Brief

A short, structured summary of one development.

Recommended sections:

- What changed
- Why it matters
- Dependency stack
- Constraint watch
- Time horizon
- Source

### Dependency Map

A visual or structured explanation of what a technology needs before it can scale.

Example:

```text
eVTOL deployment depends on batteries, certification, vertiports, air traffic systems, insurance, manufacturing, noise acceptance, weather operations, and public trust.
```

### Local System Profile

A place-based or sector-based profile showing how signals are transduced through local constraints.

Potential fields:

```text
place
key industries
energy capacity
water stress
transport infrastructure
labor availability
regulatory environment
capital flows
climate exposure
major projects
relevant signals
likely second-order effects
```

Core question:

```text
What does this signal change, where, for whom, and through which constraints?
```

### Maturity Tracker

A topic-level timeline showing movement from theory to deployment.

### Constraint Watch

A recurring format tracking bottlenecks such as power, minerals, water, capital, security, regulation, and labor.

### Future State Report

A deeper essay or report on one domain.

Examples:

- The Critical Minerals Chain
- Autonomous Aviation
- AI for Science
- Quantum Systems
- The Grid Under AI Load
- ENSO and the Food System

### 42/59 Index

A branded scoring system.

```text
42 Score: transformative potential
59 Score: urgency, fragility, or constraint pressure
```

This should not be implemented as false precision at first. Begin with qualitative labels, then evolve into a scoring rubric once enough signals exist.
