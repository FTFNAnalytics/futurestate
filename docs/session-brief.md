## Project Identity

FTFN is a future-state intelligence platform planned for `ftfn.io`.

Core framing:

```text
42 is possibility.
59 is urgency.
Civilization is a choice.

The future is not a list of inventions.
It is a stack of dependencies.
```

FTFN tracks frontier technologies, planetary conditions, resource foundations, enabling infrastructure, human systems, and local constraint environments.

The product should become:

- a news source,
- a data source,
- a roadmap for future systems,
- a narrative publication about civilization-scale choices.

## Editorial Rules

FTFN should not mirror press releases or amplify hype.

Operating rules:

- Prefer official and primary sources.
- Separate claims from evidence.
- Treat company claims as interested-party evidence.
- Do not make strong local conclusions without local sources.
- Keep speculative claims clearly labeled.
- Preserve source URLs, checked dates, credibility tiers, and known limitations.
- Keep `Draft Sample` and `In Review` visible during prelaunch, but clearly labeled.
